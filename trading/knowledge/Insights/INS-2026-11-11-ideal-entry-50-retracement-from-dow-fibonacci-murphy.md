# Ideal Entry: 50% Retracement from Dow, Fibonacci, Murphy

## Discovery Summary

C050 states secondary trends retrace one-third to two-thirds, commonly 50%. EN068 explicitly identifies 40-60% retracements as excellent entry zones for longs/shorts. N085 lists 38%, 50%, and 62% as key Fibonacci levels, with 50% as a central pivot. These three independent frameworks converge on the 50% retracement (within the 38-62% band) as the ideal pullback entry point, reinforcing Murphy's rule with quantitative precision.

## Trading Implication

In an uptrend, wait for a pullback to the 50% Fibonacci retracement of the prior advance (or the 40-60% zone) before entering long; place stops below the 62% level. Mirror this for short entries in downtrends.
