# Earnings filter for weekly reversal at role-reversal

## Discovery Summary

The weekly reversal pattern (N007) at a level where previous support becomes resistance (C065) is a role-reversal setup. E005 highlights subjectivity in defining penetration, but adding a mandatory earnings calendar check (seed) within 7 days filters out false signals due to earnings-driven volatility, improving reliability.

## Trading Implication

Before entering a weekly reversal pattern at a support-turned-resistance level, reject the trade if the underlying has an earnings release within the next 7 calendar days.
