# Converging retracement frameworks pinpoint 50% pullback as ideal entry

## Discovery Summary

EN068-4060-retracement-zones-for-timing-entries gives the actionable core: buy 40-60% pullbacks in uptrends and short 40-60% bounces in downtrends. C050-secondary-trend-retracement-range validates this with Dow's one-third-to-two-thirds range and the 50% most-common retracement, while N085-fibonacci-percentage-retracements-38-50-62 adds the outer 38%/62% boundaries and the trend-strength condition: strong trends tend to retrace only to ~38%, weak trends can go to ~62%. Together they define a bounded entry zone with 50% as the high-probability midpoint.

## Trading Implication

In an uptrend, place buy limits in the 40-60% retracement zone, prefer 50%, but adjust to 38% in strong trends and to 62% in weak trends; do not chase pullbacks beyond 62%. Mirror with short entries in downtrends.
