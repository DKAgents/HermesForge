# Ideal entry zone: 40-60% retracement, 50% core from Dow and Fibonacci

## Discovery Summary

Dow's secondary trend retracement range (one-third to two-thirds, most commonly 50%) aligns with Fibonacci levels (38%, 50%, 62%) and Murphy's explicit rule for entries (40-60% retracement). The overlap between Murphy's 40-60% zone and the Fibonacci 38-62% range, centered on 50%, validates a precise entry window. This cross-domain convergence reinforces that the ideal pullback entry point is the 50% retracement, with acceptable entries between 40% and 60%.

## Trading Implication

Wait for pullbacks to retrace between 40% and 60% of the prior move before entering, prioritizing the 50% level; avoid entries outside the 38-62% Fibonacci band as they signal either excessive strength or potential trend reversal.
