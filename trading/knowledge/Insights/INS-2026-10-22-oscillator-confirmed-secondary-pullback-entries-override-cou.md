# Oscillator-Confirmed Secondary Pullback Entries Override Counter-Trend Risk Rules

## Discovery Summary

EN041's oscillator entry strategy in trending markets likely targets entries during pullbacks, which are precisely the secondary trends described in C366. In strong trends, oscillators reaching extreme levels (e.g., oversold in an uptrend) signal the exhaustion of the secondary counter-move, allowing a trader to enter in the direction of the primary trend even though the immediate move is counter-trend. This effectively adds a condition to generic counter-trend risk rules: counter-trend trades are acceptable only when oscillator extremes align with the primary trend and the secondary trend is losing momentum.

## Trading Implication

In a strong primary trend, use oscillator extremes to time entries against the secondary trend, rather than avoiding all counter-trend trades; this turns secondary pullbacks into low-risk entry opportunities.
