# Volume Confirms Flag/Pennant Breakouts

## Discovery Summary

The flag and pennant pattern characteristics (N043) require light volume during the pause and a burst of activity on the breakout. This aligns with R082 that breakout signals must be accompanied by heavy volume. N013 further warns that a light-volume breakout is a false signal, often leading to a bear trap. Together, they create a filter: a valid flag/pennant breakout must show a volume surge; otherwise the breakout is suspect.

## Trading Implication

When trading a flag or pennant, wait for the breakout to occur on heavy volume before entering. If the breakout is on light volume, avoid the trade or tighten stops, as it may be a false breakout.
