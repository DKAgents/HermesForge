# Volume divergence triggers tighter stops for flag breakouts

## Discovery Summary

Flag and pennant patterns (N043) are continuation patterns that require heavy volume on breakout (R082). Volume acts as a filter for false breakouts (N013). When volume diverges from price during an existing trend, it signals weakening momentum, suggesting that stops should be tightened to protect against potential reversal.

## Trading Implication

When trading flag/pennant breakouts, if volume is lower than expected or diverging from price, adjust stops closer to entry or use a trailing stop to reduce risk.
