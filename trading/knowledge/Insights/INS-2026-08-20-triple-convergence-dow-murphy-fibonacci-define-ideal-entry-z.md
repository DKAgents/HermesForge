# Triple Convergence: Dow, Murphy, Fibonacci Define Ideal Entry Zone

## Discovery Summary

Three independent frameworks converge on the same entry zone: Dow Theory (C050) identifies 33-67% as the normal secondary correction range, Murphy's entry rule (EN068) specifies 40-60% pullbacks as 'excellent' long entry zones, and Fibonacci retracements (N085) place key levels at 38%, 50%, and 62%. The overlap between all three is the 40-62% zone, with 50% being explicitly validated by both Dow and Fibonacci as the most significant single level within it.

## Trading Implication

In an uptrend, prioritize long entries when a pullback reaches the 50% retracement level, treating 38-62% as the full entry window; entries outside this range (under 33% or beyond 67%) lack multi-framework support and should be avoided or sized smaller.
