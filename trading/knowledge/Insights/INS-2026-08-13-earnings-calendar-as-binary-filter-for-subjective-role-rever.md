# Earnings Calendar as Binary Filter for Subjective Role-Reversal Entries

## Discovery Summary

E005 acknowledges that 'significant penetration' in role-reversal setups is inherently subjective (Murphy, p. 83), meaning entry signals at reversed support/resistance levels already carry interpretive uncertainty. C065 and R029 both confirm that violated support becomes resistance across price charts and speedlines alike — but neither addresses external event risk that can violently invalidate these levels. The seed question introduces a binary earnings filter: if earnings are within 7 days, the already-subjective role-reversal signal (E005) should be voided, because an earnings surprise can instantly destroy the behavioral logic behind C065 (trapped longs selling rallies) and R029 (speedline resistance holding).

## Trading Implication

Before entering any role-reversal trade — whether at a violated support-turned-resistance (C065) or a broken speedline acting as resistance (R029) — a trader must check the earnings calendar; if a report falls within 7 days, skip the setup entirely, as the subjectivity already embedded in 'significant penetration' (E005) compounds unacceptably with binary event risk.
