# Earnings Filter for Role-Reversal Entry Setups

## Discovery Summary

E005 shows that deciding when a support/resistance penetration is significant is subjective, yet both C065 (previous support as future resistance in downtrend) and R029 (speedline role reversal) rely on this same penetration threshold. An earnings announcement within 7 days can trigger gap or volatility moves that penetrate these levels without confirming a genuine role-reversal. Therefore a mandatory pre-trade earnings-calendar binary filter should precede any role-reversal setup from E005, C065, or R029.

## Trading Implication

Check the earnings calendar before entering any role-reversal trade based on broken support/resistance or broken speedlines; if earnings are within 7 days, skip the setup. This avoids event-driven false penetrations and improves signal reliability.
