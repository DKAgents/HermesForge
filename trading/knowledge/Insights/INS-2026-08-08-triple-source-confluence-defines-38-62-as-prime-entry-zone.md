# Triple-Source Confluence Defines 38-62% as Prime Entry Zone

## Discovery Summary

Three independent frameworks converge on the same retracement range: Dow Theory (C050) defines secondary corrections as retracing one-third to two-thirds (33-67%) of the prior move; Murphy's entry rule (EN068) specifies 40-60% pullbacks as optimal entry zones; and Fibonacci retracements (N085) place key levels at 38%, 50%, and 62%. The 38-62% Fibonacci band nearly perfectly overlaps both the Dow secondary trend range and Murphy's 40-60% entry rule, creating a triple-confirmed decision zone that no single framework alone would justify with equal confidence.

## Trading Implication

In an uptrend, a trader should place limit orders or monitor for confirmation signals specifically within the 38-62% retracement of the prior swing, treating this zone as high-probability entry; pullbacks shallower than 38% or deeper than 62% should be treated as either insufficient or potentially trend-reversing, reducing position sizing accordingly.
