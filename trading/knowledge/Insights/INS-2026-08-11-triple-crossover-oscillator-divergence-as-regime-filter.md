# Triple Crossover Oscillator Divergence as Regime Filter

## Discovery Summary

C128 establishes that MA differences can function as oscillators revealing momentum. R323 and N037 together define the 4-9-18 triple crossover system (R.C. Allen, 1972). A non-obvious connection emerges: when the 4-9 spread and 9-18 spread are both near zero simultaneously, the system is in a compressing, range-bound regime — the oscillator interpretation of C128 applied to both MA pairs in the 4-9-18 system identifies when the pattern's signals are likely to fail (both differences approaching zero = no trend = whipsaw risk).

## Trading Implication

Traders using the 4-9-18 crossover system (N037) should compute both the 4-9 and 9-18 differences as oscillators (C128); when both oscillators compress toward zero concurrently, suppress new crossover signals as the regime is ranging and failure probability is elevated.
