# Earnings filter mitigates role-reversal subjectivity

## Discovery Summary

E005-subjectivity-in-defining-significant-penetration notes that determining a 'significant' penetration for role reversal is subjective, a known weakness. C065-previous-support-as-future-resistance-in-downtrend and R029-speed-resistance-lines-role-reversal confirm that broken levels reverse roles. The seed question adds a concrete binary filter: a pre-trade earnings calendar check. Because earnings events can cause sharp, temporary price moves that create ambiguous penetrations, avoiding role-reversal entries within 7 days of an announcement reduces the likelihood of acting on a non-significant break, directly addressing the subjectivity problem.

## Trading Implication

Before entering any trade based on support/resistance role reversal, verify that no earnings announcement is scheduled within the next 7 days; if there is, skip the trade.
