# Earnings calendar filter for role-reversal entries

## Discovery Summary

Role-reversal signals from C065 (previous support as future resistance) and R029 (speedline role reversal) depend on subjective penetration judgments as noted in E005 (Murphy p.83). An earnings announcement within seven days creates event risk that can cause false breaks or gap through these technical levels, so the seed's mandatory pre-trade earnings check is a necessary filter before acting on any role-reversal entry. If earnings are within seven days, the technical signal should be skipped or postponed until after the announcement.

## Trading Implication

Before entering any role-reversal trade based on support/resistance or speedline breaks, check the earnings calendar; if the company reports within seven days, stand aside until after the announcement.
