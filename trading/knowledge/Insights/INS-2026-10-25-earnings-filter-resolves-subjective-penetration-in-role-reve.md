# Earnings filter resolves subjective penetration in role-reversal

## Discovery Summary

The subjectivity in defining significant penetration (E005) of previous support as future resistance (C065) introduces ambiguity in role-reversal entries. The seed question adds an earnings calendar check (no trades within 7 days) as a binary, objective filter, reducing that subjectivity and preventing false signals driven by earnings-related volatility.

## Trading Implication

Before entering a role-reversal trade based on support-turned-resistance, check that earnings are not within the next 7 days; reject the trade if they are.
