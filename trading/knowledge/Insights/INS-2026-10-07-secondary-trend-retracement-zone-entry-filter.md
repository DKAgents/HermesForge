# Secondary trend retracement zone entry filter

## Discovery Summary

Concept C050 defines secondary trend retracement range as typically 1/3 to 2/3 of prior move. Rule EN068-4060 specifies 40-60% retracement zones for timing entries. Combining them gives a precise filter: enter pullbacks only when price retraces to the 40-60% Fibonacci zone of the prior impulse within a secondary uptrend, matching John Murphy's ideal entry point.

## Trading Implication

Only take long entries on pullbacks that retrace to the 40-60% zone (e.g., 0.382-0.618 Fibonacci) of the prior impulse wave within a confirmed secondary uptrend; avoid shallower or deeper retracements.
