# Old Support Flip Plus Gap Sets Stop and Position Size

## Discovery Summary

C065 identifies the short setup: after a support break in a downtrend, the old support level becomes resistance on later bounces. EN069 sharpens the timing: in a bear move, sell a rally to the lower edge of a gap or into the gap, with a protective stop above the gap. When the old support/resistance and a gap coincide, the stop is defined objectively, and that stop distance is the basis for position sizing. This connects Murphy's support/resistance concept to a concrete risk-controlled trade.

## Trading Implication

Short rallies into old support that has flipped to resistance, especially when a gap sits at that level; place the stop above the gap/resistance and size the position from the exact stop distance.
