# Double crossover as oscillator filter

## Discovery Summary

The double crossover method (10/50 MA) from N039 and EN028 is reframed as an oscillator in C128, suggesting a divergence-based condition. A trader could treat the crossover as an oscillator reading, waiting for convergence/divergence patterns (like MACD) before acting, rather than taking every crossover signal.

## Trading Implication

Filter crossover signals by checking if the MA difference is diverging from price (e.g., price makes higher high but crossover oscillator makes lower high) before entering or exiting.
