# Volume confirms flag/pennant breakouts

## Discovery Summary

The flag/pennant pattern (N043) typically shows decreasing volume during consolidation, then a breakout with heavy volume. Rule R082 mandates that all breakouts must be accompanied by heavy volume. Indicator N013 uses volume to filter false breakouts. Thus, volume confirmation is a necessary condition for valid flag/pennant breakouts, and traders should treat low-volume breakouts as false signals.

## Trading Implication

When trading flag/pennant patterns, only act on breakouts confirmed by heavy volume; ignore breakouts with below-average volume and consider placing stops below the consolidation zone.
