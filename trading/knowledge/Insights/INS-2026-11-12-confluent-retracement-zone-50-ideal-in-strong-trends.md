# Confluent Retracement Zone: 50% Ideal in Strong Trends

## Discovery Summary

Murphy's EN068 rule sets a 40-60% retracement zone for entries. The Fibonacci note N085 identifies 38% and 62% as boundaries for strong vs weak trends. Dow theory concepts C050 confirms the typical 1/3-2/3 range with 50% as the most common. Together they establish a precise entry logic: in strong uptrends, enter at 38-50% retracement; in weak trends, wait for 50-62%. The 50% level acts as the default pivot when trend strength is uncertain.

## Trading Implication

When buying pullbacks in an uptrend, scale initial entries at 38% if the trend is strong, add at 50% as confirmation, and stand aside if the retracement exceeds 62% (weak trend).
