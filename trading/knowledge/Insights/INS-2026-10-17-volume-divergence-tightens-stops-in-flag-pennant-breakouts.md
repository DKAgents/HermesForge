# Volume divergence tightens stops in flag/pennant breakouts

## Discovery Summary

N043 notes that flag and pennant patterns are continuation patterns. R082 states breakouts must be accompanied by heavy volume. N013 demonstrates how volume can filter false breakouts. The non-obvious link is that when volume diverges (e.g., declines) during the formation of a flag/pennant, the subsequent breakout is more likely to be false, so traders should adjust stop-loss levels or wait for volume confirmation.

## Trading Implication

When trading a flag or pennant continuation pattern, monitor volume trend; if volume decreases as the pattern develops (divergence), tighten stops below the pattern's low or delay entry until heavy volume confirms the breakout.
