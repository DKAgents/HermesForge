# Earnings filter reduces role-reversal subjectivity

## Discovery Summary

E005-subjectivity-in-defining-significant-penetration acknowledges that determining a 'significant' breach of support/resistance is subjective. C065-previous-support-as-future-resistance-in-downtrend and R029-speed-resistance-lines-role-reversal both depend on this penetration judgment for their role-reversal signals. The seed adds a mandatory binary filter—checking that earnings are not within 7 days—which objectively eliminates a common source of noise and false breaks, thereby tightening the subjectivity identified in E005 and improving the reliability of C065 and R029 setups.

## Trading Implication

Before acting on any role-reversal signal from violated support or speedlines, verify the stock has no scheduled earnings within the next 7 days; if it does, skip the trade or wait for the event to pass.
