# Role Reversal for Stop Placement and Position Sizing

## Discovery Summary

In downtrends, previous support becomes resistance (Murphy p.83), and in uptrends, previous resistance becomes support (C064). These role-reversed levels provide concrete reference points for setting stop-loss distances, which then determine position size according to a fixed risk per trade.

## Trading Implication

When entering a short in a downtrend, place your stop just above the prior support level (now resistance). When entering a long in an uptrend, place your stop just below the prior resistance level (now support). Use the distance from entry to that stop to calculate position size based on your maximum acceptable loss.
