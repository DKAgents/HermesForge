# Earnings Filter for Role-Reversal Entries

## Discovery Summary

The notes E005 and C065 highlight the subjectivity in defining 'significant penetration' for role reversal, while R029 extends this to speedlines. This subjectivity makes role-reversal signals prone to false triggers around scheduled news events. Adding a mandatory earnings calendar check (if earnings within 7 days, skip the trade) provides an objective, binary filter that reduces noise and false penetrations, thereby improving the reliability of the role-reversal entry criteria.

## Trading Implication

Before executing any role-reversal trade (support becomes resistance, speedline broken, etc.), verify the instrument's earnings date. If within 7 days, abstain from the trade.
