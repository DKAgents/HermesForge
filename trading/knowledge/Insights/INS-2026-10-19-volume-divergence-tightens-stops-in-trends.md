# Volume divergence tightens stops in trends

## Discovery Summary

N043-flag-and-pennant-summary-characteristics notes that volume contracts during pattern formation and must expand on breakout (R082). N013-volume-as-a-filter-for-false-breakouts reinforces that heavy volume confirms breakouts. When price trends but volume diverges (declining), it warns of weakening momentum, signaling a false breakout or trend exhaustion, so stops should be moved closer.

## Trading Implication

During a trend, if volume decreases as price makes new highs (or lows), tighten stops to breakeven or a minimal distance to protect gains against a potential false breakout.
