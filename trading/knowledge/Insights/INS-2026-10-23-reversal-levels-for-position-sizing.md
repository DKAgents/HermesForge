# Reversal Levels for Position Sizing

## Discovery Summary

The notes on previous support as future resistance in a downtrend and previous peaks as future support in an uptrend (C065 and C064) define specific price levels where stops can be placed. Combined with the seed question's insight that stop distance determines position size, traders can systematically compute position size using a fixed fractional risk model based on the distance from entry to the role-reversed level.

## Trading Implication

Set your stop-loss just beyond the most recent role-reversed level (former support for shorts, former resistance for longs) and size your position so that the stop distance equals your maximum acceptable risk per trade (e.g., 1% of account).
