# Earnings filter reduces false role-reversal penetration signals

## Discovery Summary

E005-subjectivity-in-defining-significant-penetration indicates that judging when a level is truly broken is subjective, which is critical for C065-previous-support-as-future-resistance-in-downtrend role-reversal entries. An upcoming earnings event within 7 days can create sharp price moves that penetrate a prior support/resistance level without confirming a genuine role-reversal. Adding a mandatory binary earnings filter to the entry criteria directly addresses this subjectivity by eliminating high-impact scheduled news as a false trigger.

## Trading Implication

Before taking any role-reversal entry based on C065, check the earnings calendar as a binary gate: if earnings are within 7 days, skip the trade regardless of apparent penetration significance.
