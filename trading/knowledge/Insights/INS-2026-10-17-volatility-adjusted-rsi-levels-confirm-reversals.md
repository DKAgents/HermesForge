# Volatility-Adjusted RSI Levels Confirm Reversals

## Discovery Summary

The concept note C149 compares RSI and stochastics under varying volatility, while indicator note N165 defines fixed overbought/oversold levels (e.g., 70/30). The non-obvious connection is that standard RSI thresholds fail in high volatility; combining the volatility context from C149 with N165 allows dynamic threshold adjustment (e.g., 80/20 in high vol) to better confirm reversal patterns.

## Trading Implication

Trader should replace fixed RSI levels with volatility-adjusted thresholds (e.g., widen to 80/20 when ATR expands) when scanning for reversal patterns, improving signal reliability.
