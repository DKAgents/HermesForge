# Previous resistance becomes support in uptrend

## Discovery Summary

The notes on C065 and C064 describe the classic Murphy principle that prior support becomes resistance in a downtrend, and by symmetry, prior peaks become support in an uptrend. This principle can be used to set logical stop-loss distances just below former resistance-turned-support in an uptrend, or above former support-turned-resistance in a downtrend, directly informing position sizing via risk per trade.

## Trading Implication

When entering a long position in an uptrend, place a stop slightly below the nearest prior peak that now acts as support; when shorting in a downtrend, place a stop just above the nearest prior support that now acts as resistance. This ties stop distance to a proven structural level, improving risk-adjusted position sizing.
