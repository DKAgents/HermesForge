# S/R Levels Define Stops and Position Size

## Discovery Summary

C065 shows that violated support becomes resistance in downtrends, providing a specific ceiling for short entries with stops above. EN069 adds that price gaps also act as support/resistance, with stops placed beyond the gap. Together, these Murphy-based rules establish a sequence: identify a S/R level (from prior support or gaps), use it to set a precise stop distance, then derive position size from that distance based on risk guidelines.

## Trading Implication

When shorting a bounce into former support or selling into a gap, place the protective stop just above that level and size the position so the stop distance matches the predefined risk per trade.
