# 10/50 Crossover as Oscillator for MACD-style signals

## Discovery Summary

The Double Crossover Method (10 and 50 day) from N039 and EN028 is typically used for trend signals. The concept note C128 explains that comparing two moving averages creates an oscillator, similar to MACD. This suggests traders can plot the difference (10‑50) to gain additional overbought/oversold or divergence signals beyond the simple crossover.

## Trading Implication

Plot the difference between the 10‑ and 50‑day MAs and look for extreme readings or divergences with price to enter or exit positions earlier than the crossover alone.
