# Secondary trends validate oscillator entries in strong trends

## Discovery Summary

EN041's rule to buy oversold in uptrends assumes pullbacks occur, but strong trends can keep oscillators overbought for extended periods, making such signals rare or misleading. C366's secondary trends describe the counter-trend corrections (lasting 3 weeks to months) that actually create the oversold conditions needed for EN041 entries. The McClellan oscillator (N186) can then confirm breadth during these secondary corrections, while risk rules about counter-trend trades are respected because buying during a secondary dip aligns with the primary trend.

## Trading Implication

Only take oscillator-based entries (EN041) when a secondary trend correction is clearly in progress, not during parabolic trends. Use the McClellan oscillator to confirm that the correction is broad-based before entering.
