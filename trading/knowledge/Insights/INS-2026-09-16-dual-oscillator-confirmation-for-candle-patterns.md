# Dual Oscillator Confirmation for Candle Patterns

## Discovery Summary

Notes C149 (RSI vs Stochastics) emphasize that best signals occur when both oscillators are simultaneously overbought or oversold. Note R177 (Filtered Candle Patterns) states any oscillator can filter patterns if it's in its presignal area. Combining these, a trader can require both RSI and Stochastics to be in overbought/oversold territory together before accepting a candle reversal pattern, leveraging the reduced volatility of RSI and the sensitivity of Stochastics for higher confirmation.

## Trading Implication

When a bearish or bullish candle reversal pattern appears, only act if both RSI (above 70/below 30) and Stochastics (overbought/oversold) are simultaneously in their extreme zones, thereby filtering out weaker signals.
