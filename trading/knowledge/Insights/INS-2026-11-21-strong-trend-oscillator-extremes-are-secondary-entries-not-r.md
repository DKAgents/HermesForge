# Strong Trend Oscillator Extremes Are Secondary Entries, Not Reversals

## Discovery Summary

C366 defines secondary trends as counter-primary corrections/rallies. EN041 says to buy oversold conditions in uptrends and sell overbought conditions in downtrends, meaning these oscillator extremes should be used as entries aligned with the primary trend. N186's McClellan oscillator, however, can remain overbought/oversold in strong trends, so treating its extremes as reversal signals would lead to counter-trend trades against the primary trend. This adds a condition to EN041: in strong trends, use the zero-line crossover alternative rather than waiting for extreme oscillator readings, and avoid fading the primary trend.

## Trading Implication

In a strong primary trend, use McClellan oscillator oversold/overbought readings to time entries during secondary corrections, but never short an overbought reading in an uptrend or buy an oversold reading in a downtrend. If the oscillator stays at extremes, switch to zero-line crosses for entry signals.
