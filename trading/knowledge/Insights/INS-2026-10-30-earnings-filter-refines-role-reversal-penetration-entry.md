# Earnings filter refines role-reversal penetration entry

## Discovery Summary

C065 describes the role-reversal setup where previous support becomes resistance in a downtrend, and E005 highlights the subjectivity in defining a 'significant penetration' of such levels. The seed refines this by mandating an earnings calendar check as a binary filter: if earnings are within 7 days, the trade is skipped. This filter reduces the ambiguity of E005 by removing high-impact news events that can produce false or exaggerated penetrations of the role-reversal level.

## Trading Implication

Before taking any role-reversal short based on C065, check the earnings calendar for the underlying stock; if earnings are within 7 days, stand aside regardless of how significant the penetration appears.
