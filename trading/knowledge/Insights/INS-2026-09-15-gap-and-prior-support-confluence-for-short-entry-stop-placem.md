# Gap and prior support confluence for short entry stop placement

## Discovery Summary

In a downtrend, prior support becomes resistance (C065) and price gaps function as support/resistance levels (EN069). Combining these with risk guidelines (RG035) that stops must be at valid technical levels creates a sequence: identify a broken support level, wait for a rally that creates a gap near that level, then sell the rally to the lower end of the gap with a stop placed just above the gap (or above the former support level), ensuring the stop satisfies both technical and money management criteria.

## Trading Implication

In a downtrend, after a prior support level is breached, use a price gap near that level as a precise entry for shorting the rally, and set a protective stop above the gap to align with both the new resistance and money management rules.
