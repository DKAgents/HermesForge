# Weekly reversal confirms role-reversal penetration

## Discovery Summary

The weekly reversal pattern (N007) provides an objective definition of 'significant penetration' (E005) for the role-reversal concept (C065). When a weekly reversal candle closes beyond a former support level now acting as resistance, it confirms the level has been decisively broken, reducing subjectivity and increasing entry reliability.

## Trading Implication

Enter short only after a weekly reversal pattern that closes beyond previous support (now resistance), rather than relying on subjective breakouts or intraday moves.
