# Commodity Exporter Stress Triggers 10/50 MA Short Signals

## Discovery Summary

E040 identifies commodity-exporting nations (Australia, Canada, Mexico, Russia) as especially vulnerable to commodity price plunges, which Murphy's intermarket chain links to downstream equity market stress. EN028 and N039 define a concrete mechanical signal — the 10-day MA crossing below the 50-day MA — as a sell trigger for stocks. The non-obvious connection is that monitoring commodity price trends (E040) can provide an early warning to pre-position for the 10/50 crossover sell signal in equity markets of commodity-exporting countries specifically, tightening the filter from 'all stocks' to a high-probability subset.

## Trading Implication

When broad commodity indices show a sustained plunge, actively watch equity indices of Australia, Canada, Mexico, and Russia for the 10-day MA crossing below the 50-day MA as a high-conviction sell/short entry, since the intermarket headwind amplifies the reliability of the crossover signal in those markets.
