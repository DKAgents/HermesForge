# Role Reversal Levels for Position Sizing

## Discovery Summary

The concept of previous support becoming resistance in downtrends (Murphy p.83) and previous resistance becoming support in uptrends provides objective levels for stop loss placement. Applying risk management rules, the distance from entry to these levels determines position size for a given risk tolerance. This integrates technical analysis with money management.

## Trading Implication

Set stop losses just beyond the identified support/resistance levels and calculate position size accordingly to risk a fixed percentage of capital per trade.
