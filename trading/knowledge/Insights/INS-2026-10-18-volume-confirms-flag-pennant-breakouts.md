# Volume confirms flag/pennant breakouts

## Discovery Summary

Flag and pennant patterns (N043) are continuation patterns that require a breakout to confirm. Rule R082 states breakouts must be accompanied by heavy volume, and indicator N013 provides a volume-based filter to identify false breakouts. When volume diverges from price during a flag/pennant breakout, it signals a potential false move, suggesting that stops should be tightened or the position reduced.

## Trading Implication

When a flag or pennant breakout occurs with below-average volume, treat it as a false breakout and adjust stops closer to the pattern's edge to minimize risk.
