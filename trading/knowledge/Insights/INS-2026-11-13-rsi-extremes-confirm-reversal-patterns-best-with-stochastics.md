# RSI Extremes Confirm Reversal Patterns, Best with Stochastics

## Discovery Summary

R177 says any oscillator can filter candle patterns, but it must be in its presignal overbought/oversold area before a reversal pattern is valid. N165 defines RSI's presignal area as above 70 or below 30 and notes divergence there warns of reversals. C149 adds that RSI is less volatile than Stochastics, so RSI extremes are rarer, and the best confirmation occurs when both RSI and Stochastics are simultaneously extreme. Therefore, using RSI extremes, especially with Stochastics confirmation, creates a more selective filter for candle reversal patterns.

## Trading Implication

Before acting on a bullish or bearish candle reversal pattern, require RSI below 30 or above 70, and preferably the same extreme in Stochastics; avoid reversal signals without this oscillator confirmation.
