# Volatility-Adjusted RSI Confirms Reversal Patterns

## Discovery Summary

N165 fixed RSI overbought/oversold levels, while C149 compares RSI and stochastics in volatility regimes. The non-obvious connection is that RSI extremes are not absolute but should be interpreted relative to volatility; stochastics may offer better reversal confirmation when volatility is high. Therefore, using the volatility comparison from C149 to condition RSI readings from N165 can improve pattern confirmation.

## Trading Implication

When confirming reversal patterns, adjust RSI overbought/oversold thresholds based on current volatility or prefer stochastics in high-volatility conditions to avoid false signals.
