# Earnings filter resolves penetration subjectivity for role reversal

## Discovery Summary

The subjectivity in defining significant penetration (E005) complicates the use of previous support turning into resistance in a downtrend (C065). By adding a mandatory earnings calendar check as a binary filter—if earnings are within 7 days, skip the role-reversal entry—the trader removes ambiguity and avoids false breaks driven by event risk, making the rule more robust.

## Trading Implication

Before entering a role-reversal trade based on support-as-resistance in a downtrend, check if the stock has an earnings announcement within the next 7 days; if yes, cancel the trade.
