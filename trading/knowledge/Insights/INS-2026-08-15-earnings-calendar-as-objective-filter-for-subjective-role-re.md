# Earnings Calendar as Objective Filter for Subjective Role-Reversal Entries

## Discovery Summary

E005 identifies that 'significant penetration' in role-reversal setups is inherently subjective (Murphy, p. 83), creating ambiguous entry signals. C065 and R029 both rely on role-reversal mechanics — violated support becoming resistance in downtrends, and broken speedlines reversing roles — yet neither provides an objective qualifier for trade timing. The seed question introduces earnings proximity as a mandatory binary filter: because role-reversal entries already suffer from subjective penetration thresholds, adding an earnings calendar check within 7 days resolves a second layer of uncertainty by eliminating setups where gap risk could invalidate the technical level entirely before it can be tested.

## Trading Implication

Before entering any role-reversal trade (whether from a violated support level per C065 or a broken speedline per R029), check the earnings calendar; if an earnings event falls within 7 days, skip the setup regardless of how clean the technical penetration appears.
