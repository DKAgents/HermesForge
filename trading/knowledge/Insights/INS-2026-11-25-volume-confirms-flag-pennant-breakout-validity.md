# Volume Confirms Flag/Pennant Breakout Validity

## Discovery Summary

N043-flag-and-pennant-summary-characteristics states that flags and pennants resume the trend on a burst of trading activity, while R082-breakouts-must-be-accompanied-by-heavy-volume mandates that all pattern breakouts require heavy volume to be valid. N013-volume-as-a-filter-for-false-breakouts reinforces this by warning that light-volume breakouts are often false. Together, they establish that the flag/pennant pattern is only tradeable when the breakout volume surges; otherwise, the breakout is suspect.

## Trading Implication

Wait for a distinct volume surge on the breakout from a flag or pennant before entering; if volume is lacking, do not enter or place stops just beyond the pattern boundary to protect against a false move.
