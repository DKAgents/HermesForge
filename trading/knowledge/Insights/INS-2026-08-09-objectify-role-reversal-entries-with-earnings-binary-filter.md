# Objectify Role-Reversal Entries With Earnings Binary Filter

## Discovery Summary

E005 identifies that 'significant penetration' in role-reversal setups is inherently subjective (Murphy, p. 83), creating inconsistent entry signals. C065 and R029 both depend on this same subjective threshold — whether a prior support becomes resistance in a downtrend or whether a broken speedline reverses roles. The seed question introduces a concrete binary filter (earnings within 7 days = no trade) that partially resolves E005's subjectivity problem by eliminating a known high-noise event window where false role-reversal signals are most likely to occur, since earnings gaps can mimic 'significant' penetrations without establishing durable new roles.

## Trading Implication

Before entering any role-reversal trade based on C065 or R029 principles, a trader must first check the earnings calendar; if earnings are within 7 days for that instrument, skip the setup entirely regardless of how convincing the penetration appears.
