# Ideal pullback entry: 40–60% retracement, 50% core

## Discovery Summary

EN068-4060-retracement-zones-for-timing-entries specifies Murphy's actionable long-entry zone as a 40–60% pullback of the prior advance. C050-secondary-trend-retracement-range (pp. 61–80) independently bounds valid secondary corrections at one-third to two-thirds, with 50% most common, so Murphy's zone sits inside a confirmed secondary correction. N085-fibonacci-percentage-retracements-38-50-62 adds the 38%/62% Fib boundaries and trend-strength guide: strong trends retrace to ~38%, weak trends to ~62%. Together they make 50% the ideal entry point, with 40–60% as the practical trigger zone and 62%/two-thirds as the invalidation boundary.

## Trading Implication

In an established uptrend, enter longs only when the pullback reaches the 40–60% retracement zone, center entries near 50%, and abandon the setup if price retraces beyond 62% or two-thirds of the prior move.
