# Confluence of Dow, Fibonacci, and Murphy's 40-60% Entry Zone

## Discovery Summary

Dow Theory's secondary trend retracement range of one-third to two-thirds (C050) aligns closely with Fibonacci retracement levels of 38% and 62% (N085). Murphy's rule (EN068) identifies the 40-60% zone as the optimal entry area, which sits exactly within both the Dow and Fibonacci boundaries, creating a confluence of theoretical foundation and specific entry timing.

## Trading Implication

Use the 40-60% retracement zone as the primary entry window; in strong trends expect a shallow 38% retracement, in weak trends a deeper 62% retracement, and adjust stop placement or position size accordingly.
