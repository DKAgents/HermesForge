# Trend-Strength Fib Retracement Entry Filter

## Discovery Summary

C050-secondary-trend-retracement-range establishes corrections between one-third and two-thirds, with 50% most common. EN068-4060-retracement-zones-for-timing-entries narrows the ideal Murphy entry to 40-60%, while N085-fibonacci-percentage-retracements-38-50-62 adds 38/50/62 levels and states strong trends retrace minimally to 38% while weak trends max out near 62%. The non-obvious connection is that trend strength should determine which half of the 40-60% zone to use: strong trends favor the shallow 38-50% side, weak trends require the deeper 50-62% side.

## Trading Implication

Instead of buying any 40-60% pullback, classify trend strength first and set buy limits at 38-50% retracement in strong uptrends or 50-62% in weak uptrends, using the 50% level as the pivot.
