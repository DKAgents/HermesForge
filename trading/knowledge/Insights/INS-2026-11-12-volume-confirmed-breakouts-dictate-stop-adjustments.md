# Volume-Confirmed Breakouts Dictate Stop Adjustments

## Discovery Summary

N043-flag-and-pennant-summary-characteristics states that flags/pennants form on light volume and break out on a burst of activity. R082-breakouts-must-be-accompanied-by-heavy-volume confirms that valid breakouts require heavy volume, while N013-volume-as-a-filter-for-false-breakouts warns that a light-volume breakout is a bull trap. Thus, when a flag/pennant breakout lacks volume, it is likely false, and stops should be tightened or the position exited. Additionally, if a trend shows price making new highs on declining volume (divergence), this signals weakening momentum, warranting tighter stops to protect gains.

## Trading Implication

Only enter breakouts from consolidation patterns if volume surges; otherwise treat as false and set stops at the pattern's boundary. In an existing trend, if price sets a new high on lower volume, tighten stops to the most recent swing low or a trailing stop.
