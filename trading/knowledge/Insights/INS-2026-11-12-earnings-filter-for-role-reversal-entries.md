# Earnings Filter for Role-Reversal Entries

## Discovery Summary

E005 highlights the subjectivity in judging whether a support/resistance penetration is significant, while C065 and R029 rely on that role-reversal signal. An earnings release within 7 days can create a sharp, news-driven break that is not a genuine technical role-reversal, so adding a mandatory pre-trade earnings calendar check removes a major source of false penetrations. This filter directly reduces the ambiguity noted in E005 and applies equally to C065's prior-support-as-resistance setup and R029's speedline role-reversal rule.

## Trading Implication

Before taking any role-reversal entry based on a support/resistance breakout or speedline break, check the earnings calendar and stand aside if the company reports earnings within 7 days; re-evaluate only after the earnings event and subsequent confirmation.
