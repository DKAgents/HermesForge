# Volume-confirmed flag/pennant breakouts

## Discovery Summary

Flag and pennant patterns (N043) are continuation patterns requiring breakout confirmation. Rule R082 mandates breakouts must have heavy volume, and indicator N013 reinforces volume as a filter against false breakouts. Together, they create a conditional entry rule: only trade a flag/pennant breakout if volume spikes significantly above recent average.

## Trading Implication

Enter only when a flag/pennant breakout coincides with heavy volume; if volume is low, treat as false breakout and tighten stops or avoid the trade entirely.
