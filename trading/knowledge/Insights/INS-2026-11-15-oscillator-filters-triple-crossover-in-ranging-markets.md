# Oscillator filters triple crossover in ranging markets

## Discovery Summary

C128-moving-averages-as-oscillators-via-double-crossover shows that the difference between moving averages acts as an oscillator, while R323 and N037 rely on triple crossover signals such as the 4-9-18 day combination. In volatile or ranging markets, these crossover signals are prone to whipsaw failures. The oscillator version can be used as a regime filter: only act on triple crossover signals when the moving-average oscillator is expanding in the trade direction, not when it is flat or oscillating around zero.

## Trading Implication

Traders should apply the triple crossover only when the double-crossover oscillator confirms directional momentum; otherwise stand aside or reduce size in choppy, range-bound conditions.
