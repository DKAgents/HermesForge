# Ideal Pullback Entry: 40-60% Retrace with Fibonacci 50%

## Discovery Summary

C050 defines typical retracement range of secondary trends (often 38-62%), EN068 narrows entry timing to 40-60% zones, and N085 provides Fibonacci levels (38%, 50%, 62%). Together, they pinpoint the 50% Fibonacci level as the sweet spot within the 40-60% zone, aligning with Murphy's recommended ideal entry point for pullbacks in uptrends.

## Trading Implication

In an uptrend, wait for a pullback to reach the 40-60% retracement zone and place entry orders near the 50% Fibonacci level to maximize probability of trend continuation.
