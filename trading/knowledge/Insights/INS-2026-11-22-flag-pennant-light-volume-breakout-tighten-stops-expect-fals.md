# Flag/pennant light-volume breakout: tighten stops, expect false signal

## Discovery Summary

N043-flag-and-pennant-summary-characteristics expects volume to contract during the flag/pennant pause and then surge on the breakout. R082-breakouts-must-be-accompanied-by-heavy-volume and N013-volume-as-a-filter-for-false-breakouts establish that a breakout on light volume is suspect, and a subsequent heavy-volume decline confirms a false breakout/bull trap. Applied to a flag/pennant, this means the expected volume surge is a condition for trend continuation; its absence should trigger defensive risk management rather than adding to the trend position.

## Trading Implication

If a flag/pennant breaks out on light volume, tighten stops below the pattern or below the breakout price, and exit if the subsequent decline occurs on heavy volume.
