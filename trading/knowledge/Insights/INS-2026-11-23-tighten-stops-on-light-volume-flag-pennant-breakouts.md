# Tighten Stops on Light-Volume Flag/Pennant Breakouts

## Discovery Summary

N043 flag-and-pennant-summary-characteristics describes a valid continuation as a pause on light volume followed by a trend resumption on heavy volume. R082 confirms that all pattern breakouts require heavy volume to be valid. N013 adds that a light-volume breakout is likely a false bull trap, and a subsequent heavy-volume decline confirms the failure. Therefore, when a flag/pennant breaks out on light volume, the breakout should be treated as suspect and stops should be tightened rather than relying on the normal continuation signal.

## Trading Implication

After a light-volume flag/pennant breakout, tighten the stop to just below the pattern's low or breakout level, and exit if a heavy-volume decline follows the failed breakout.
