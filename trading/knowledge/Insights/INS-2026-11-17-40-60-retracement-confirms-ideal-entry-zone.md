# 40-60% retracement confirms ideal entry zone

## Discovery Summary

The Dow theory's secondary trend retracement range (one-third to two-thirds, most common 50%) is narrowed to 40-60% by Murphy's rule (EN068) for timing entries. This aligns with Fibonacci's 50% retracement (N085) as the midpoint, while 38% (strong trend minimum) and 62% (weak trend maximum) mark extremes. Thus, the ideal entry point in an uptrend is a 40-60% pullback, not at the 38% or 62% levels.

## Trading Implication

Wait for a 40-60% retracement of the prior advance in an uptrend before entering long; similarly, short on 40-60% bounces in downtrends. This sets a specific zone for entries rather than arbitrary Fibonacci levels.
