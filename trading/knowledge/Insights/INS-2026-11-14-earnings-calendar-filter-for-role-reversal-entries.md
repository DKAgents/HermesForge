# Earnings Calendar Filter for Role-Reversal Entries

## Discovery Summary

The subjectivity in defining significant penetration (E005) and the role-reversal principle (C065, R029) create ambiguous entry signals. Adding a pre-trade earnings calendar check as a binary filter (as per the seed question) addresses this by excluding trades where an earnings announcement within 7 days could produce false breakouts or violations of support/resistance. This connects technical analysis role-reversal concepts with event-risk management.

## Trading Implication

Before executing any role-reversal trade (support becoming resistance or speedline break), check the earnings calendar; if the instrument reports earnings within 7 days, skip the trade or wait until after the announcement.
