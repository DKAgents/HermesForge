# Murphy's 40-60% entry zone confirmed by Dow and Fibonacci

## Discovery Summary

EN068-4060 states Murphy's ideal entry is a 40–60% retracement in an uptrend or downtrend. C050 (Dow theory) independently shows secondary retracements commonly reach 50% and range from one-third to two-thirds, while N085 identifies 38%, 50%, and 62% as key Fibonacci levels. Together they define a confluence zone centered on 50%, with 38% as the minimum pullback in strong trends and 62% as the maximum in weaker trends.

## Trading Implication

In an uptrend, wait for a pullback to the 40–60% retracement zone (ideally 50%) before entering long; adjust the lower bound to 38% in strong trends and treat 62% as the maximum acceptable retracement before doubting the trend.
