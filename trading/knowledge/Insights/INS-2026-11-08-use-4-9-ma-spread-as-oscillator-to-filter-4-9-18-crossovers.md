# Use 4-9 MA Spread as Oscillator to Filter 4-9-18 Crossovers

## Discovery Summary

C128 explains that a double crossover can be viewed as an oscillator, as in MACD. Applying this to the shortest pair in the 4-9-18 triple crossover (N037, R323) creates an internal momentum oscillator: the 4-9 moving-average spread can confirm or reject the slower 9/18 and 4/18 crossover signals. Since moving-average crossover systems are trend-following, this filter helps reduce whipsaw entries in ranging or volatile regimes.

## Trading Implication

Before taking a 4-9-18 triple crossover signal, require the 4-9 MA spread to be directionally aligned with the crossover, and stand aside when the spread is flat or contradictory.
