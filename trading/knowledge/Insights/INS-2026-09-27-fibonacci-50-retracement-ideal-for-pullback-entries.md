# Fibonacci 50% retracement ideal for pullback entries

## Discovery Summary

Murphy's 40-60% retracement rule (EN068-4060) aligns with the most common Dow retracement of 50% (C050) and the Fibonacci 50% level (N085). Together, they identify the 50% zone as the ideal entry point within a strong trend.

## Trading Implication

In a strong uptrend, enter long on pullbacks specifically to the 50% Fibonacci retracement of the prior move, as this combines Murphy's optimal zone with Dow theory's typical retracement limit.
