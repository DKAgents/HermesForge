# Oscillator filter reduces triple crossover whipsaws in ranges

## Discovery Summary

Triple crossover systems like the 4-9-18 day method (N037) generate numerous false signals in volatile or ranging markets because they are inherently trend-following. Viewing moving averages through a double crossover oscillator lens, as with MACD (C128), reveals regime: wide separation signals trend, while narrow or flat readings flag range-bound conditions. Filtering R323 triple crossover entries by requiring the double crossover oscillator to be above a threshold (e.g., MACD line above zero and expanding) adds a regime condition that avoids low-probability environments.

## Trading Implication

Before acting on a 4-9-18 or similar triple crossover signal, check a double crossover oscillator like MACD; only take the trade when the oscillator’s absolute value exceeds a minimum level, confirming the market is not stuck in a range.
