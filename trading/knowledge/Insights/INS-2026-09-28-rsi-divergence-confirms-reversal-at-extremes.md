# RSI Divergence Confirms Reversal at Extremes

## Discovery Summary

The RSI divergence confirmation rule (EN036) validates reversal patterns signaled by RSI overbought/oversold levels (N165). The comparison of RSI and stochastic oscillators in volatile conditions (C149) further refines this confirmation, addressing the typical separation between pattern analysis and indicator use.

## Trading Implication

Wait for RSI price divergence to confirm a reversal after RSI enters extreme zones, ignoring reversals without divergence.
