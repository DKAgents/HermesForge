# Ideal Pullback Entry: 40–60% Retracement, Centered on 50%

## Discovery Summary

C050 (Secondary Trend Retracement Range) establishes Dow's 33–66% correction range with 50% most common. EN068 (40–60% Retracement Zones) narrows this to the actionable 40–60% pullback entry zone. N085 (Fibonacci Retracements) provides the precise 38%/50%/62% levels and ties them to trend strength. Together they reveal that the ideal pullback entry is a 50% retracement, with strong trends favoring the 38–50% sub-zone and weak trends the 50–62% sub-zone.

## Trading Implication

In an uptrend, wait for a pullback to retrace 40–60% of the prior leg (ideally 50%) before entering; in strong trends enter near 38–50%, in weaker trends allow deeper 50–62% retracements.
