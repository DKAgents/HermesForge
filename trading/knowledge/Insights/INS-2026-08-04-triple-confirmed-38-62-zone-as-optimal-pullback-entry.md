# Triple-Confirmed 38-62% Zone as Optimal Pullback Entry

## Discovery Summary

Three independent frameworks converge on the same retracement zone: Dow Theory (C050) identifies secondary corrections as retracing one-third to two-thirds (~33-66%) of prior moves; Murphy's entry rule (EN068) specifies 40-60% pullbacks as 'excellent' entry opportunities; Fibonacci levels (N085) place key retracements at 38%, 50%, and 62%. The overlap of all three is the 38-62% band, with the 50% level receiving triple confirmation as Dow's 'most common' retracement, Murphy's midpoint target, and a Fibonacci ratio.

## Trading Implication

In an uptrend, wait for pullbacks to reach the 38-50% Fibonacci retracement zone before entering long — if price stalls at 50% specifically, all three frameworks align, providing the highest-confidence entry signal; treat 62% as the maximum acceptable retracement before the trade thesis is invalidated.
