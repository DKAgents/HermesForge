# Objectify support-resistance flip penetration rule

## Discovery Summary

The concept of previous support as future resistance in a downtrend (C065) relies on identifying when support has been 'significantly' breached to trigger the role reversal. However, edge-condition E005 notes the subjectivity in defining such a significant penetration. To make the concept actionable, a trader must adopt a quantifiable, objective rule for penetration (e.g., a close beyond a fixed percentage or number of ATRs) rather than relying on subjective interpretation.

## Trading Implication

Define a specific, measurable threshold for 'significant penetration' (e.g., price closes 1% below support or 1.5 ATR below) before treating that level as new resistance in a downtrend.
