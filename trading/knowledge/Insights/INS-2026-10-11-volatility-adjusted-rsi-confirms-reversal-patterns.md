# Volatility-Adjusted RSI Confirms Reversal Patterns

## Discovery Summary

C149-rsi-vs-stochastics-volatility-comparison implies that RSI and stochastics respond differently to volatility, so the fixed overbought/oversold levels in N165-relative-strength-index-rsi-overboughtoversold-levels are not universally reliable for confirming reversal patterns. A trader should treat RSI extremes as confirmation only when volatility conditions match the chosen threshold, or prefer the oscillator more suited to the current volatility regime.

## Trading Implication

Before accepting an RSI overbought/oversold signal as confirmation of a reversal pattern, assess volatility and either scale the RSI thresholds or switch to stochastic readings that adapt better to that volatility.
