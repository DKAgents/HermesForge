# S/R flip sets stop, size via Murphy

## Discovery Summary

C065 describes how prior support becomes resistance in a downtrend, which R312 formalizes as the reversal-of-roles rule. Together, these notes confirm that a break below support flips that level into resistance, providing a natural stop placement above that level, which then determines position size via Murphy's risk management rules.

## Trading Implication

After a support breakdown, place your stop just above the old support level (now resistance) and size your position using Murphy's fixed-percentage risk rule to maintain consistent risk per trade.
