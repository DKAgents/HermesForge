# Earnings filter for role-reversal penetration

## Discovery Summary

E005 highlights the subjectivity in defining significant penetration of levels, while C065 describes previous support becoming resistance in a downtrend. The seed question proposes adding a mandatory earnings calendar check within 7 days as a binary filter to role-reversal entries, which directly addresses the subjectivity by requiring a concrete external condition to confirm the validity of the support-to-resistance flip.

## Trading Implication

Before taking a role-reversal entry (e.g., short at former support now resistance), verify that the company has no earnings announcement within the next 7 days; if it does, skip the trade to avoid false breakouts.
