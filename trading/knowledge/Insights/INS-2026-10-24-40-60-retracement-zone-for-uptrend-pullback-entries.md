# 40-60% Retracement Zone for Uptrend Pullback Entries

## Discovery Summary

C050-secondary-trend-retracement-range defines the typical retracement range within a secondary trend, while EN068-4060-retracement-zones-for-timing-entries prescribes a specific 40-60% retracement zone for entry timing. This narrows the broader concept to actionable entry levels, consistent with Murphy's ideal 50% retracement entry.

## Trading Implication

In an uptrend, only enter on pullbacks that retrace 40-60% of the prior swing, with the 50% level as the optimal entry point.
