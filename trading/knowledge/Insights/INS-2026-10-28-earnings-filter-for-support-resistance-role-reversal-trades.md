# Earnings filter for support-resistance role-reversal trades

## Discovery Summary

C065 describes how previous support becomes resistance in a downtrend, forming a role-reversal entry zone. E005 warns that defining a significant penetration of this zone is subjective, creating signal risk. The lesson-derived seed refines this by mandating an earnings calendar check as a binary filter: if earnings fall within 7 days, the role-reversal trade is disqualified. This connects subjective technical entry rules to objective event-risk avoidance.

## Trading Implication

Before any role-reversal entry, verify the earnings date; if within 7 days, stand aside or delay until after the report, regardless of penetration strength.
