# Commodity 10/50 crossover leads exporter equity trades

## Discovery Summary

The double crossover method (N039, EN028) uses 10/50-day MA crossovers to identify trends. Edge condition E040 notes commodity prices as a leading indicator for stress in commodity-exporting equity markets. By applying the crossover to a broad commodity index (e.g., CRB), traders obtain early signals to trade ETFs of countries like Australia or Canada, creating a sequential leading relationship.

## Trading Implication

Use the 10/50 crossover on a commodity index; sell exporter ETFs (e.g., EWA, EWC) on a bearish cross, buy on a bullish cross, and manage positions with the equity crossover as confirmation.
