# Triple-Source Retracement Confluence Defines Optimal Pullback Entry Zone

## Discovery Summary

Three independent frameworks converge on the same entry zone: Dow Theory (C050) defines secondary corrections as retracing one-third to two-thirds of the prior move; Murphy's entry rule (EN068) narrows the actionable window to 40-60%; and Fibonacci retracements (N085) place key levels at 38%, 50%, and 62%. The non-obvious insight is that the 40-60% entry zone from EN068 is not arbitrary but is bounded by the 38% Fibonacci minimum retracement in strong trends and the 62% maximum in weaker trends, while Dow's 50% most-common retracement sits exactly at the center of the actionable window.

## Trading Implication

In an uptrend, prioritize entries when price pulls back to the 50% retracement level, treating 38% as the early entry trigger in strong trends and 62% as the maximum acceptable retracement before the entry thesis is invalidated; a pullback exceeding two-thirds signals potential primary trend reversal per Dow Theory.
