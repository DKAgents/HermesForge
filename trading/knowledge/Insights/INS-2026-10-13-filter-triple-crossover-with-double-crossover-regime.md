# Filter Triple Crossover with Double-Crossover Regime

## Discovery Summary

C128 moving-averages-as-oscillators-via-double-crossover treats a dual moving-average crossover as an oscillator, which can identify ranging or choppy regimes. R323 triple-crossover-method-moving-averages and N037 triple-crossover-method-4-9-18-day-moving-average provide trend signals based on three moving averages. In volatile or ranging markets, those triple-crossover signals are prone to whipsaw, so the double-crossover oscillator from C128 can act as a regime filter before following R323/N037.

## Trading Implication

A trader should avoid taking 4/9/18 triple-crossover signals when the corresponding double-crossover spread is oscillating without directional persistence, and instead only act when the spread shows a sustained trend.
