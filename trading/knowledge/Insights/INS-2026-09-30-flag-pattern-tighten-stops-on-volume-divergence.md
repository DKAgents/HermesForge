# Flag pattern: tighten stops on volume divergence

## Discovery Summary

Flag and pennant patterns (N043) are continuation patterns where volume typically declines during the pause, creating a divergence from the trending price. Rule R082 requires heavy volume to confirm breakouts, and indicator N013 uses volume to filter false breakouts. This connection implies that when price continues but volume declines, a flag/pennant may be forming, so stops should be tightened until a high-volume breakout confirms the trend's resumption.

## Trading Implication

Traders should tighten stop-losses when volume diverges from price in a trend, and only move stops aggressively after a breakout accompanied by heavy volume.
