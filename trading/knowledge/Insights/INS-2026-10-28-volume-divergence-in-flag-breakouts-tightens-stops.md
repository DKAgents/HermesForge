# Volume divergence in flag breakouts tightens stops

## Discovery Summary

In flag/pennant patterns (N043), breakouts must be accompanied by heavy volume (R082) to be valid. Volume acts as a filter for false breakouts (N013). When volume diverges (e.g., low volume on breakout), the pattern likely fails, so stops should be placed just beyond the opposite side of the flag to limit losses.

## Trading Implication

Adjust stop-loss orders to just outside the flag/pennant boundary immediately if the breakout lacks heavy volume, rather than holding for a full trend continuation.
