# Oscillator Strategy Uses Secondary Trends to Time Primary-Trend Entries

## Discovery Summary

C366 secondary trends describes counter-trend corrections within a primary trend. EN041's oscillator entry strategy in trending markets becomes precise when applied to these secondary swings—the oscillator's extreme aligns with the end of the secondary trend, not a primary trend reversal. This resolves the conflict with counter-trend risk rules by showing that entering on oscillator extremes during a secondary trend is actually a primary-trend-aligned trade, not a counter-trend trade. The edge condition for oscillators in strong trends is therefore dependent on identifying the secondary trend's exhaustion point.

## Trading Implication

In a strong primary trend, take oscillator-based entries only when the oscillator reaches an extreme during a secondary correction, entering in the direction of the primary trend and placing stops beyond the secondary swing extreme to avoid mistaking the entry for a counter-trend trade.
