# Volatility-Adjusted RSI Divergence for Reversals

## Discovery Summary

C149 compares RSI and stochastics in volatile markets, indicating that standard overbought/oversold levels (N165) may become unreliable. EN036 provides rules for RSI divergence confirmation. Combining these, a trader can improve reversal pattern detection by requiring that divergence occur at extreme RSI levels (N165) and during elevated volatility (C149), as the signal is more significant under such conditions.

## Trading Implication

When identifying reversal patterns, prioritize RSI divergence signals that occur at overbought/oversold thresholds while volatility is high, as this combination provides stronger confirmation.
