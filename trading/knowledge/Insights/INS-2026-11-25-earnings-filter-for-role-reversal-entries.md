# Earnings Filter for Role-Reversal Entries

## Discovery Summary

The notes E005, C065, and R029 collectively describe the role-reversal principle for support/resistance and speedlines, but E005 explicitly acknowledges the subjectivity in defining a 'significant' penetration. This ambiguity creates uncertainty in entry timing. Adding a mandatory binary filter that blocks trades when earnings are within 7 days addresses this by eliminating a major catalyst-driven risk, making the role-reversal signal more robust and less dependent on subjective penetration thresholds.

## Trading Implication

Before entering any role-reversal trade (e.g., buying at former resistance or selling at former support), check the earnings calendar. If earnings are within 7 days, skip the trade to avoid price gaps and volatility that could invalidate the penetration signal.
