# Unified retracement zone: 40-60%, Fibonacci 38-62, ideal 50%

## Discovery Summary

C050 (Secondary Trend Retracement Range, pp. 61–80) establishes that corrections typically retrace one-third to two-thirds, most often 50%. EN068 (40–60% Retracement Zones for Timing Entries) turns that range into a timing rule: buy pullbacks of 40–60% in uptrends and short bounces in downtrends. N085 (Fibonacci Percentage Retracements) supplies exact levels—38%, 50%, and 62%—and notes that strong trends retrace to roughly 38% while weak trends can reach 62%. Thus the actionable entry zone is the 38–62% Fibonacci band, with 50% as the high-probability pivot.

## Trading Implication

In an uptrend, wait for a pullback of the prior advance to reach at least 38% and ideally 40–60% (centered on 50%) before entering long; in weak trends, allow the 61.8% level as a final entry. Mirror the approach for short entries in downtrends.
