# Earnings filter mandatory for role-reversal entries

## Discovery Summary

C065-previous-support-as-future-resistance-in-downtrend and R029-speed-resistance-lines-role-reversal rely on a broken level reversing roles, but E005-subjectivity-in-defining-significant-penetration admits that determining a significant break is subjective. An earnings announcement within 7 days can cause a gap through a level that has not been genuinely confirmed as broken, creating a false role-reversal signal. Therefore the seed's pre-trade earnings calendar check should be a mandatory binary filter before acting on any role-reversal entry.

## Trading Implication

Before entering a trade based on prior support becoming resistance or a broken speedline, check the earnings calendar; if earnings are within 7 days, stand aside or wait for post-earnings confirmation.
