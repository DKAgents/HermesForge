# Oscillator entry filter in secondary trends

## Discovery Summary

C366-secondary-trends describes counter-trend moves within a primary trend. EN041-oscillator-entry-strategy-in-trending-markets uses oscillators for entries in the direction of the trend. Combining them yields a rule: use oscillator oversold/overbought signals only for entries aligned with the primary trend, not against it, turning them into secondary trend entry triggers.

## Trading Implication

When the primary trend is strong, only take oscillator buy signals when price pulls back within the uptrend (secondary reaction), avoiding counter-trend trades that risk larger drawdowns.
