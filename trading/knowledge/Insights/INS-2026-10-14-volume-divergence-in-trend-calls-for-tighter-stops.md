# Volume Divergence in Trend Calls for Tighter Stops

## Discovery Summary

N043-flag-and-pennant-summary-characteristics describes a pause within a trend that typically resolves with a breakout. R082-breakouts-must-be-accompanied-by-heavy-volume requires volume confirmation for that breakout, while N013-volume-as-a-filter-for-false-breakouts shows that missing volume exposes false moves. When price extends a trend but volume diverges (e.g., new highs on falling volume), these notes combine to flag a weak breakout and justify adjusting stops tighter.

## Trading Implication

In a trend or flag/pennant context, if price makes a new extreme without rising volume, tighten stops to breakeven or below the pattern boundary to protect profits and limit loss from a false breakout.
