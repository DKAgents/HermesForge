# S/R Flip for Position Sizing

## Discovery Summary

The concept of prior support becoming resistance in a downtrend (C065, Murphy p.83) and its mirror for uptrends (C064) directly define key price levels for stop-loss placement. These stop distances then determine position size when combined with a fixed risk per trade, creating an actionable risk management rule.

## Trading Implication

In a downtrend, set stop-loss just above the prior support (now resistance) and size positions so that the dollar loss equals your risk limit; reverse for uptrends using prior peaks as support.
