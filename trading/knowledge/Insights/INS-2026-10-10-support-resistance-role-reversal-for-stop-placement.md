# Support/Resistance Role Reversal for Stop Placement

## Discovery Summary

Previous support becomes resistance in downtrends (C065) and previous resistance becomes support in uptrends (C064). Combining these with the seed question's insight that support/resistance determines stop distance yields a rule: place stops just beyond the flipped level to allow volatility while respecting role reversal, consistent with Murphy's technical analysis.

## Trading Implication

When shorting a downtrend, set stop-loss slightly above the former support (now resistance); when buying an uptrend, set stop-loss slightly below the former resistance (now support).
