# Quantify Role-Reversal Entries Using Earnings as Binary Filter

## Discovery Summary

E005 identifies that 'significant penetration' in role-reversal is inherently subjective (Murphy, p. 83), creating inconsistent entries. C065 and R029 both rely on this same subjective threshold to confirm resistance at former support — in downtrends (C065, p. 83) and at speed resistance lines (R029). The seed condition — a mandatory earnings calendar check within 7 days — directly addresses the subjectivity problem by adding an objective binary filter: earnings events amplify false role-reversal signals because volatility can create temporary penetrations that appear 'significant' but reverse post-announcement.

## Trading Implication

Before entering a short trade based on previous-support-turned-resistance (C065) or a broken speedline reversal (R029), a trader must check whether earnings are within 7 days; if so, skip the trade entirely to avoid mistaking earnings-driven volatility for a confirmed role reversal.
