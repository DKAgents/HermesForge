# Oscillator failure in strong trends: counter-trend risk

## Discovery Summary

C366-secondary-trends notes that powerful primary trends can override counter-trend signals. EN041-oscillator-entry-strategy-in-trending-markets provides a specific rule to enter with the trend when oscillators are overbought/oversold, not against it. RG017-overboughtoversold-readings-in-strong-trends warns that overbought/oversold extremes are unreliable for counter-trend trades in strong trends. Together, they form a filter: in a strong trend, ignore oscillator-based counter-trend entries and use oscillator extremes as continuation signals instead.

## Trading Implication

In a strong secondary trend, do not take counter-trend trades based on overbought/oversold oscillator readings. Instead, wait for an oscillator pullback to the trend line or moving average to enter with the trend.
