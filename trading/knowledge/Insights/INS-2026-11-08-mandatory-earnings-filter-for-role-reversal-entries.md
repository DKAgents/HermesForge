# Mandatory Earnings Filter for Role-Reversal Entries

## Discovery Summary

Role-reversal entries in C065-previous-support-as-future-resistance-in-downtrend and R029-speed-resistance-lines-role-reversal rely on technical levels that are already subject to ambiguity in defining a significant penetration (E005, p.83). A mandatory pre-trade earnings calendar check acts as a binary filter: if earnings are within 7 days, the technical role-reversal signal is unreliable because the level may be driven by event risk rather than pure supply/demand. This transforms a subjective penetration call into a concrete exclusion rule.

## Trading Implication

Before taking any role-reversal trade, check the earnings calendar and automatically stand down if the stock reports within 7 days; only proceed when the reversal is free of immediate earnings-event contamination.
