# 50% Retracement: Confluence for Ideal Pullback Entries

## Discovery Summary

C050 (Secondary Trend Retracement Range) states corrections typically retrace 1/3 to 2/3, with 50% most common. EN068-4060 explicitly cites Murphy's rule that 40-60% retracements offer excellent entry points. N085 (Fibonacci Percentage Retracements) includes 50% as a key level between the 38% (strong-trend minimum) and 62% (weak-trend maximum). Together, these three sources converge on the 50% retracement as the ideal entry zone for counter-trend pullbacks, combining Dow theory, Fibonacci ratios, and Murphy's practical rule.

## Trading Implication

Wait for a pullback in an uptrend to retrace 50% of the prior advance (within the 40-60% zone) before entering long, with a stop below the 62% level. In downtrends, use the same 50% bounce to initiate shorts.
