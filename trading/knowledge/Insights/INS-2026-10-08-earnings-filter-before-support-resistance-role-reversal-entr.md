# Earnings filter before support-resistance role-reversal entries

## Discovery Summary

E005 notes that defining a 'significant penetration' is subjective, while C065 uses previous support as future resistance in a downtrend. Adding a mandatory pre-trade earnings calendar check to the role-reversal entry criteria prevents subjective price penetrations around scheduled events from being treated as valid role-reversal signals. If earnings are within 7 days, the trader must either skip the entry or wait for confirmation after the event.

## Trading Implication

Before acting on a support-turned-resistance role-reversal setup, check the earnings calendar; if earnings are within 7 days, do not enter until the post-earnings price action confirms the penetration is genuine.
