# Oscillator pullback entries in secondary trends

## Discovery Summary

C366-secondary-trends describes counter-trend moves within a primary trend. EN041-oscillator-entry-strategy-in-trending-markets suggests using oscillators to time entries. Combining these, in strong trends oscillator extremes (overbought/oversold) often correspond to secondary trend pullbacks, not reversals; thus a filter is created: only take oscillator signals that align with the primary trend, using secondary trends as entry opportunities.

## Trading Implication

Ignore oscillator reversal signals against the primary trend; instead, use overbought/oversold readings to identify pullbacks (secondary trends) for entries in the direction of the primary trend.
