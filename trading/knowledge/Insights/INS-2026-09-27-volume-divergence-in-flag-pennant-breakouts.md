# Volume Divergence in Flag/Pennant Breakouts

## Discovery Summary

Flag and pennant patterns (N043) require a volume surge at breakout to confirm trend continuation. Rule R082 and indicator N013 state that valid breakouts must be on heavy volume; light volume breakouts are often false. Therefore, a breakout from a flag/pennant on light volume signals a likely false move, triggering a need for stop adjustment or reversal consideration.

## Trading Implication

Do not enter trades on flag/pennant breakouts unless volume is heavy. If a breakout occurs on light volume, tighten stops or consider shorting the false breakout.
