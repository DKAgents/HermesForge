# Intermarket sequence filters 10/50 stock crossover signals

## Discovery Summary

Murphy's intermarket chain (commodities → bonds → stocks) implies a lead-lag sequence across markets. The 4-9-18 triple crossover is faster and can time the leading commodity/bond legs, while the 10/50 double crossover is the stock position-timing trigger. Waiting for the commodity and bond crossovers to complete in chain order before taking a 10/50 stock crossover converts an isolated stock signal into an intermarket-confirmed entry.

## Trading Implication

Before acting on a 10/50 stock crossover, check that the 4/9/18 commodity crossover has already turned the chain-consistent way—for longs, commodities down and bonds up before buying stocks. This filters out intermediate stock entries that contradict the intermarket lead.
