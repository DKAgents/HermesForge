# Earnings Calendar as Objective Filter for Subjective Role-Reversal Entries

## Discovery Summary

E005 explicitly acknowledges that 'significant penetration' in role-reversal is subjective with no universal definition (Murphy, p. 83). C065 and R029 both describe role-reversal mechanics — violated support becoming resistance in downtrends and speedlines reversing roles — but neither addresses external event risk that could invalidate the technical signal. The seed question introduces an earnings calendar binary filter: since role-reversal entries already suffer from subjective penetration thresholds (E005), adding a hard earnings-within-7-days exclusion rule converts one dimension of ambiguity into a binary, rules-based gate, reducing the risk that a volatility event disguises or fakes a legitimate penetration.

## Trading Implication

Before entering any role-reversal trade (support-turned-resistance per C065, or speedline role-reversal per R029), a trader must check the earnings calendar; if an earnings event falls within 7 days, skip the trade entirely regardless of how clean the technical setup appears.
