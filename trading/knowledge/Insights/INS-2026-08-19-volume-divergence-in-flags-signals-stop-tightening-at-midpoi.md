# Volume Divergence in Flags Signals Stop Tightening at Midpoint

## Discovery Summary

Flags and pennants (N043) occur at roughly the midpoint of a move and require volume contraction during formation followed by a volume surge on breakout (R082). If volume fails to surge at breakout, the pattern is invalid per R082. Since flags occur near the midpoint (N043), and secondary corrections retrace 33-67% of prior moves (C050), a low-volume breakout from a flag near the 50% retracement zone suggests the move may be ending rather than resuming — a direct stop-tightening trigger.

## Trading Implication

If a flag or pennant breaks out without a volume surge near the 50% retracement level of the prior move, tighten stops aggressively or exit, as the absence of confirming volume at the pattern midpoint suggests the primary trend continuation is failing.
