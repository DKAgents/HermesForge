# Secondary Corrections Are the Entry Point for Oscillator Signals

## Discovery Summary

C366-secondary-trends defines intermediate counter-trend moves within the primary trend, while EN041-oscillator-entry-strategy-in-trending-markets turns those secondary corrections into entries by buying oversold conditions only when the primary trend is up and selling overbought conditions only when it is down. N186-mcclellan-oscillator supplies a breadth-based overbought/oversold reading for the broad market, so an oversold McClellan reading during a secondary correction can trigger a long in a bull market. This reconciles oscillator mean-reversion signals with the risk guideline against counter-trend trades: the oscillator times the end of the secondary move rather than fading the primary trend.

## Trading Implication

In a confirmed primary uptrend, wait for a secondary correction to push the McClellan oscillator (or another momentum oscillator) to an oversold reading, then buy; in a confirmed downtrend, wait for a secondary rally to an overbought reading, then short. Do not trade oscillator extremes against the primary trend, because oscillators can remain stretched in strong trends.
