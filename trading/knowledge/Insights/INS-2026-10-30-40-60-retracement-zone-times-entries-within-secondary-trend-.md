# 40-60% Retracement Zone Times Entries Within Secondary Trend Range

## Discovery Summary

C050-secondary-trend-retracement-range establishes that secondary corrections occur within a defined retracement range of the prior trend. EN068-4060-retracement-zones-for-timing-entries adds the specific condition that entries inside an uptrend should be timed to the 40-60% retracement zone, not any random pullback depth. This links the broader concept to a precise entry rule: wait for the pullback to enter that zone before acting.

## Trading Implication

In an uptrend, avoid buying until the pullback retraces into the 40-60% zone of the prior upswing; treat moves beyond that zone as a warning that the secondary trend may be failing.
