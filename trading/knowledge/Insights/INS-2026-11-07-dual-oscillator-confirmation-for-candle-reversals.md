# Dual Oscillator Confirmation for Candle Reversals

## Discovery Summary

The 'RSI vs. Stochastics Volatility Comparison' note states that best signals occur when both RSI and stochastics are simultaneously overbought or oversold. Combined with 'Filtered Candle Patterns — Oscillator Alternatives' (R177), which allows any oscillator to filter candle patterns, this implies a stronger reversal confirmation: a candle reversal pattern is valid only when both oscillators are in their presignal zones. RSI's lower volatility suggests stochastics may trigger first, but RSI confirms the extreme condition.

## Trading Implication

Wait for both RSI (above 70 for sell, below 30 for buy) and stochastics to be in overbought/oversold territory before acting on a bullish/bearish reversal candle pattern, increasing signal reliability.
