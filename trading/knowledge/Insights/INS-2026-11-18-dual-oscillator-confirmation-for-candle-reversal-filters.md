# Dual Oscillator Confirmation for Candle Reversal Filters

## Discovery Summary

C149 states that RSI is less volatile than stochastics and that the best signals occur when both are simultaneously overbought/oversold. N165 adds that RSI overbought (>70) or oversold (<30) with divergence warns of trend reversal. R177 establishes that any oscillator can filter candle patterns, but only when it is in its presignal area. Together, they imply that a robust reversal confirmation requires both RSI and stochastics to be in extreme territory simultaneously, and RSI divergence adds further weight.

## Trading Implication

Before acting on a candle reversal pattern, require both RSI and stochastics to be in their respective overbought/oversold zones and check for RSI divergence to increase signal reliability.
