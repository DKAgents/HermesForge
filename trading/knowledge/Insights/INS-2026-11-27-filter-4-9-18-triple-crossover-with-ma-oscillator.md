# Filter 4-9-18 Triple Crossover with MA Oscillator

## Discovery Summary

C128 describes the double-crossover method as an oscillator (MACD), while R323 and N037 define the 4-9-18 triple crossover as a standalone signal generator. The 4-9-18 rule is trend-following and lacks a regime filter, so it is prone to whipsaws in volatile or ranging markets. The 9-18 moving-average spread from the 4-9-18 system can be treated as a double-crossover oscillator and used to confirm the direction of triple-crossover signals.

## Trading Implication

Take 4-9-18 triple-crossover signals only when the slower 9-18 moving-average spread is aligned in the same direction; otherwise stand aside to avoid range-bound false signals.
