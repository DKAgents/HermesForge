# Dual oscillator confirmation for candle patterns

## Discovery Summary

C149 (RSI vs. Stochastics Volatility Comparison) states that the best signals occur when both oscillators are simultaneously in overbought or oversold territory. R177 (Filtered Candle Patterns — Oscillator Alternatives) allows any oscillator to filter candle patterns. Combining these, a trader can require both RSI and stochastics to be in extreme zones before accepting a candle reversal pattern, increasing reliability.

## Trading Implication

Only take a candle reversal pattern as valid when both RSI and stochastics are in overbought (for bearish) or oversold (for bullish) territory simultaneously.
