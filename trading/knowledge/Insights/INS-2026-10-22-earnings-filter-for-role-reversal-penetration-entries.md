# Earnings filter for role-reversal penetration entries

## Discovery Summary

Role-reversal entries depend on C065-previous-support-as-future-resistance-in-downtrend, but E005-subjectivity-in-defining-significant-penetration warns that penetration calls are subjective. Adding a mandatory pre-trade earnings calendar check as a binary filter removes a major source of false penetrations, since earnings-driven moves can mimic structural role reversals. This converts part of the subjective entry judgment into an objective go/no-go condition.

## Trading Implication

Before taking any role-reversal entry, check the earnings calendar and treat earnings within 7 days as a hard disqualifier, avoiding ambiguous penetration signals caused by event-driven price moves.
