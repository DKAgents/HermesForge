# 40-60% retracement zone for entry timing

## Discovery Summary

C050-secondary-trend-retracement-range defines the typical retracement range of a secondary trend (e.g., 33% to 66%). EN068-4060-retracement-zones-for-timing-entries specifies a narrower 40-60% zone as the ideal entry point per Murphy. This adds a precise condition to the broader concept, making the entry rule more actionable.

## Trading Implication

On a pullback within a secondary uptrend, only enter if the retracement falls into the 40-60% range of the prior move, as this zone historically offers the best risk/reward.
