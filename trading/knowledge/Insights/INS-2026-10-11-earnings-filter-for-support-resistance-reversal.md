# Earnings filter for support-resistance reversal

## Discovery Summary

E005 highlights the subjectivity in defining significant penetration of support/resistance levels, which directly impacts the reliability of the role-reversal pattern in C065 (previous support becomes resistance in a downtrend). The seed question adds a mandatory earnings calendar check within 7 days as a binary filter, making the entry criteria more objective and reducing false signals from earnings-driven volatility.

## Trading Implication

When trading role-reversal setups in downtrends, define a specific penetration threshold (e.g., 1-2% close beyond support) and then apply an earnings calendar check to avoid entries within 7 days of earnings.
