# Use 10/50 crossovers across Murphy's commodity-bond-stock chain

## Discovery Summary

N039 and EN028 describe the 10/50 day double crossover as a timely intermediate-term stock signal, but this same signal can be applied to each market in Murphy's intermarket chain: commodities → bonds → stocks. Because these markets tend to lead and lag in sequence, a 10/50 crossover in commodities should be read as an early warning for bonds, and a crossover in bonds as an early warning for stocks. This creates an intermarket-confirmed position trading signal that is earlier and more robust than the stock crossover alone.

## Trading Implication

Track 10/50 day crossovers in commodity futures, Treasury bond futures, and stock index futures; take long stock positions only when the chain is bullish in sequence, and exit or reduce exposure when the commodity crossover reverses before the stock crossover does.
