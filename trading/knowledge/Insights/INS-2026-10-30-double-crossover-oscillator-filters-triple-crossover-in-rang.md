# Double Crossover Oscillator Filters Triple Crossover in Ranging Markets

## Discovery Summary

C128 shows how a double moving-average crossover can be treated as an oscillator rather than a trend signal, offering a regime check for R323 and N037's 4-9-18-day triple crossover method. In volatile or ranging markets, the triple crossover is prone to repeated false signals; the oscillator interpretation helps identify chop and avoid those conditions.

## Trading Implication

Traders should apply the triple crossover method only when the double-crossover oscillator confirms a sustained directional trend, and stand aside when it indicates range-bound oscillation.
