# Simultaneous RSI and Stochastics Extremes Confirm Candle Reversal Patterns

## Discovery Summary

R177 states any oscillator can filter candle reversal patterns, but only when the oscillator is already in its presignal overbought/oversold zone. N165 defines RSI's presignal zone as above 70 or below 30. C149 adds that RSI reaches extremes less often than stochastics, and the strongest confirmations occur when RSI and stochastics are simultaneously in overbought or oversold territory. Therefore, the most reliable filter for a candle reversal pattern is to require both oscillators to be in their extreme zones at the same time, rather than relying on either one alone.

## Trading Implication

Before acting on a reversal candle pattern, wait for RSI to be above 70 for bearish reversals or below 30 for bullish reversals while stochastics is simultaneously in the corresponding overbought or oversold zone.
