# Triple-Source Confluence at 38-62% Defines Optimal Pullback Entry Zone

## Discovery Summary

Three independent frameworks converge on the same retracement zone: Dow Theory (C050) identifies one-third to two-thirds (33-66%) as the normal secondary correction range; Murphy's entry rule (EN068) narrows this to 40-60% as the actionable entry zone; Fibonacci retracements (N085) independently validate 38%, 50%, and 62% as key levels within the same range. The non-obvious insight is that 38-62% is not merely a trading rule but a zone where Dow Theory, classical technical analysis, and Fibonacci mathematics all independently agree, giving the 50% level triple confirmation as the highest-probability entry point.

## Trading Implication

In an uptrend, prioritize entries specifically when a pullback reaches the 50% retracement level (triple-confirmed by all three frameworks); treat the 38% and 62% levels as the outer boundaries where position sizing should be adjusted based on whether the trend is strong (enter near 38%) or weak (wait for 62% before committing full size).
