# Triangulating Pullback Entries: Dow, Murphy, and Fibonacci Agree on 38-62%

## Discovery Summary

Three independent frameworks converge on the same retracement zone for trend-following entries. Dow's secondary trend theory (C050) identifies 1/3 to 2/3 as the expected correction range. Murphy's entry rule (EN068) operationalizes this as 40-60% pullbacks for long entries. Fibonacci retracements (N085) provide sub-zones within that range: 38% as a strong-trend minimum, 50% as the classic midpoint, and 62% as the weak-trend maximum. The non-obvious insight is that trend strength itself — not just the pullback level — determines which Fibonacci sub-level within the 40-60% window is the highest-probability entry.

## Trading Implication

In a confirmed uptrend, a trader should distinguish strong from weak trend conditions: target the 38-42% retracement zone for high-momentum entries and reserve 58-62% zone entries for weaker or later-stage trends, using the midpoint 50% as a default when trend strength is ambiguous.
