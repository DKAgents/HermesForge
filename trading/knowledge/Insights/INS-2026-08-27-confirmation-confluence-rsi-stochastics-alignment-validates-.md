# Confirmation confluence: RSI-Stochastics alignment validates filtered candle reversal patterns

## Discovery Summary

C149 establishes that RSI is less volatile than Stochastics and the best signals occur when both are simultaneously overbought or oversold. R177 expands this by stating any oscillator can filter candle patterns, requiring the oscillator to be in its presignal area before a reversal pattern is considered valid. N165 defines RSI overbought/oversold levels at 70/30. The connection: requiring both RSI and Stochastics to be in extreme zones simultaneously creates a superior filter for candle reversal patterns, reducing false signals from the more volatile Stochastics alone.

## Trading Implication

Before acting on a candle reversal pattern, confirm that both RSI (14-period, above 70 or below 30) and Stochastics are simultaneously in overbought or oversold territory per Murphy's dual-oscillator rule.
