# 40-60% retracement entry in secondary trends

## Discovery Summary

Concept C050 (secondary-trend-retracement-range) describes typical retracements of 33-67% in secondary reactions. Rule EN068 (4060-retracement-zones-for-timing-entries) refines this to a specific 40-60% zone for entry timing, aligning with Murphy's ideal entry near 50% retracement. This narrows the broad concept into a precise actionable rule for pullback entries in uptrends.

## Trading Implication

Wait for pullbacks to retrace 40-60% of the prior impulse move before entering long in an uptrend; this zone offers an optimal risk-reward balance within the expected secondary trend range.
