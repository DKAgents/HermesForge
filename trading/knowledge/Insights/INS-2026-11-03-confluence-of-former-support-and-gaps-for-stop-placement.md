# Confluence of Former Support and Gaps for Stop Placement

## Discovery Summary

C065 establishes that in a downtrend, violated support flips to resistance, while EN069 shows that gaps act as support/resistance for timing entries. When a price gap forms at or near a former support level that has become resistance, the two levels reinforce each other, giving a more precise short entry zone and a tighter stop (above the gap/resistance). The stop distance from entry, per the seed question, directly drives position sizing under fixed-risk rules.

## Trading Implication

In a downtrend, wait for a rally into a gap that coincides with a former support level now turned resistance; sell into that zone with a stop above the confluence, then size the position based on the stop distance and your predefined risk per trade.
