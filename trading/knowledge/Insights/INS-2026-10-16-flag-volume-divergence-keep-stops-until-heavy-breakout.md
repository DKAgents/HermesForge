# Flag Volume Divergence: Keep Stops Until Heavy Breakout

## Discovery Summary

N043 flag and pennant summary characteristics shows volume normally contracts during the pattern, so price/volume divergence inside the flag is not itself a warning. N013 volume as a filter for false breakouts and R082 breakouts must be accompanied by heavy volume both condition the pattern's validity on volume expansion at breakout. Therefore, stops should not be tightened merely because volume diverges during the consolidation; they should sit outside the pattern until a heavy-volume breakout confirms continuation.

## Trading Implication

Place stops beyond the flag/pennant boundary and keep them there until the breakout occurs on heavy volume. After a heavy-volume breakout, tighten/trail stops with the trend; if the breakout lacks volume, do not trail stops as if the trend has resumed.
