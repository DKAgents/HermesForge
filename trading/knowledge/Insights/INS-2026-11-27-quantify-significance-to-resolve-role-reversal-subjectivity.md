# Quantify Significance to Resolve Role-Reversal Subjectivity

## Discovery Summary

Note E005 explicitly acknowledges the subjectivity in defining 'significant' penetration for role reversal, which applies equally to support/resistance (C065) and speedline (R029) reversals. Without a quantitative threshold, traders cannot systematically confirm when a broken level truly reverses. By adopting a precise rule—such as a close beyond the level by a fixed percentage or over two consecutive periods—this subjectivity is eliminated, making role-reversal signals deterministic and testable.

## Trading Implication

Adopt a predefined penetration criterion (e.g., 1.5% close beyond a support/resistance level or two daily closes) for all role-reversal entries, including speedlines, to avoid discretionary errors and improve consistency.
