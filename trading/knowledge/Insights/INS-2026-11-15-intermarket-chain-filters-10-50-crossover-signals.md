# Intermarket Chain Filters 10/50 Crossover Signals

## Discovery Summary

N039 and EN028 both describe the 10/50-day moving average crossover as a stock trend timing signal. Murphy's intermarket chain (commodities → bonds → stocks) implies the crossover should be read as the final confirmation in a sequence, not as a standalone trigger. A position trader can wait for the earlier intermarket links to align before acting on the crossover, improving timing and reducing whipsaws.

## Trading Implication

Take the 10/50 crossover in stocks only when the prior intermarket links are supportive, and use the crossover to manage exits when the chain turns against the position.
