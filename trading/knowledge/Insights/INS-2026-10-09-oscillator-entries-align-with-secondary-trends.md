# Oscillator entries align with secondary trends

## Discovery Summary

C366-secondary-trends defines counter-trend moves within a primary trend. EN041-oscillator-entry-strategy-in-trending-markets typically uses oscillator extremes to time entries. The apparent conflict with risk rules against counter-trend trades is resolved because the strategy uses oversold signals in an uptrend (buying the secondary dip) to enter with the primary trend, not against it.

## Trading Implication

In a strong primary uptrend, use oscillator oversold signals to buy pullbacks, and avoid overbought signals that would trigger counter-trend sells.
