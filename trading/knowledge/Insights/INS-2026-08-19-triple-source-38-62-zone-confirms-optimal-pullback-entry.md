# Triple-Source 38–62% Zone Confirms Optimal Pullback Entry

## Discovery Summary

Three independent frameworks converge on the same retracement zone: Dow Theory (C050) defines secondary corrections as retracing one-third to two-thirds (~33–67%) of the prior move; Murphy's entry rule (EN068) narrows the actionable window to 40–60% for timing long entries in uptrends; Fibonacci levels (N085) place key retracement markers at 38%, 50%, and 62%, with 50% being the most common. The 40–60% entry zone from EN068 is bracketed by the 38% and 62% Fibonacci levels from N085, and falls within Dow's secondary correction range from C050 — three distinct analytical traditions defining essentially the same zone.

## Trading Implication

In an uptrend, initiate or add long positions only when price retraces into the 38–62% zone of the prior advance, treating the 50% level as the highest-probability entry point; this zone has triple-framework confirmation and reduces the risk of entering too early or too late in a correction.
