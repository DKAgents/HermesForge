# Oscillator lies in strong trends: trade secondary pullbacks

## Discovery Summary

In strong trends, oscillators produce misleading overbought/oversold signals (RG017). The oscillator entry strategy (EN041) should only be applied in direction of the primary trend, using secondary trends (C366) as pullbacks for entry. Counter-trend entries based on oscillator extremes in strong trends are invalid due to false signals.

## Trading Implication

Traders should ignore overbought/oversold readings in strong trends for counter-trend trades and instead use such extremes as entry signals for continuation trades in the primary trend direction during secondary pullbacks.
