# Inflation-Adjusted Commodity Crossover Leads Stocks

## Discovery Summary

Applying the 10/50 crossover method (N039) to an inflation-adjusted commodity index (R310) can identify deflationary conditions described in E040. Per Murphy's intermarket chain, a bearish commodity crossover signals falling bond yields and a subsequent stock decline, providing a leading indicator for position trading.

## Trading Implication

Use a 10/50 crossover on a deflation-adjusted commodity ETF; when it turns bearish, reduce long exposure in stocks or prepare for a potential downturn in the next intermarket phase.
