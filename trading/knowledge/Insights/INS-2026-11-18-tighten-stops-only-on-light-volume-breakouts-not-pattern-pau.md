# Tighten Stops Only on Light-Volume Breakouts, Not Pattern Pause

## Discovery Summary

N043 flag/pennant characteristics show that volume drying up during the one-to-three-week pause is normal, not a warning. R082 and N013 add that validity is determined at the breakout: heavy volume confirms, while light volume marks a false breakout. Therefore, a trader should not tighten stops just because volume declines while price is still in the flag/pennant; only a light-volume breakout, or a heavy-volume decline after such a breakout, warrants tighter stops.

## Trading Implication

Keep the normal pattern-based stop during the flag/pennant pause; if price breaks out without a volume surge, tighten stops or exit because the continuation signal is invalid.
