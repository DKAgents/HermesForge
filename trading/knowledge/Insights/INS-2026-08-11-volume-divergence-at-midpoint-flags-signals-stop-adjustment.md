# Volume Divergence at Midpoint Flags Signals Stop Adjustment

## Discovery Summary

Flags and pennants (N043) occur at roughly the midpoint of a move, with volume drying up during consolidation and surging on breakout per R082. When volume fails to surge on the breakout — i.e., diverges from price — R082 signals the breakout is invalid. C050 adds context: if the consolidation has retraced more than one-third of the prior flagpole move, the pattern may already be failing as a continuation and transitioning into a secondary correction, suggesting the stop should be tightened toward the two-thirds retracement level rather than held below the full flagpole.

## Trading Implication

If a flag or pennant breakout occurs on below-average volume AND the consolidation has retraced more than 33% of the flagpole, tighten stops to just below the 50% retracement level rather than the standard below-pattern placement, as both signals suggest the continuation thesis is weakening.
