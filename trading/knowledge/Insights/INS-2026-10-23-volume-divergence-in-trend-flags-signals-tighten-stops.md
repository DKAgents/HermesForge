# Volume divergence in trend flags signals tighten stops

## Discovery Summary

N043 flag/pennant patterns are continuation setups, but R082 states breakouts must be backed by heavy volume. N013 further refines this by using volume to filter false breakouts. So when price moves in the breakout direction but volume diverges or fails to expand, the pattern signal is suspect and stops should be moved tighter instead of relying on standard pattern targets.

## Trading Implication

If a flag or pennant breakout lacks volume confirmation, tighten stops to breakeven or just beyond the recent swing point. Only maintain normal pattern-based stops when breakout volume is clearly heavy.
