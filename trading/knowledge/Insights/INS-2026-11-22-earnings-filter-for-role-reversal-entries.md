# Earnings filter for role-reversal entries

## Discovery Summary

C065 and R029 both rely on role-reversal after a significant penetration, but E005 notes that defining 'significant' is subjective (Murphy, p.83). Adding a mandatory pre-trade earnings calendar check removes a major source of false or unreliable penetrations by barring trades within 7 days of earnings. This creates a concrete binary filter around the otherwise subjective role-reversal rule.

## Trading Implication

Before entering any support/resistance or speedline role-reversal trade, check the company's earnings date; if earnings are within 7 days, skip the trade entirely.
