# Earnings Filter for Subjective Role-Reversal Penetrations

## Discovery Summary

E005 notes that defining a 'significant penetration' is inherently subjective, which is critical for C065's support-to-resistance role reversal because the trade depends on a definitive break. Using an earnings calendar as a mandatory binary filter — skipping entries when earnings are within 7 days — removes a primary source of non-technical volatility that could otherwise create false penetration signals and invalid role reversals.

## Trading Implication

Before acting on a support/resistance role-reversal signal, check the earnings calendar; if the company reports within 7 days, do not enter the trade to avoid subjective penetration calls driven by earnings noise.
