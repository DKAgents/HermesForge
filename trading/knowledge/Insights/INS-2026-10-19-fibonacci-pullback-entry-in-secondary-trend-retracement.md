# Fibonacci pullback entry in secondary trend retracement

## Discovery Summary

The concept C050 defines secondary trend retracement ranges as typically 33% to 66% of the prior primary move. Rule EN068-4060 refines timing entries within those retracements using Fibonacci levels, with Murphy noting 38.2% to 50% as ideal pullback entry zones. Together, they confirm that entries near the 38.2%–50% Fibonacci zone are both theoretically sound (within the secondary retracement range) and actionable for timing.

## Trading Implication

In an uptrend, wait for a pullback to the 38.2%–50% Fibonacci retracement of the prior swing before entering long, as this aligns with the natural secondary trend retracement range and Murphy's ideal entry point.
