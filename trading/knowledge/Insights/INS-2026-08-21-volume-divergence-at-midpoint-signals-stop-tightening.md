# Volume Divergence at Midpoint Signals Stop Tightening

## Discovery Summary

Flags and pennants (N043) are expected to occur at the midpoint of a move and resume on a burst of volume (characteristic 3). R082 states that breakouts without heavy volume are suspect. C050 establishes that secondary corrections retrace 33-66% of prior moves. If a flag/pennant breakout occurs near the 50% retracement level but WITHOUT the required volume surge (per R082), the breakout is unconfirmed — meaning the pattern may actually be resolving as a full secondary correction rather than a continuation, warranting immediate stop tightening to the recent consolidation low rather than the full flagpole extension.

## Trading Implication

When a flag or pennant breakout occurs near the 50% retracement zone (C050) and lacks confirming volume surge (R082), tighten stops to just below the consolidation pattern rather than using the measured flagpole target, as the continuation signal is unconfirmed and a full secondary correction remains possible.
