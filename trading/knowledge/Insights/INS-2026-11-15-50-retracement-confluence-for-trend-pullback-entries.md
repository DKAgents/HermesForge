# 50% Retracement Confluence for Trend Pullback Entries

## Discovery Summary

C050's Dow-based observation that secondary retracements commonly reach ~50% aligns with EN068's Murphy rule that 40–60% pullbacks provide excellent entries, and N085's Fibonacci level of 50%. Together they converge on the 50% retracement as the high-probability midpoint where a pullback entry is most favorable. N085 adds the crucial condition of trend strength: strong trends tend to bottom near 38%, while weak trends extend to 62%, so the 40–60% zone should be refined accordingly.

## Trading Implication

On pullbacks in the direction of the primary trend, place entry orders near the 50% retracement of the prior swing, favoring the 38% side in strong trends and treating the 62% level as the invalidation boundary in weaker trends.
