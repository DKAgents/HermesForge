# Earnings Filter Resolves Subjectivity in Role-Reversal Entry Timing

## Discovery Summary

E005 identifies that 'significant penetration' for role-reversal is inherently subjective (Murphy, p. 83), creating ambiguity in entry timing. C065 and R029 both describe role-reversal mechanics across price levels and speedlines, meaning multiple potential resistance/support flip signals may appear simultaneously. The seed question introduces an earnings calendar binary filter — since earnings events can invalidate or dramatically distort role-reversal setups (a resistance bounce becomes a gap-through event), requiring a mandatory 7-day earnings exclusion window converts the subjective 'significance' problem into a partially objective one: any role-reversal signal within 7 days of earnings is automatically disqualified, regardless of how 'significant' the penetration appears.

## Trading Implication

Before acting on any support-turned-resistance or resistance-turned-support entry (whether from price levels per C065 or speedlines per R029), check the earnings calendar: if earnings are within 7 days, skip the trade entirely to avoid having the subjectivity of 'significance' compounded by binary event risk.
