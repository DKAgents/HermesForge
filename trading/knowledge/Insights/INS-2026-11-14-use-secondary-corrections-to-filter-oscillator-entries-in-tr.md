# Use Secondary Corrections to Filter Oscillator Entries in Trends

## Discovery Summary

EN041's 'Oscillator Entry Strategy in Trending Markets' says to buy oversold conditions in an uptrend, but strong trends can leave oscillators overbought/oversold for extended periods. C366 'Secondary Trends' supplies the missing timing filter: these oscillator signals should be taken during a 3-week-to-months counter-trend correction within the primary trend. N186 'McClellan Oscillator' can confirm the broad-market oversold/overbought extreme that marks such a secondary correction.

## Trading Implication

Do not act on every oscillator signal; wait for a secondary correction in the primary trend and confirm it with the McClellan Oscillator before entering in the direction of the primary trend.
