# Converging Retracement Zones Pinpoint 50% Entry

## Discovery Summary

C050-secondary-trend-retracement-range (Dow) states corrections typically retrace one-third to two-thirds, most commonly 50%. EN068-4060-retracement-zones-for-timing-entries (Murphy) explicitly recommends 40-60% retracements for entries. N085-fibonacci-percentage-retracements-38-50-62 highlights 38%, 50%, and 62% as key levels. These three independent frameworks converge on a 50% retracement as the ideal entry zone, with Murphy's 40-60% range providing a precise actionable band and Fibonacci levels offering boundaries.

## Trading Implication

In an uptrend, wait for a pullback that retraces 40-60% of the prior advance (ideally near the 50% Fibonacci level) before initiating long positions, and use 38% and 62% as confirmation limits for trend strength.
