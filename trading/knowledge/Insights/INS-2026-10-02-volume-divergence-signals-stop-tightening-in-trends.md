# Volume Divergence Signals Stop Tightening in Trends

## Discovery Summary

N043 describes flag and pennant patterns as continuation setups, which typically resolve on breakouts. R082 and N013 both stress that breakouts require heavy volume to be valid, and volume acts as a filter against false breakouts. When volume diverges from price within a trend (e.g., new highs on declining volume), the pattern's resolve becomes suspect, and the same principle applies to stop placement: if a breakout or trend extension lacks volume confirmation, stops should be tightened to protect against a failed move.

## Trading Implication

Traders should monitor volume relative to price during trend continuations and breakouts. If volume fails to confirm new price extremes or breakout direction, tighten stops to reduce risk of being caught in a false move.
