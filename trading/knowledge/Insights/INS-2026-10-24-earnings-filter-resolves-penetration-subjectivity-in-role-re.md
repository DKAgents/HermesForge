# Earnings filter resolves penetration subjectivity in role-reversal

## Discovery Summary

The subjectivity in defining significant penetration (E005) undermines the reliability of the previous-support-as-future-resistance concept (C065) during downtrends. Adding a mandatory pre-trade earnings calendar check as a binary filter eliminates the need to subjectively judge penetration significance by skipping trades near earnings, thus improving the signal quality of role-reversal entries.

## Trading Implication

Before entering a role-reversal trade based on support turning to resistance, check if earnings are within 7 days; if yes, skip the trade to avoid false penetrations.
