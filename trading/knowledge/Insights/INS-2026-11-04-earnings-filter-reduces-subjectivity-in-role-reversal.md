# Earnings Filter Reduces Subjectivity in Role Reversal

## Discovery Summary

The notes E005 and R029 both acknowledge that role-reversal identification is subjective (e.g., defining 'significant penetration' in support/resistance, or speedline breaks). The seed question proposes adding a mandatory earnings calendar check as a binary filter to role-reversal entries, which directly addresses this subjectivity by providing an objective, external criterion that can prevent false confirmations around earnings events. This transforms a discretionary technical signal into a rules-based entry with a clear disqualifying condition.

## Trading Implication

Before entering any trade based on support/resistance or speedline role reversal, check the earnings calendar. If the company reports within 7 days, skip the trade or require a confirmed post-earnings breakout instead.
