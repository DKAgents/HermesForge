# Murphy's Ideal Entry: 40-60% Retracement Zone Confirmed

## Discovery Summary

C050 describes secondary trend retracement ranges, which typically extend 1/3 to 2/3 of the primary move. EN068-4060 specifies 40-60% as the optimal timing zone for entries, while N085 provides the Fibonacci anchors (38.2%, 50%, 61.8%) that bracket that zone. Murphy's ideal entry point for pullbacks in uptrends is the 50% retracement, which sits squarely within the 40-60% range and between the key Fibonacci levels, creating a multi-domain confirmation for a precise entry filter.

## Trading Implication

Traders should wait for pullbacks in uptrends to reach the 40-60% (or 38.2-61.8% Fibonacci) retracement zone before initiating long positions, using this confluence as a higher-probability timing trigger.
