# Double crossover reduces whipsaws, confirms rule

## Discovery Summary

E020 explains that using a double crossover (10 & 50-day) reduces whipsaws compared to a single average crossover. This directly supports the EN028 rule and validates the N039 indicator method, giving traders a reason to prefer it over simpler alternatives.

## Trading Implication

Use the 10- and 50-day moving average crossover for trend signals, as it is more robust against whipsaws than single average crossovers.
