# 50% Retracement: Ideal Entry Across Dow, Murphy, Fibonacci

## Discovery Summary

Dow's secondary correction range (33-66%) and Murphy's 40-60% retracement zone (EN068-4060) both center on 50%, which is also the most famous Fibonacci level (N085). The Fibonacci levels 38%, 50%, and 62% provide precise entry points within the broader Dow range, and Murphy's rule explicitly uses 40-60% for entries. The non-obvious insight is that trend strength determines which Fibonacci level to use: strong trends retrace to 38%, weak trends to 62%, with 50% as the default, effectively merging the three frameworks into a single decision rule.

## Trading Implication

In an uptrend, enter long on a pullback to 38% (strong trend), 50% (normal), or 62% (weak trend) of the prior advance; abandon the entry if the pullback exceeds 62%, as it likely signals trend weakness or reversal.
