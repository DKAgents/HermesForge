# 50% retracement as ideal entry zone

## Discovery Summary

Dow theory (C050) states secondary retracements commonly retrace 50% of the prior move, Murphy's rule (EN068) recommends entries on 40-60% pullbacks, and Fibonacci retracements (N085) highlight 50% as a key level. Together they converge on the 50% retracement as the ideal entry point within Murphy's zone, with the 38% level serving as a minimum in strong trends and 62% as a maximum in weak trends.

## Trading Implication

In an uptrend, wait for a pullback to the 50% Fibonacci retracement level of the prior advance to initiate long positions; if the trend is strong, consider entering near 38% instead, and if weak, allow a deeper pullback to 62%.
