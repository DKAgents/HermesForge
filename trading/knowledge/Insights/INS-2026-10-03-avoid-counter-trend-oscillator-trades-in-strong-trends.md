# Avoid counter-trend oscillator trades in strong trends

## Discovery Summary

C366-secondary-trends describes counter-trend movements within a primary trend. EN041-oscillator-entry-strategy-in-trending-markets likely recommends entries in trend direction. RG017-overboughtoversold-readings-in-strong-trends warns that overbought/oversold readings are unreliable in strong trends, making counter-trend trades risky. Together, they confirm that oscillator extremes in strong trends should not be used as counter-trade signals.

## Trading Implication

When a strong trend is present, ignore overbought/oversold oscillator readings for counter-trend entries; only use oscillator signals that align with the primary trend.
