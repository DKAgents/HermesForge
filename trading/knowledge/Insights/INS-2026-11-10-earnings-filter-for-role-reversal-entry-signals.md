# Earnings Filter for Role-Reversal Entry Signals

## Discovery Summary

C065 and R029 both rely on the role-reversal of prior support/resistance or speedlines after a penetration, but E005 admits that defining a 'significant penetration' is subjective. Earnings announcements create gap risk and elevated volatility that can produce misleading penetrations and retests, making the role-reversal signal unreliable. Adding a mandatory pre-trade earnings calendar check as a binary filter removes a major source of false signals around these subjective price levels.

## Trading Implication

Before acting on any role-reversal entry based on prior support/resistance or speedline breaks, check the earnings calendar; if earnings are within 7 days, stand aside until the event passes.
