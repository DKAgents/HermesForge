# Trade oscillator pullbacks in secondary trends, not counter-trend fades

## Discovery Summary

EN041-oscillator-entry-strategy-in-trending-markets identifies the edge that oscillators remain overbought/oversold in strong trends, so extreme readings do not justify counter-trend entries. C366-secondary-trends defines the corrective move within a primary trend that can offer a safer timing window. Together they add a condition to counter-trend risk rules: only take an oscillator-based entry when it aligns with the secondary trend and the primary trend, never as a pure fade of an extreme. This turns a vague 'avoid counter-trend trades' rule into a specific filter for oscillator signals.

## Trading Implication

In a strong trend, ignore oscillator extremes as standalone reversal signals; wait for a secondary pullback in the direction of the primary trend and enter only on oscillator confirmation aligned with that higher timeframe trend.
