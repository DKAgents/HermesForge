# Volatility-Adjusted RSI Thresholds for Reversal Confirmation

## Discovery Summary

C149 compares RSI and Stochastics under varying volatility, showing that fixed overbought/oversold thresholds from N165 are less reliable in high-volatility regimes. The comparison suggests that Stochastics may respond differently to the same price moves, making oscillator selection conditional on volatility. This cross-domain insight refines how to confirm reversal patterns: the better oscillator reading depends on whether volatility is expanding or contracting.

## Trading Implication

Traders should assess current volatility before using RSI overbought/oversold levels; in high volatility, either widen RSI thresholds or switch to Stochastics to avoid false reversal confirmations.
