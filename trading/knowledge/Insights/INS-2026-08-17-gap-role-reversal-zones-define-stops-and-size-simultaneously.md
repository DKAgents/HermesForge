# Gap + Role-Reversal Zones Define Stops and Size Simultaneously

## Discovery Summary

C065 establishes that violated support becomes resistance in a downtrend, giving a precise price zone for shorting bounces. EN069 adds a second technical layer: gaps in a bear move also define resistance zones (lower end of the gap), creating a convergence point where two independent resistance signals overlap. RG035 then closes the loop by requiring stops to be placed at valid technical levels — above resistance for shorts — and ties stop distance directly to position sizing via the 10% commitment / 5% risk rule. Together, the three notes form a sequence: identify the gap-resistance zone that coincides with the role-reversed support level, place the stop just above that confluence, and back-calculate position size from the resulting dollar risk.

## Trading Implication

When a downtrend bounce rallies into a zone where a prior violated support level AND a gap's lower end converge, sell there, place the protective stop just above that confluence, and size the position so the stop distance does not exceed 5% of total account equity per RG035's money management rule.
