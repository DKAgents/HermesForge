# Murphy's ideal pullback entry at 50% Fibonacci retracement

## Discovery Summary

C050 defines secondary trend retracement ranges; EN068-4060 specifies using 40-60% retracement zones for timing entries; N085 provides exact Fibonacci percentages of 38%, 50%, and 62%. Murphy's ideal entry point in uptrends is a pullback to the 50% Fibonacci retracement level, which lies within the 40-60% zone recommended by the rule, resolving any ambiguity between the three common Fib levels.

## Trading Implication

On pullbacks in uptrends, set limit orders specifically at the 50% Fibonacci retracement level rather than 38% or 62%, as it aligns with both the retracement zone rule and Murphy's ideal entry point.
