# Weekly Price Channel as Trend Filter for Daily Entries

## Discovery Summary

Murphy's principle (R142) advocates using weekly signals as trend filters for MACD and Stochastics crossovers. The weekly price channel (C130) is a simpler trend-following alternative that can serve the same filtering role. Combining this with long-term weekly moving averages (N044) provides multiple ways to define the weekly trend direction before taking daily signals.

## Trading Implication

A trader should use the weekly price channel breakout (high/low of last N weeks) to determine trend direction, then take only daily signals (e.g., MACD, Stochastics) that align with that weekly trend, similar to Murphy's advice but applied to a channel system.
