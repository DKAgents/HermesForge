# RSI extremes confirm reversal patterns

## Discovery Summary

C149 notes RSI vs Stochastics volatility comparison, suggesting RSI's overbought/oversold levels (N165) are more reliable in trending vs. ranging markets. Reversal patterns gain confirmation when RSI reaches extremes (above 70 or below 30) in sync with pattern completion, particularly in low-volatility conditions where RSI extremes are rarer and thus more significant.

## Trading Implication

When a reversal pattern forms, enter only if RSI is at an overbought (>70) or oversold (<30) extreme, as this reduces false signals from random noise.
