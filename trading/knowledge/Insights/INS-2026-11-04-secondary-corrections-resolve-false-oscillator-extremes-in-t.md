# Secondary Corrections Resolve False Oscillator Extremes in Trends

## Discovery Summary

EN041's rule to buy oversold in an uptrend conflicts with strong trends, where oscillators can stay overbought/oversold and produce false signals. C366 resolves this by explaining that counter-trend secondary trends lasting weeks to months create the corrections that produce genuine oversold/overbought entry points. The McClellan Oscillator (N186) can then be used as a breadth-based confirmation that those secondary extremeness levels are real rather than premature.

## Trading Implication

In a primary uptrend, wait for a secondary down correction to reach an oversold oscillator condition (confirmed by breadth, e.g., McClellan Oscillator) before buying; avoid shorting mere overbought readings in a strong uptrend.
