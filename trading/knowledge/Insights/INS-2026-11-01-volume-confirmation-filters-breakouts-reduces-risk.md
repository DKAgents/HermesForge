# Volume Confirmation Filters Breakouts, Reduces Risk

## Discovery Summary

EN008 requires a noticeable volume increase at pattern completion for reliability, while N013 warns that light-volume upside breakouts are often bull traps. Together they establish a consistent precondition: only act on breakouts accompanied by heavy volume, otherwise treat the signal as suspect and either stand aside or reduce exposure.

## Trading Implication

Before entering any breakout position, verify that volume expands significantly on the breakout bar; if volume is light, skip the trade or enter with a smaller size than you would on a confirmed high-volume breakout.
