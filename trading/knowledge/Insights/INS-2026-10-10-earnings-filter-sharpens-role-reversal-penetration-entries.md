# Earnings filter sharpens role-reversal penetration entries

## Discovery Summary

C065 establishes the role-reversal setup where previous support becomes resistance in a downtrend. E005 warns that defining a 'significant penetration' is subjective, which makes the entry criteria vulnerable to false or earnings-driven gaps. The seed refines this by adding a mandatory earnings-calendar binary filter: if earnings are within 7 days, the role-reversal entry is disqualified, preventing ambiguous penetrations from being misread as genuine support/resistance failures.

## Trading Implication

Before acting on a previous-support-as-resistance role-reversal setup, check the earnings calendar and stand aside if the stock reports within 7 days. Use this binary filter to keep penetration signals clean and avoid earnings-driven false breaks.
