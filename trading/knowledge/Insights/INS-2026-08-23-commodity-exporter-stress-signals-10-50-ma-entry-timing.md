# Commodity Exporter Stress Signals 10/50 MA Entry Timing

## Discovery Summary

E040 identifies commodity-exporting nations (Australia, Canada, Mexico, Russia) as especially vulnerable to commodity price plunges, making their equity markets leading candidates for short setups. EN028 and N039 provide the mechanical entry rule: a 10-day MA crossing below the 50-day MA on those equity indices or ETFs confirms the downtrend and triggers the sell signal. The intermarket chain (commodities → economic stress → equity weakness) provides the directional bias, while the 10/50 crossover provides the precise, rules-based entry timing — avoiding premature shorts before trend confirmation.

## Trading Implication

When commodity prices are in confirmed decline, monitor equity indices of commodity-exporting nations (e.g., EWA, EWC, EWW) for a 10-day MA crossing below the 50-day MA as the trigger to initiate or add to short positions, using the commodity trend as a macro filter to avoid false signals in non-exporter markets.
