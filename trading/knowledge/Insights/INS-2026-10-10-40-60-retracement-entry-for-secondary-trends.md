# 40-60% retracement entry for secondary trends

## Discovery Summary

C050-secondary-trend-retracement-range defines the typical retracement range of a secondary trend (often 1/3 to 2/3 of the primary move). EN068-4060-retracement-zones-for-timing-entries narrows this down to a 40-60% zone, which Murphy identifies as the ideal entry point for pullbacks in uptrends. Combining these, a trader can target entries within the 40-60% retracement of the prior primary move during a secondary trend pullback.

## Trading Implication

Wait for a pullback in an uptrend that retraces 40-60% of the prior impulse wave before entering long, using this zone as a precise entry trigger.
