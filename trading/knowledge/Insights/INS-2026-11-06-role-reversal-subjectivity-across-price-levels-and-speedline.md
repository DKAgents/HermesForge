# Role Reversal Subjectivity Across Price Levels and Speedlines

## Discovery Summary

The notes E005-subjectivity-in-defining-significant-penetration, C065-previous-support-as-future-resistance-in-downtrend, and R029-speed-resistance-lines-role-reversal all rely on the principle of role reversal after a break. E005 explicitly flags that determining a 'significant' penetration is subjective, a limitation that applies equally to horizontal support/resistance and speedlines. This connection confirms that any trader using role reversal rules must predefine an objective significance criterion to avoid ambiguous signals.

## Trading Implication

Before acting on role reversal (e.g., selling at broken support or trading speedline breaks), specify a concrete threshold for penetration significance—such as a percentage closing basis—to make the rule systematic and testable.
