# Light-Volume Flag Breakout: Tighten Stops, Expect Bull Trap

## Discovery Summary

N043 flag and pennant characteristics expect heavy volume on the flagpole and breakout, with a light-volume pause. R082 says a valid breakout must be on heavier volume. N013 warns that a light-volume upside breakout is a bull trap, and a subsequent heavy-volume decline confirms it. Thus a flag/pennant upside breakout on light volume is not a continuation signal; it should trigger stop tightening or exit, especially if heavy selling follows.

## Trading Implication

In an uptrend, treat a light-volume breakout above a flag/pennant as suspect rather than adding; tighten stops below the pattern or breakout level, and exit immediately if the subsequent decline is on heavy volume.
