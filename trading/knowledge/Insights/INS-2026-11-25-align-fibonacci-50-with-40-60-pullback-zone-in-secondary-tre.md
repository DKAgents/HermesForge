# Align Fibonacci 50% with 40-60% pullback zone in secondary trends

## Discovery Summary

C050's Dow theory establishes secondary corrections retrace one-third to two-thirds, with 50% most common. EN068-4060 operationalizes entries into a 40-60% retracement zone for precise timing. N085's Fibonacci levels (38%, 50%, 62%) add trend-strength context: strong trends bottom near 38%, weak trends extend to 62%. Together, the ideal entry point converges on the 50% Fibonacci retracement within the 40-60% zone, adjusted for trend strength.

## Trading Implication

Use the 50% Fibonacci as the core entry target, but shift the entry zone shallower (38-50%) in strong trends and deeper (50-62%) in weak trends, all within the 40-60% rule where applicable.
