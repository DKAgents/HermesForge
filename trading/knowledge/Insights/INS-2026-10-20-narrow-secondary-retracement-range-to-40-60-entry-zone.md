# Narrow secondary retracement range to 40-60% entry zone

## Discovery Summary

C050-secondary-trend-retracement-range identifies the broad secondary correction range, while EN068-4060-retracement-zones-for-timing-entries narrows that range to a specific 40-60% retracement zone for entries. This aligns with Murphy's ideal pullback entry near the 50% retracement level. The rule filters the broader concept into a tighter, timing-specific window.

## Trading Implication

Enter pullback longs only when price reaches the 40-60% retracement zone of the prior upswing; wait out shallower retracements and avoid entries beyond 60%. Use Fibonacci measurements to define that zone precisely.
