# Confluence of Dow, Fibonacci, and Murphy on 50% Retracement Entry

## Discovery Summary

C050-secondary-trend-retracement-range establishes Dow's 1/3–2/3 retracement norm with 50% as the most common. N085-fibonacci-percentage-retracements-38-50-62 independently highlights 38%, 50%, and 62% as key levels. EN068-4060-retracement-zones-for-timing-entries narrows the actionable window to 40–60%. The overlap of all three frameworks identifies the 50% retracement as the ideal pullback entry point in an ongoing trend.

## Trading Implication

In an established uptrend, wait for a pullback to 40–60% of the prior leg, with 50% as the sweet spot, before entering long; mirror this in downtrends for short entries. Use this confluence zone as a higher-probability trigger rather than entering on any pullback.
