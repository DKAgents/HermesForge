# Confluence of Broken Support and Gaps for Short Entries

## Discovery Summary

C065 states that in a downtrend, violated support levels become resistance on bounces, providing a short entry zone. EN069 adds that price gaps also act as support/resistance, with rules for entries and stop placement. Combined, a prior support level that has been broken and coincides with a price gap creates a strong resistance confluence, giving a precise short entry with a defined stop above the resistance/gap, which then dictates position size.

## Trading Implication

In a downtrend, short rallies into a former support level that was violated and also contains a gap, placing a protective stop above the confluence zone; use the stop distance to size the position appropriately.
