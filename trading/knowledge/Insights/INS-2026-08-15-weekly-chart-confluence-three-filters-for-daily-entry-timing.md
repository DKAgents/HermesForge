# Weekly Chart Confluence: Three Filters for Daily Entry Timing

## Discovery Summary

R142 establishes that MACD and Stochastics daily signals should only be acted upon when aligned with the weekly trend direction. N044 provides a concrete weekly trend definition: the 10/40-week MA relationship establishes bull/bear bias, with the 40-week MA as dynamic support. C130 adds a third weekly-timeframe tool — price channel breakouts — which Murphy calls superior to MAs for trend-following. Together, these three weekly-chart tools (MA crossover, price channel, MACD/Stochastic direction) can form a multi-confirmation filter before any daily signal is traded.

## Trading Implication

Before acting on a daily MACD or Stochastics crossover, require at least two weekly confirmations: price above the 40-week MA AND within an upward price channel breakout; only then take long daily signals, never counter-trend ones.
