# Volume Divergence in Flag/Pennant: Tighten Stops on Weak Breakout

## Discovery Summary

N043 flag-and-pennant-summary-characteristics describes how these patterns form with a volume contraction then expansion, while R082-breakouts-must-be-accompanied-by-heavy-volume requires volume to confirm breakouts. N013-volume-as-a-filter-for-false-breakouts shows that weak volume can identify false moves, so when price continues in a trend but volume diverges, the breakout is suspect and the initial stop should be adjusted. Specifically, if volume fails to confirm, stops should be tightened to the breakout level or pattern boundary instead of left at a wider trend-based stop.

## Trading Implication

After a flag/pennant breakout, monitor volume immediately; if price advances but volume declines, tighten stops to just below the breakout or pattern low to protect gains from a likely false breakout.
