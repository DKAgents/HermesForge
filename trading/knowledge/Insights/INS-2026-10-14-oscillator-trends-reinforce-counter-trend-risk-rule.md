# Oscillator Trends Reinforce Counter-Trend Risk Rule

## Discovery Summary

C366-secondary-trends defines the context of price moves within a larger trend, while EN041-oscillator-entry-strategy-in-trending-markets implies oscillator signals are only reliable when aligned with that trend. In strong trends, oscillators stay stretched, so counter-trend oscillator extremes are not reversal signals. This confirms risk rules against counter-trend trades: the oscillator edge is directional, not mean-reverting.

## Trading Implication

Traders should take oscillator entries only in the direction of the secondary trend and avoid counter-trend oscillator extremes as reversal triggers while the trend remains strong.
