# Dual RSI-Stochastics extreme confirms reversal patterns

## Discovery Summary

C149 states the best signals occur when RSI and stochastics are both in overbought or oversold territory simultaneously. R177 requires any oscillator to be in its overbought/oversold zone before a candle reversal pattern is considered valid. Combining these, the strongest confirmation for a reversal pattern emerges when both RSI (above 70/below 30, per N165) and stochastics are simultaneously in their extreme zones, acting as a dual filter that reduces false signals beyond using a single oscillator.

## Trading Implication

A trader should only accept a candle reversal pattern as valid when both RSI and stochastics are in overbought (for bearish reversals) or oversold (for bullish reversals) territory at the same time.
