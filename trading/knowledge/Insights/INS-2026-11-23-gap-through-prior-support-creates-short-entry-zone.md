# Gap Through Prior Support Creates Short Entry Zone

## Discovery Summary

C065 states that in a downtrend, violated support becomes resistance on bounces. EN069 adds that price gaps also act as support/resistance for timing entries. When a gap down breaches a prior support level, the gap zone and the old support converge into a single resistance area, providing a precise short entry on a rally into the gap. This combination gives a concrete rule: sell into the gap (old support) with a stop above the gap, aligning with Murphy's principles on both sides.

## Trading Implication

In a downtrend, if a price gap breaks through a prior support level, wait for a rally into the gap and sell short, placing a protective stop just above the upper end of the gap to manage risk and size the position accordingly.
