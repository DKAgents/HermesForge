# Secondary pullbacks with oscillators confirm counter-trend risk rule

## Discovery Summary

EN041 oscillator entry strategy in trending markets depends on identifying secondary trends (C366) to time pullback entries in the direction of the primary trend. In strong trends, oscillators reach extreme readings but do not signal reversal; the valid signal is an oscillator reset within a secondary correction. This reinforces the risk guideline against counter-trend trades: oscillator signals are only usable when they align with the primary trend, never against it.

## Trading Implication

Use oscillators to enter only on secondary-trend pullbacks aligned with the primary trend; disregard oscillator extremes that suggest counter-trend reversals.
