# Triple crossover weak in ranging markets

## Discovery Summary

The notes C128, R323, and N037 all describe the triple crossover method using moving averages (4-9-18 day), which is a trend-following system. In volatile or ranging markets, such crossover signals fail more often because price oscillates around the averages, generating false signals. This links to regime edge conditions: trend-following patterns underperform in non-trending regimes, suggesting a filter is needed.

## Trading Implication

A trader should avoid acting on triple crossover signals during confirmed ranging or high-volatility regimes, or use a trend filter (e.g., ADX > 25) to suppress false entries.
