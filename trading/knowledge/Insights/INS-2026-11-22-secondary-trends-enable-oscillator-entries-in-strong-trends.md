# Secondary Trends Enable Oscillator Entries in Strong Trends

## Discovery Summary

C366 secondary trends are counter-trend moves lasting weeks to months. EN041 says to buy oversold conditions in uptrends, but in strong trends oscillators rarely reach oversold except during these secondary corrections. Combining them shows that counter-trend secondary moves are the ideal entry points for pro-trend trades, resolving any conflict with risk rules against counter-trend trading. The same logic applies to breadth oscillators like N186-mcclellan-oscillator: wait for a secondary pullback before acting on oversold/overbought readings.

## Trading Implication

In a strong primary trend, do not act on oscillator signals unless they occur during a secondary correction; buy oversold readings in uptrend pullbacks and sell overbought readings in downtrend rallies, staying aligned with the primary trend.
