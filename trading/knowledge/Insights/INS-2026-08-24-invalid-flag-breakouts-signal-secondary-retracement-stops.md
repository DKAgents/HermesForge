# Invalid Flag Breakouts Signal Secondary Retracement Stops

## Discovery Summary

Flag and pennant patterns form at roughly the midpoint of a move and require heavy volume on breakout for validity (N043, R082). When a breakout from such a consolidation occurs without the required volume surge, the signal is questionable. In this scenario, the pause may instead represent a secondary trend correction, which typically retraces 50% of the prior move (C050). Thus, a low-volume breakout warns the trader that price might fail to continue and could fall back to the 50% retracement level of the flagpole.

## Trading Implication

If a flag or pennant breakout lacks heavy volume, do not treat it as a valid continuation; instead, tighten stops and anticipate a possible move to the 50% retracement level of the prior advance or decline, setting protective stops beyond that deeper retracement zone.
