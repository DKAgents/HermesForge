# Earnings filter reduces subjectivity in role-reversal penetration

## Discovery Summary

E005 highlights subjectivity in defining significant penetration of a level. C065 describes how previous support becomes resistance in a downtrend (role-reversal). Combining these, the subjectivity of penetration is especially problematic when earnings announcements cause noise; therefore, adding a mandatory pre-trade earnings calendar check (within 7 days) acts as a binary filter to eliminate false breakouts, making the role-reversal entry criteria more robust.

## Trading Implication

Before entering a role-reversal trade based on support/resistance flip, verify that no earnings report is scheduled within the next 7 days; if there is, skip the trade to avoid ambiguous penetration.
