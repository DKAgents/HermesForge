# Earnings Calendar Filter for Role-Reversal Entries

## Discovery Summary

Role-reversal entries from C065-previous-support-as-future-resistance-in-downtrend and R029-speed-resistance-lines-role-reversal depend on a 'significant' penetration, but E005-subjectivity-in-defining-significant-penetration admits that threshold is subjective. An earnings announcement within 7 days can produce a sharp, news-driven penetration of prior support/resistance or speedlines, creating a false role-reversal signal. Adding a mandatory pre-trade earnings check converts this subjective judgment into an objective binary filter: if earnings are within 7 days, the role-reversal entry is invalid.

## Trading Implication

Before entering any role-reversal trade based on broken support/resistance or speedlines, check the earnings calendar and stand aside if earnings are within 7 days. Re-evaluate only after the event passes and the penetration is confirmed without earnings distortion.
