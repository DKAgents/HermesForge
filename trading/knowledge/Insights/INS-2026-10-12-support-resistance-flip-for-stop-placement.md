# Support/Resistance Flip for Stop Placement

## Discovery Summary

C065 (previous support becomes resistance in downtrend) and C064 (previous peaks become support in uptrend) together define symmetrical levels for stop-loss placement. Violated support/resistance levels provide precise stop distances, which directly determine position size per standard risk management rules (e.g., 1% risk).

## Trading Implication

Place stop-loss orders just beyond the flipped level: above former support in downtrends, below former resistance in uptrends, then size positions based on the stop distance.
