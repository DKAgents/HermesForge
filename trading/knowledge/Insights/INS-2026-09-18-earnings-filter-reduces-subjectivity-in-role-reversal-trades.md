# Earnings filter reduces subjectivity in role-reversal trades

## Discovery Summary

The subjectivity in defining significant penetration (E005) undermines the reliability of role-reversal signals from previous support becoming resistance (C065) and speedlines (R029). Adding a mandatory pre-trade earnings calendar check (within 7 days) as a binary filter addresses this weakness by avoiding trades around earnings events, which can cause false breakouts or failures.

## Trading Implication

Before entering a role-reversal trade (e.g., support becoming resistance), verify the stock's next earnings date is at least 7 days away; if not, skip the trade.
