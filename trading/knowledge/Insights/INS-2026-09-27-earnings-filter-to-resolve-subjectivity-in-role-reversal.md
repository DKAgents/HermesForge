# Earnings filter to resolve subjectivity in role-reversal

## Discovery Summary

The subjectivity in defining significant penetration (E005) weakens the reliability of role-reversal patterns like previous support becoming resistance in a downtrend (C065) and speedline role-reversal (R029). The seed question proposes adding a pre-trade earnings calendar check as a binary filter—if earnings are within 7 days, the trade is skipped—thereby replacing subjective judgment with an objective fundamental condition to improve signal quality.

## Trading Implication

Before acting on a role-reversal signal (e.g., price approaching a former support now resistance), verify that the stock has no earnings announcement within the next 7 calendar days; if it does, avoid the trade to reduce false breaks.
