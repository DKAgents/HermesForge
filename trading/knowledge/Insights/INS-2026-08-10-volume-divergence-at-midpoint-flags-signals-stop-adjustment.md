# Volume Divergence at Midpoint Flags Signals Stop Adjustment

## Discovery Summary

N043 establishes that flags and pennants occur at approximately the midpoint of a move and require a volume burst at breakout to confirm trend resumption. R082 mandates heavy volume for any valid breakout. C050 identifies that secondary corrections retrace one-third to two-thirds of the prior move. When volume diverges from price during a flag/pennant consolidation — meaning volume fails to surge at the expected breakout — the trader can use C050's retracement range (33%-67% of the flagpole) to anticipate how far a failed breakout might retrace, allowing a stop to be placed just beyond the two-thirds retracement level of the flagpole rather than just below the consolidation low.

## Trading Implication

If volume fails to confirm a flag or pennant breakout per R082, tighten stops to no wider than the two-thirds retracement of the flagpole (per C050 secondary trend ranges), since absent volume confirmation the pattern is likely failing and a full secondary correction is probable.
