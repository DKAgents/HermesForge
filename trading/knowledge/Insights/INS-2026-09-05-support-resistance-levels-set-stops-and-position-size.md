# Support/Resistance Levels Set Stops and Position Size

## Discovery Summary

C065 establishes that violated support becomes resistance in a downtrend, providing a technical ceiling for short entries. EN069 adds that price gaps also serve as support/resistance zones for timing entries and stop placement. RG035 then mandates stops at valid technical levels and ties the stop distance to position size via a fixed percentage risk rule. Together, they form a complete sequence: identify the relevant resistance (prior support or gap), sell short against it, place a protective stop just above that level, and calculate shares based on the stop distance and the 5% account risk limit from RG035.

## Trading Implication

In a downtrend, short a rally to a prior support-turned-resistance or the lower edge of a bearish gap, place a stop above that level, and size the position so that the dollar risk equals 5% of account equity (e.g., $5,000 on a $100,000 account) using the distance to the stop.
