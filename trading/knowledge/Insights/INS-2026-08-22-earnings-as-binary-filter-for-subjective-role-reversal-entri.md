# Earnings as Binary Filter for Subjective Role-Reversal Entries

## Discovery Summary

E005 acknowledges that 'significant penetration' for role-reversal is inherently subjective (Murphy, p. 83), meaning entry signals from C065 (support-becomes-resistance in downtrends) and R029 (speedline role reversals) already carry discretionary risk. The seed question introduces an earnings calendar check as a mandatory binary filter: because role-reversal setups depend on repeated behavioral patterns (sellers re-entering at violated support), an imminent earnings event introduces a discontinuous price shock that can override those behavioral anchors entirely, rendering the subjectivity problem acute and the setup unreliable.

## Trading Implication

Before entering any role-reversal trade based on C065 or R029, a trader must check the earnings calendar; if earnings fall within 7 days, the setup should be skipped regardless of how 'significant' the penetration appears, converting a subjective judgment into a hard binary gate.
