# RSI + Stochastics Simultaneous Extremes Filter Reversal Patterns

## Discovery Summary

R177 states any oscillator can filter candle patterns and requires the oscillator to be in its presignal overbought/oversold area before a reversal pattern is valid. N165 defines RSI's extreme zones as above 70 and below 30, and notes that divergence from price action warns of reversal. C149 adds that RSI is less volatile than stochastics and that the strongest signals occur when both oscillators are simultaneously in overbought or oversold territory. Therefore, combining RSI and stochastics as a dual filter for candle reversal patterns is more selective than relying on one oscillator alone.

## Trading Implication

Before acting on a candle reversal pattern, require both RSI and stochastics to be in the same overbought/oversold zone at the same time. This filters out weaker reversal signals that only one oscillator confirms.
