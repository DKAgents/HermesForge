# Volume Filter Confirms Breakouts; Heavy Declines Expose Bull Traps

## Discovery Summary

R052-filters-for-confirming-breakouts lists price-based breakout filters plus volume as a reliability check. N013-volume-as-a-filter-for-false-breakouts shows a light-volume upside breakout is suspect, and a subsequent heavy-volume decline confirms it was likely a false breakout. N028-bull-trap-false-upside-breakout labels this exact sequence as a bull trap. Thus volume acts not just as a confirmation filter but as an exit/stop trigger once the breakout fails.

## Trading Implication

Do not enter long on a breakout unless volume expands; if already long and a light-volume breakout is followed by a heavy-volume decline, treat that decline as the stop/exit signal instead of waiting for price to fall back below the breakout level.
