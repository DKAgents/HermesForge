# Breadth Oscillator Confirms Trend Phase Before Counter-Trend Entry

## Discovery Summary

EN041 warns that oscillators can 'lie' in strong trends, rendering oversold/overbought signals premature or false during powerful primary moves. C366 defines secondary trends as counter-primary corrections lasting weeks to months — exactly the timeframe where oscillator entries are most dangerous. N186's McClellan Oscillator, being a breadth-based tool rather than a price-only oscillator, adds a market-wide confirmation layer: if breadth diverges from price during an alleged secondary correction, it suggests the primary trend remains intact and counter-trend oscillator entries should be avoided or sized down.

## Trading Implication

Before acting on an oversold/overbought oscillator signal (per EN041), check the McClellan Oscillator for breadth confirmation: only take counter-trend entries when breadth also shows exhaustion consistent with a secondary trend reversal, not merely a price dip within a strong primary trend.
