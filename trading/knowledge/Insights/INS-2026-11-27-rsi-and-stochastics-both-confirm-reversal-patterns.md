# RSI and Stochastics Both Confirm Reversal Patterns

## Discovery Summary

The 'Filtered Candle Patterns — Oscillator Alternatives' rule states that any oscillator must be in its presignal (overbought/oversold) area before a reversal candle pattern is valid. Meanwhile, the 'RSI vs. Stochastics Volatility Comparison' notes that RSI is less volatile and reaches extremes less frequently, and that the best signals occur when both oscillators are simultaneously in overbought or oversold territory. Combining these, a reversal pattern is most reliable when both RSI (per 'RSI Overbought/Oversold Levels' with 70/30 thresholds) and Stochastics are in extreme zones, because RSI's rarity in hitting extremes adds confirmation weight.

## Trading Implication

Before acting on a reversal candle pattern, require both RSI (above 70 or below 30) and Stochastics (in overbought/oversold) to be in their extreme zones simultaneously, increasing signal reliability.
