# Align Dow, Fibonacci, Murphy on 40-60% pullback entries

## Discovery Summary

The Dow note 'Secondary Trend Retracement Range' establishes corrections at one-third to two-thirds, centered on 50%. 'Fibonacci Percentage Retracements' anchors key levels at 38%, 50%, and 62%, with 50% the common center. Murphy's rule '40–60% Retracement Zones for Timing Entries' narrows these ranges into an actionable entry zone. Together they form a non-obvious filter: wait for an uptrend pullback to enter the 40–60% retracement band, using the 50% level as the reference entry point.

## Trading Implication

In an uptrend, place buy entries on pullbacks only after price retraces 40–60% of the prior advance, with 50% as the ideal reference point; use the 38% and 62% Fib levels as outer assessments of trend strength.
