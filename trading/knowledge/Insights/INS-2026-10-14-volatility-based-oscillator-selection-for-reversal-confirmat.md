# Volatility-Based Oscillator Selection for Reversal Confirmation

## Discovery Summary

C149 compares RSI and stochastics under different volatility regimes, while N165 defines RSI overbought/oversold levels. Together they suggest that RSI's fixed 70/30 thresholds become less reliable in highly volatile markets, where stochastics' responsiveness better confirms reversal patterns. Traders should filter oscillator choice by volatility regime instead of applying RSI extremes uniformly.

## Trading Implication

Use stochastics overbought/oversold readings to confirm reversals when volatility is high; rely on RSI extremes when volatility is low, reducing false signals.
