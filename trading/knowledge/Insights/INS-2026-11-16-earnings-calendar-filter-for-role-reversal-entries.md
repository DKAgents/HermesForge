# Earnings Calendar Filter for Role-Reversal Entries

## Discovery Summary

E005-subjectivity-in-defining-significant-penetration acknowledges that judging whether a support/resistance penetration is meaningful is subjective. C065-previous-support-as-future-resistance-in-downtrend and R029-speed-resistance-lines-role-reversal both rely on that penetration judgment for role-reversal entries. Adding a mandatory earnings-calendar check as a binary filter directly narrows this subjectivity by excluding setups where scheduled event risk could distort price action and invalidate the technical role-reversal signal.

## Trading Implication

Before entering any role-reversal trade based on C065 or R029, check the earnings calendar; if earnings are within 7 days, stand aside until after the event and the penetration/retest is confirmed.
