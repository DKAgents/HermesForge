# Earnings Filter for Role-Reversal Entries

## Discovery Summary

Role-reversal entries rely on identifying a significant penetration, which E005 notes is inherently subjective, whether applied to previous support becoming resistance (C065) or broken speedlines reversing roles (R029). Adding a mandatory pre-trade earnings-calendar check creates an objective binary filter: if earnings are within seven days, the role-reversal setup is disqualified. This prevents earnings-driven price moves from being mistaken for genuine support/resistance or speedline role-reversal signals.

## Trading Implication

Before entering any role-reversal trade, check the earnings calendar and skip the trade if earnings fall within seven days. This avoids false role-reversal signals during high-impact event windows where penetration may not reflect normal supply/demand dynamics.
