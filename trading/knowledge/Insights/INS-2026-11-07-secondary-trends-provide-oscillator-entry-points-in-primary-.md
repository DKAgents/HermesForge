# Secondary Trends Provide Oscillator Entry Points in Primary Trend

## Discovery Summary

EN041 'Oscillator Entry Strategy in Trending Markets' instructs buying oversold conditions in an uptrend, but strong trends often prevent oscillators from reaching oversold extremes. C366 'Secondary Trends' identifies the counter-trend pullbacks that create these oversold opportunities within the primary trend. Adding N186 'McClellan Oscillator' as a breadth-based confirmation during these secondary corrections gives a concrete condition: only enter when the oscillator is oversold AND the price move is a secondary pullback within a confirmed primary uptrend. This transforms a generic rule into a timing strategy that respects the primary trend while exploiting the secondary trend's pullback.

## Trading Implication

Traders should combine trend analysis (identify primary uptrend/downtrend) with secondary trend identification, then wait for the McClellan Oscillator to reach oversold/overbought extremes during secondary pullbacks before entering in the direction of the primary trend. Avoid counter-trend trades based solely on oscillator extremes without confirming the secondary trend context.
