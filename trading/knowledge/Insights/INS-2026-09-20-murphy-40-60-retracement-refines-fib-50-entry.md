# Murphy 40-60% Retracement Refines Fib 50% Entry

## Discovery Summary

Murphy's rule (EN068-4060) states that 40-60% retracements of the prior advance in an uptrend provide excellent long entries. This directly complements the 50% retracement level from Fibonacci (N085), which is also the most common secondary retracement per Dow (C050). The connection adds a precise range (40-60%) that bridges the gap between the broad one-third to two-thirds Dow guideline and the discrete Fibonacci levels, making the 40-60% zone the actionable sweet spot for pullback entries.

## Trading Implication

In an uptrend, wait for a pullback that retraces between 40% and 60% of the prior advance before entering long, using the 50% retracement as the focal point. Avoid entering on shallower (<40%) or deeper (>60%) pullbacks unless other confirmation is present.
