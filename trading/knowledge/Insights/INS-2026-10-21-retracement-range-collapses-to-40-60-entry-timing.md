# Retracement range collapses to 40–60% entry timing

## Discovery Summary

C050 defines the secondary trend retracement range, the expected pullback depth within an ongoing trend. EN068 operationalizes that concept into a precise timing rule by limiting entry zones to 40–60% retracement levels. The concept gives the theoretical boundary; the rule converts it into an actionable filter for when to enter pullbacks.

## Trading Implication

Wait for a pullback to reach the 40–60% retracement of the prior move in a secondary trend before entering, rather than buying early dips or chasing entries beyond that zone.
