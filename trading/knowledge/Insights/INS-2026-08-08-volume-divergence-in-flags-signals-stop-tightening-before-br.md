# Volume Divergence in Flags Signals Stop Tightening Before Breakout

## Discovery Summary

N043 specifies that flags and pennants form on 'very light volume' during the pause phase, while R082 mandates that valid breakouts require a volume surge. C050 establishes that these consolidation patterns occur near the midpoint of the move (roughly 50% retracement zone per Dow theory). When volume during the flag/pennant consolidation fails to contract as expected — i.e., volume diverges upward from price during the pause — this contradicts the canonical pattern signature, signaling the formation may not be a true continuation pattern and that the anticipated breakout volume surge may be unreliable.

## Trading Implication

If volume does not contract meaningfully during a flag/pennant consolidation, a trader should tighten stops toward the midpoint of the flagpole rather than waiting for a breakout, since elevated volume during consolidation indicates the pattern lacks the structural validity required by R082 for a reliable breakout signal.
