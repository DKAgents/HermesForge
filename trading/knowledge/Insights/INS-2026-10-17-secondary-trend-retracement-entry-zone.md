# Secondary Trend Retracement Entry Zone

## Discovery Summary

C050 defines secondary trend retracements as typically 33-66% of the prior move. EN068 specifies that the 40-60% retracement zone is optimal for timing entries. This narrows the theoretical range into a concrete entry filter, aligning with Murphy's ideal 50% retracement entry point.

## Trading Implication

In an uptrend, wait for a pullback to reach between 40% and 60% of the prior advance before entering a long position, avoiding earlier or deeper retracements.
