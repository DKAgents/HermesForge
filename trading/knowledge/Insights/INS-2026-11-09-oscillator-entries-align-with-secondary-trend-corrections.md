# Oscillator Entries Align with Secondary Trend Corrections

## Discovery Summary

C366 secondary trends are the counter-trend corrections within a primary trend, which create the oversold/overbought conditions used by EN041's oscillator entry strategy. However, in strong trends oscillators can remain extreme for extended periods, leading to premature signals. N186's McClellan Oscillator, a breadth oscillator, can confirm whether the overbought/oversold condition is broad-based or just a shallow move. Thus, the non-obvious connection is that traders should wait for a secondary trend correction (i.e., a meaningful pullback) before acting on oscillator extremes, ensuring the entry aligns with the primary trend and respects the risk rule against counter-trend trades.

## Trading Implication

In a strong uptrend, do not buy on the first oversold oscillator reading; instead, wait for a confirmed secondary trend pullback (e.g., a 3-week to several-month correction) before entering long, and use the McClellan Oscillator to confirm breadth coincides with the extreme.
