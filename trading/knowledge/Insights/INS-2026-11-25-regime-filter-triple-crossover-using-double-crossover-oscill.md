# Regime-filter triple crossover using double-crossover oscillator

## Discovery Summary

The triple crossover rule (R323/N037) generates signals purely from moving-average alignments, making it a trend-following pattern. C128 shows the same moving-average difference can be read as an oscillator, giving a direct read on whether the market is trending or ranging. In volatile/ranging regimes, triple crossover signals whipsaw, so the oscillator interpretation can serve as a regime filter rather than a separate signal generator.

## Trading Implication

When the double-crossover oscillator is flat or oscillating around zero, avoid taking 4-9-18 triple crossover buy/sell signals. Only take the triple crossover after the oscillator confirms directional expansion, indicating a trending regime.
