# Earnings filter for role-reversal entries reduces subjectivity

## Discovery Summary

The subjectivity in defining significant penetration (E005) becomes critical when using previous support as future resistance in a downtrend (C065). Adding a mandatory pre-trade earnings calendar check (within 7 days) as a binary filter to role-reversal entry criteria directly addresses the ambiguity of penetration levels during high-impact events, making the entry rule less subjective and more robust.

## Trading Implication

Traders must skip role-reversal entries if earnings are due within 7 days, reducing false breakouts and whipsaws around fundamental events.
