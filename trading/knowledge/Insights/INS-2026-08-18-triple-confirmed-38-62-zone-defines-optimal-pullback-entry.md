# Triple-Confirmed 38–62% Zone Defines Optimal Pullback Entry

## Discovery Summary

Three independent frameworks converge on the same retracement band: Dow Theory (C050) defines secondary corrections as retracing one-third to two-thirds (~33–66%) of prior moves; Murphy's entry rule (EN068) narrows the actionable zone to 40–60% for timing longs in uptrends; Fibonacci levels (N085) place the 38%, 50%, and 62% ratios within that same band and further qualify it — 38% signals a strong trend, 62% a weaker one. The overlap of all three at 38–62% is not coincidental; they are mutually reinforcing.

## Trading Implication

In an uptrend, wait for a pullback to reach at least 38% of the prior advance before entering long; treat the 50% level as the primary target entry and the 62% level as the final acceptable entry point — a retrace beyond 66% per Dow Theory signals the secondary trend may be invalidating the primary trend, making it a hard stop on adding longs.
