# Volume Divergence at Midpoint Flags Signals Stop Adjustment

## Discovery Summary

Flags and pennants (N043) occur at approximately the midpoint of a market move with characteristically light volume during consolidation followed by a volume surge at breakout. R082 requires that breakout volume be heavy to validate the signal. When volume fails to surge at the flag/pennant breakout, the pattern is unconfirmed — and since these patterns mark roughly the midpoint, the expected remaining move is only half the flagpole length, making a tighter stop essential to preserve a favorable risk/reward. C050's 1/3 to 2/3 retracement range further defines where a failed continuation would retrace, giving a concrete stop placement zone.

## Trading Implication

If a flag or pennant breakout occurs WITHOUT the required volume surge (R082), tighten the stop to within the 1/3 retracement of the flagpole rather than the full 2/3, since low-volume breakouts frequently fail and retrace deeply before any resumption occurs.
