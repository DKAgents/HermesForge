# Use 4-18 MA Oscillator to Filter 4-9-18 Triple Crossovers

## Discovery Summary

C128 explains that comparing two moving averages creates an oscillator, as in MACD. R323 and N037 define the 4-9-18 triple crossover system. Applying C128's logic to N037's parameters, the 4-period vs 18-period moving-average spread becomes a regime oscillator: when this spread is flat or oscillating, the market is ranging and triple-crossover signals are prone to whipsaw; when it expands in the signal direction, the crossover is more reliable.

## Trading Implication

Before acting on a 4-9-18 triple crossover signal, confirm the 4-18 moving-average spread is expanding in the same direction. Skip signals when the spread is flat or choppy, as those are lower-quality, range-bound conditions.
