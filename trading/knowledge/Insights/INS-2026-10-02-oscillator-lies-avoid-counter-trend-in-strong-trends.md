# Oscillator lies: avoid counter-trend in strong trends

## Discovery Summary

EN041's oscillator entry strategy is designed for trending markets, but in strong trends, oscillators often generate false overbought/oversold signals (RG017). This confirms that traders should not use oscillator signals for counter-trend entries when the primary trend is strong, directly reinforcing RG017's risk guideline. C366-secondary-trends may describe corrective moves, but the core insight is to avoid contradictory trades by aligning oscillator entries with the main trend.

## Trading Implication

Only take oscillator-based entries in the direction of the primary trend; ignore overbought/oversold readings as reversal signals during strong trends.
