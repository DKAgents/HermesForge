# Murphy S/R Levels Set Short Stops and Position Size

## Discovery Summary

C065 establishes that bullish support in a downtrend flips to resistance on bounces (Murphy p.83), and EN069 establishes that price gaps also act as S/R for timing entries. In a downtrend, both old support and bearish gaps are therefore objective resistance zones for short entries. The stop belongs beyond those zones, and this stop distance is the basis for position sizing under a fixed-risk rule.

## Trading Implication

When shorting a downtrend bounce, use rallies into former support or gap resistance as entries, place the protective stop just above the zone, and size the position based on that stop distance. This turns Murphy's S/R concepts into a concrete risk-managed short setup.
