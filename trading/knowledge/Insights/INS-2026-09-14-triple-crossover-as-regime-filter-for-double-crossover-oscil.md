# Triple Crossover as Regime Filter for Double Crossover Oscillator

## Discovery Summary

The double crossover oscillator (C128) generates signals from MA differences, but in ranging/volatile markets these signals often whipsaw. The triple crossover method (R323) with 4-9-18 day MAs (N037) requires alignment of three averages, which can act as a confirming regime filter: only trade the double crossover signal when the triple crossover also points in the same direction, reducing false signals in choppy conditions.

## Trading Implication

In ranging or volatile markets, filter MACD or double crossover signals by requiring the 4-9-18 day triple crossover to confirm the same direction before entering; stand aside when the three averages are tangled.
