# Ideal Pullback Entry: 40-60% Zone with Trend Strength

## Discovery Summary

C050 establishes that secondary trends retrace one-third to two-thirds, with 50% most common. EN068-4060 states 40–60% retracements provide excellent entries, and N085-Fibonacci refines this by noting strong trends retrace ~38% and weak trends up to 62%. Together, they specify that the 40–60% zone (centered on 50%) is the primary ideal entry, but trend strength adjusts the optimal trigger: earlier at 38% in strong trends, later at 62% in weak trends.

## Trading Implication

In an uptrend, place buy limits at the 50% Fibonacci retracement, but adjust to 38% if the trend is strong (e.g., steep advance) and to 62% if the trend is weak (e.g., shallow advance). This makes pullback entries conditional on trend momentum.
