# Volatility Regime Dictates RSI vs Stochastics for Reversals

## Discovery Summary

C149 compares RSI and Stochastics across volatility conditions, while N165 defines RSI overbought/oversold levels. The non-obvious link is that RSI extremes are not standalone reversal confirmations; their reliability depends on volatility. In high-volatility/trending conditions RSI can remain overbought/oversold without a reversal, so a volatility filter or Stochastics comparison should be applied before treating the indicator as confirming a reversal pattern.

## Trading Implication

Before acting on a reversal pattern, check the volatility regime and only accept an RSI overbought/oversold reading as confirmation in normal or low-volatility conditions; in elevated volatility, use Stochastics or wait for RSI to exit its extreme before entering.
