# Volume confirms bull trap false breakout

## Discovery Summary

N013 states valid upside breakouts need heavy volume, while false breakouts occur on light volume. N028 adds that a light-volume breakout followed by heavy-volume decline is a negative chart combination identifying a bull trap. R052 provides additional confirmation filters (close beyond resistance, percentage penetration) but the volume sequence offers a clear sequential trigger: first confirm breakout volume, then watch for reversal volume.

## Trading Implication

After a light-volume breakout, wait for a heavy-volume decline to confirm a bull trap; then consider a short position or tighten stops below the breakout level.
