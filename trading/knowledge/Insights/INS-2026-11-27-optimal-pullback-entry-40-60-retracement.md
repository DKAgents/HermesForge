# Optimal pullback entry: 40-60% retracement

## Discovery Summary

Dow theory (C050) defines secondary trend retracements as one-third to two-thirds of the prior move, while Murphy's rule (EN068) specifies 40-60% as the ideal entry zone. Fibonacci retracements (N085) provide the same range via 38% and 62% levels, with 50% as a common midpoint. The convergence of these three independent sources validates the 40-60% (or 38-62%) zone as a high-probability entry area for pullbacks in the direction of the primary trend.

## Trading Implication

In an uptrend, wait for pullbacks to retrace 40-60% of the prior advance (ideally near the 50% Fibonacci level) before entering long positions; in a downtrend, use the same zone for short entries. Avoid entries at shallower or deeper retracements unless another signal overrides.
