# 50% Retracement Is the Ideal Pullback Entry Zone

## Discovery Summary

EN068-4060's 40–60% retracement zone matches the overlapping sweet spot of Dow's one-third to two-thirds secondary retracement range (C050) and Fibonacci's 38–62% boundaries (N085). The apparent conflict between these ranges is resolved by recognizing 50% as the central, most common retracement level — Murphy's ideal entry point for pullbacks in an uptrend.

## Trading Implication

Enter long on uptrend pullbacks only when price retraces 40–60% of the prior move, prioritizing 50%; avoid entries below 38% in strong trends and treat a retrace beyond 62% as a sign of weakness rather than an entry opportunity.
