# Oscillator Entries Align with Secondary Trends

## Discovery Summary

The oscillator entry strategy (EN041) advises buying oversold conditions in an uptrend, but oscillators often lie in strong trends by remaining overbought/oversold. The resolution is that these oversold/overbought readings typically occur during secondary trends (C366) – corrections or rallies within the primary trend – not as primary trend reversals. Thus, the entry rule is effectively a secondary-trend entry, aligning with the primary trend. The McClellan oscillator (N186) can provide breadth confirmation of these secondary conditions, adding robustness to the signal.

## Trading Implication

Only act on oscillator oversold signals when they appear during a secondary correction in a primary uptrend (and overbought signals during secondary rallies in a downtrend). Use the McClellan oscillator to verify that breadth supports the secondary trend before entering.
