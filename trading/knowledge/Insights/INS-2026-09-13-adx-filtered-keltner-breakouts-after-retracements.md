# ADX-Filtered Keltner Breakouts After Retracements

## Discovery Summary

E036 recommends using moving-average-based indicators when ADX is rising and oscillators when it is falling. Keltner Channels (N190) are an MA-based tool, so their breakout signals become reliable only during trending regimes. C050's Dow Theory retracement range provides a location within a primary trend where secondary corrections end; a trader can sequence these concepts by waiting for a retracement to the 1/3–2/3 zone, confirming trend resumption via a rising ADX, then acting on a Keltner Channel breakout.

## Trading Implication

After a secondary retracement reaches 50% of the prior move, wait for ADX to tick up, then take the next Keltner Channel breakout in the primary trend direction; avoid Keltner signals when ADX is declining.
