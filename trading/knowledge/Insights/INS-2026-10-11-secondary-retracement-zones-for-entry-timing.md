# Secondary Retracement Zones for Entry Timing

## Discovery Summary

The concept C050 defines the secondary trend retracement range, which typically spans 33% to 66% of the primary move. Rule EN068 narrows this to a 40-60% retracement zone for timing entries, providing a specific actionable range to enter pullbacks in uptrends.

## Trading Implication

Traders should wait for pullbacks to reach the 40-60% retracement of the prior primary move before initiating long positions in an uptrend, as this zone offers a higher probability entry point.
