# Volume Divergence Triggers Midpoint Retracement Stop Adjustment

## Discovery Summary

Flags and pennants (N043) are expected to form at approximately the midpoint of a move, with the consolidation phase showing very light volume before a burst at breakout. R082 establishes that a breakout without heavy volume is invalid. C050 identifies that secondary corrections retrace 1/3 to 2/3 of the prior move. When volume diverges — i.e., a breakout from a flag/pennant occurs without the required volume surge — the breakout signal is invalid per R082, and the trader should anticipate a deeper retracement toward the 1/3–2/3 range of the flagpole (C050), rather than assuming the trend continues from the midpoint.

## Trading Implication

If a flag or pennant breaks out on weak volume, do not trail stops at the pattern boundary; instead, widen stops or exit, anticipating a secondary retracement of 33–67% of the prior flagpole move before trend resumption.
