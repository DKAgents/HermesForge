# Volume confirmation reduces false breakout risk

## Discovery Summary

EN008 and N013 both emphasize that valid reversals require increased volume, while C324 defines confirmation as multiple factors agreeing. Together, they establish that volume confirmation acts as a filter to avoid false breakouts, reducing risk before entry.

## Trading Implication

Traders should only enter on pattern breakouts accompanied by above-average volume, and avoid entries on low-volume breakouts to reduce false signals.
