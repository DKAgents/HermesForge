# Regime filter: oscillators for ranges, triple crossover for trends

## Discovery Summary

N037 and R323 describe the 4-9-18-day triple-crossover method as a trend-following signal, while C128 explains how moving averages can be interpreted as oscillators via double crossover. In volatile or ranging markets, the seed question notes that crossover patterns fail more often; therefore the double-crossover oscillator interpretation of the same MAs provides a regime-based filter for when not to use the triple-crossover trend signal. This connects indicator construction to an edge condition about market regime.

## Trading Implication

Traders should apply the 4-9-18 triple-crossover only when a trend regime is confirmed, and in ranging/volatile conditions switch to a double-crossover oscillator interpretation of the same moving averages or stand aside to avoid whipsaw losses.
