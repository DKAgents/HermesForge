# Volume Divergence Trades: Tighten Stops in Flag/Pennant Breakouts

## Discovery Summary

N043 observes that flag and pennant patterns typically show volume contraction during consolidation, while R082 mandates heavy volume on any valid breakout. N013 further confirms that volume filters false breakouts. When price trends but volume diverges (e.g., rising price, falling volume), the eventual breakout lacks the required heavy volume, signaling a higher risk of false breakout. Therefore, stops should be adjusted tighter—placed just inside the pattern boundary or at the recent swing point—rather than relying on normal breakout follow-through.

## Trading Implication

In a trend continuation pattern like a flag or pennant, if volume diverges from price, tighten stops to just beyond the pattern's edge or recent swing high/low, and require heavy volume on the breakout before adding risk.
