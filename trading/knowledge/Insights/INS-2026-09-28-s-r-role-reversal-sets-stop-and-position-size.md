# S/R role reversal sets stop and position size

## Discovery Summary

C065 (previous-support-as-future-resistance-in-downtrend) and R312 (reversal-of-roles-supportresistance) both describe the principle that broken support becomes resistance. This confirms the Murphy rule that stop distance (to the now-resistance level) directly determines position size, linking S/R identification to concrete risk management sizing.

## Trading Implication

When a support level is broken in a downtrend, use that level as the stop for short entries; compute position size as the maximum allowed risk divided by the distance to that former support.
