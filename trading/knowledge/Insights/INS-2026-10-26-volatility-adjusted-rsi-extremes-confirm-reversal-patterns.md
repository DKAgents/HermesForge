# Volatility-adjusted RSI extremes confirm reversal patterns

## Discovery Summary

C149-rsi-vs-stochastics-volatility-comparison indicates that RSI and stochastics respond differently to volatility, so a single RSI threshold is not universally reliable. N165-relative-strength-index-rsi-overboughtoversold-levels supplies the standard RSI 70/30 overbought/oversold readings. Together they imply that reversal patterns should only be confirmed by RSI extremes that are consistent with the prevailing volatility regime, otherwise the pattern signal is weak.

## Trading Implication

Before trusting a reversal pattern, compare RSI extreme readings to current volatility conditions; require more extreme RSI values in high-volatility regimes and standard 70/30 levels only in quieter markets.
