# Weekly Price Channel as Filter for Daily MACD/Stoch

## Discovery Summary

R142 states John Murphy's principle that weekly signals should filter daily MACD and Stochastics crossovers to avoid trading against the prevailing trend. C130 introduces the weekly price channel (weekly rule) as a simple, well-researched trend-following alternative to moving averages. Combining these, the weekly price channel can serve as the objective trend filter: a daily MACD/Stoch crossover is only valid in the direction of the weekly channel breakout or its current position.

## Trading Implication

Define the weekly trend by the weekly price channel (e.g., 20-week high/low) and only take daily MACD/Stoch crossover signals that align with that weekly channel direction, otherwise stand aside.
