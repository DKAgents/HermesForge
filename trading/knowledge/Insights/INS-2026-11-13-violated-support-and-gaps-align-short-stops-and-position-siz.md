# Violated Support and Gaps Align Short Stops and Position Size

## Discovery Summary

C065 shows that in a downtrend, violated support becomes overhead resistance, while EN069 shows that price gaps also act as resistance and gives explicit stop placement for short entries. Together, a trader can wait for a bounce that reaches both prior support-turned-resistance and a gap's lower edge, then place the protective stop above the combined resistance zone. Following the seed insight, the distance from entry to that stop sets the risk per share, which then determines position size.

## Trading Implication

In a downtrend, sell rallies that enter a confluence of prior support-turned-resistance and a downside gap's lower edge, with a stop above the cluster; size the position from that stop distance to keep per-trade risk constant.
