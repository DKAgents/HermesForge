# Broken support gaps as short-entry resistance

## Discovery Summary

C065 (Previous Support as Future Resistance in Downtrend) explains that violated support becomes resistance, and EN069 (Price Gaps as Support and Resistance for Timing) states gaps act as S/R levels with stops beyond them. Combining these, a gap that previously acted as support and is then broken in a downtrend becomes a specific resistance zone to sell into, with a protective stop above the gap. The stop distance from entry determines position size per standard risk guidelines.

## Trading Implication

In a downtrend, identify prior support gaps that have been broken; short rallies into the lower end of those gaps with a stop above the gap, and size the position based on that stop distance.
