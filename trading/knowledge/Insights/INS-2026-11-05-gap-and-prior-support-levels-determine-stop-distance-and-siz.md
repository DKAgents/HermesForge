# Gap and Prior Support Levels Determine Stop Distance and Size

## Discovery Summary

C065 shows violated support becomes resistance in downtrends, while EN069 identifies gaps as support/resistance for entries. Both provide concrete levels for placing protective stops, such as below gap support or above prior support-turned-resistance. The distance from entry to these stops determines position size, linking Murphy's S/R rules to risk management.

## Trading Implication

When entering on a bounce into former support-turned-resistance or a gap, place stops just beyond the level and size positions based on that stop distance to maintain consistent risk.
