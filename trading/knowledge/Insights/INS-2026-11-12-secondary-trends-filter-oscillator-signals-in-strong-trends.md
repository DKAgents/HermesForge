# Secondary Trends Filter Oscillator Signals in Strong Trends

## Discovery Summary

EN041's rule to buy oversold in an uptrend is effectively a trade against the secondary trend that creates the pullback, as described in C366. In strong primary trends, the McClellan oscillator can remain overbought/oversold and 'lie,' so an oscillator extreme alone is insufficient. The secondary-trend context — a counter-move lasting weeks to months — is the condition that separates a normal correction from a possible primary reversal.

## Trading Implication

Before acting on an overbought/oversold McClellan reading, confirm the move is a secondary trend within an intact primary trend; enter in the primary trend's direction and treat the trade as counter-secondary-trend risk.
