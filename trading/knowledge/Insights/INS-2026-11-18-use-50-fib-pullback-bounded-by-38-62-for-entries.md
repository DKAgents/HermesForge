# Use 50% Fib pullback, bounded by 38-62%, for entries

## Discovery Summary

The 'Secondary Trend Retracement Range' note establishes that Dow corrections typically retrace one-third to two-thirds, with 50% the most common value. Murphy's '40–60% Retracement Zones for Timing Entries' turns this into an entry trigger for longs on pullbacks in uptrends. The Fibonacci note sharpens this by adding trend-strength limits: strong trends may only retrace to 38%, while 62% is the maximum retracement in weaker trends. Combining them makes the 50% retracement the central ideal entry, with 38% an early-entry zone in strong trends and 62% an invalidation/weak-trend boundary.

## Trading Implication

For uptrend pullbacks, draw Fibonacci retracement levels and buy in the 40–60% zone (50% ideal); in strong trends, enter earlier at 38–50%, and abandon the pullback-long setup if price exceeds the 62% retracement. Mirror the same levels for short entries in downtrend bounces.
