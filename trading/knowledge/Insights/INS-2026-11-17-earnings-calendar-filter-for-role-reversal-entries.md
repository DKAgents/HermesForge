# Earnings Calendar Filter for Role-Reversal Entries

## Discovery Summary

Role-reversal entries rely on identifying significant support/resistance breaks, as noted in E005-subjectivity-in-defining-significant-penetration, and on prior support becoming resistance in downtrends (C065) or speedlines reversing roles after breaks (R029). An earnings announcement within 7 days can cause high-volatility price moves that produce false penetrations and premature role-reversal signals. Adding a mandatory earnings calendar check as a binary filter prevents taking role-reversal trades contaminated by scheduled event risk. This directly refines the subjective penetration rule with an objective pre-trade condition.

## Trading Implication

Before acting on any role-reversal entry, check the earnings calendar; if the stock has earnings within 7 days, do not take the trade. This filters out event-driven false breakouts and aligns technical entries with risk guidelines.
