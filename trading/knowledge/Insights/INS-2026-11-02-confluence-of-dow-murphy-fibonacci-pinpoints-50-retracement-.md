# Confluence of Dow, Murphy, Fibonacci pinpoints 50% retracement entry

## Discovery Summary

C050-secondary-trend-retracement-range sets normal corrections at one-third to two-thirds of the prior move, with 50% as the most common retracement. EN068-4060-retracement-zones-for-timing-entries turns this into a specific entry rule: buy 40–60% pullbacks in uptrends and sell 40–60% bounces in downtrends. N085-fibonacci-percentage-retracements-38-50-62 adds the corresponding Fibonacci levels of 38%, 50%, and 62%, with 50% being the famous midpoint. The cross-domain convergence creates a precise filter: the ideal pullback entry zone is 40–60%, centered on 50%, with 62% as the maximum acceptable retracement.

## Trading Implication

In an established trend, wait for a retracement to reach approximately 50% of the prior impulse leg (within the 40–60% band) before entering in the direction of the primary trend. Avoid entries on shallow retracements below 38% and treat a retracement beyond 62% as a sign of trend weakness rather than an entry opportunity.
