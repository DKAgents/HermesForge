# Fibonacci retracement zones for pullback entries

## Discovery Summary

C050-secondary-trend-retracement-range and EN068-4060-retracement-zones-for-timing-entries both emphasize that retracements within a secondary trend should be contained to specific ranges (Murphy's ideal 40-60% pullback). N085-fibonacci-percentage-retracements-38-50-62 provides the common Fibonacci levels (38.2%, 50%, 61.8%) that align with these zones, with 50% being the precise midpoint. Together, they confirm that a pullback to the 50% Fibonacci retracement within a secondary uptrend is the ideal entry point, as it respects both retracement zone limits and the Fibonacci level.

## Trading Implication

When entering on pullbacks in an uptrend, wait for price to reach the 50% Fibonacci retracement level and only enter if the retracement does not exceed the 62% level, as deeper retracements may signal trend weakness.
