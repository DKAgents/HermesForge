# Inflation-Adjusted Crossover for Commodity Deflation

## Discovery Summary

The double crossover indicator (N039) applied to commodity prices becomes more reliable for position trading when combined with inflation-adjusted charts (R310) and the deflation risk edge condition (E040). For commodity exporters, a downside crossover on inflation-adjusted prices confirms deflation risk, which precedes bond and stock weakness per Murphy's intermarket chain. This creates a specific entry signal for short positions or defensive rotations.

## Trading Implication

Apply the 10/50-day moving average crossover to an inflation-adjusted commodity index (e.g., CRB adjusted for CPI). When a bearish crossover occurs, initiate shorts on commodity exporter stocks or rotate into bonds, expecting the intermarket sequence to follow.
