# Earnings Filter for Role-Reversal Entry Signals

## Discovery Summary

Role-reversal entries are already subjective (E005) because 'significant penetration' lacks a precise benchmark, and they rely on prior levels or broken speedlines (C065, R029). An earnings release within 7 days can produce sharp price moves that masquerade as penetrations or role-reversal bounces, but they are not driven by the technical logic of support/resistance flipping. The seed therefore adds a mandatory pre-trade earnings-calendar filter to the role-reversal entry criteria, making the setup conditional on a clean event window.

## Trading Implication

Before entering any role-reversal trade from violated support or a broken speedline, check the earnings calendar. If earnings are within 7 days, stand aside and re-evaluate only after the release.
