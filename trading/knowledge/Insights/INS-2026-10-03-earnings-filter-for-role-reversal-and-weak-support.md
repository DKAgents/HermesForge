# Earnings filter for role-reversal and weak support

## Discovery Summary

The seed question identifies a mandatory binary filter from earnings calendar checks (within 7 days) for role-reversal entry criteria. C065 notes that previous support becomes resistance in a downtrend, often tested with indecision; N007's weekly reversal pattern could form at such levels. E005 highlights subjectivity in defining 'significant penetration' of those levels, which earnings risk amplifies. Adding an earnings proximity filter removes subjectivity by forcing a strict no-trade rule when earnings are near.

## Trading Implication

Do not enter a role-reversal trade at a prior support-turned-resistance level (C065) if earnings are within 7 days, unless the weekly reversal pattern (N007) has already fully completed after the earnings event.
