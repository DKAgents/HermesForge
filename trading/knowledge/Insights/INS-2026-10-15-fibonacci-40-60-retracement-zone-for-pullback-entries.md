# Fibonacci 40-60% retracement zone for pullback entries

## Discovery Summary

C050-secondary-trend-retracement-range defines the typical depth of secondary pullbacks in an uptrend, while EN068-4060-retracement-zones-for-timing-entries specifies the 40-60% retracement band as the optimal timing zone. This directly matches John Murphy's guideline that the ideal entry point in a pullback is the 40-60% retracement area, confirming the rule with the underlying concept of secondary trend retracement ranges.

## Trading Implication

In an established uptrend, wait for pullbacks to reach the 40-60% Fibonacci retracement zone before entering long, rather than buying earlier or deeper, to align with Murphy's high-probability entry area.
