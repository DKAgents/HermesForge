# Oscillator Entry via Secondary Trends

## Discovery Summary

C366 defines secondary trends as counter-trend moves within a primary trend. EN041 advises caution for oscillator entries in trending markets to avoid violating counter-trend risk. The connection reveals that oscillators can be safely entered when timed with secondary trends, as these pullbacks align with the primary trend and meet risk rule conditions.

## Trading Implication

Use oscillator entry strategies only during secondary trends (pullbacks) in strong trends; this aligns counter-trend moves with the overall direction and reduces risk.
