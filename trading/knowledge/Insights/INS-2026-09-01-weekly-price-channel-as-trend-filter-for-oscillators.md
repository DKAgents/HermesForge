# Weekly Price Channel as Trend Filter for Oscillators

## Discovery Summary

R142 states that weekly signals should filter daily MACD and Stochastics crossovers to prevent trading against the weekly trend. C130 presents the weekly price channel (weekly rule) as a simpler, high-performing alternative to moving averages for trend following. Combining these, a trader can use a weekly price channel breakout to define the primary trend, then only take daily MACD or Stochastics signals in that direction, effectively replacing the weekly moving averages discussed in N044 with a more straightforward trend-following device.

## Trading Implication

Use a weekly price channel (e.g., 4-week high/low breakout) to determine the trend, then only enter daily MACD or Stochastics crossover signals that align with the channel direction; ignore countertrend signals.
