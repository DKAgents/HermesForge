# Earnings calendar filter for role-reversal subjectivity

## Discovery Summary

The note E005 highlights subjectivity in defining a 'significant' penetration for support/resistance role reversal. Earnings events can cause sharp, temporary breaks that may be deemed significant but lack technical follow-through, exacerbating the subjectivity problem. Adding a mandatory 7-day earnings-free filter before accepting any role-reversal entry (as suggested by the seed) reduces false signals from news-driven penetrations.

## Trading Implication

Traders should implement a binary pre-trade check: if any company in the trade has earnings within the next 7 calendar days, skip the role-reversal entry to avoid unreliable penetrations.
