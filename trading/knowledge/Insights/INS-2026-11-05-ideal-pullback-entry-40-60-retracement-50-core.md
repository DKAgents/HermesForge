# Ideal pullback entry: 40-60% retracement, 50% core

## Discovery Summary

C050 (Secondary Trend Retracement Range) establishes that corrections typically retrace one-third to two-thirds, most often 50%. EN068 (40-60% Retracement Zones) directly specifies that pullbacks in uptrends retracing 40-60% of the prior advance are excellent for long entries. N085 (Fibonacci Percentage Retracements) adds nuance: in strong trends the minimum retracement is ~38%, while in weaker trends the maximum is ~62%. Together, they refine the entry rule: the 50% retracement is the sweet spot, but trend strength should dictate whether to act at 38% (strong trend) or wait toward 62% (weak trend).

## Trading Implication

In an uptrend, buy pullbacks that retrace 40-60% of the prior advance, prioritizing 50%; if the trend is unambiguously strong (e.g., persistent higher highs and higher lows), consider scaling in at 38% retracement, and if the trend appears weak, wait for a deeper 60-62% retracement before entering.
