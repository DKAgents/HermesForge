# Oscillator entries via secondary trend pullbacks

## Discovery Summary

C366-secondary-trends identifies intermediate counter-trend moves within a primary trend. EN041-oscillator-entry-strategy-in-trending-markets likely uses oscillator signals to enter in the direction of the trend. The connection is that oscillator entries should be conditioned on secondary trends (pullbacks) to avoid false signals from overextended oscillators in strong trends, which aligns with risk rules prohibiting counter-trend trades.

## Trading Implication

Traders should only take oscillator-based entries in the direction of the primary trend during secondary retracements, ignoring oscillator signals that would lead to counter-trend positions in strong trends.
