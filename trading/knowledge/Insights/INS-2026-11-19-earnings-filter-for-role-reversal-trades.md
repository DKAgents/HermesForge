# Earnings Filter for Role-Reversal Trades

## Discovery Summary

The role-reversal principle (C065, R029) is a core technical signal but is undermined by subjectivity in defining significant penetration (E005, Murphy p.83). The seed adds a mandatory pre-trade earnings calendar check as a binary filter to this entry criterion, thereby reducing event risk that could cause false penetrations or failed reversals. This connects technical role-reversal concepts with an earnings-risk overlay, making the subjective signal more robust.

## Trading Implication

Before entering any trade based on support-turned-resistance or speedline role reversal, check the earnings calendar and stand aside if earnings are within 7 days to avoid event-driven noise.
