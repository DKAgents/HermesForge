# Layered S/R Confluence Drives Stop Placement and Position Sizing

## Discovery Summary

C065 establishes that violated support becomes resistance in a downtrend (Murphy p.83), and EN069 adds that price gaps also function as support/resistance zones for precise entry timing. RG035 then completes the chain: once a valid technical level is identified — whether a role-reversed support or a gap boundary — it determines stop distance, which in turn sets position size under the 10% commitment / 5% max-risk rule. The non-obvious insight is that layering two independent S/R signals (gap boundary + role-reversed support) at the same price creates a tighter, more defensible stop location, allowing a larger position size within the same risk budget.

## Trading Implication

When a downtrend rally approaches a level that is simultaneously a violated support (now resistance) AND the lower end of a prior gap, place the short entry and stop just above that confluence zone; the tighter justified stop permits a larger position size under the RG035 money-management formula without exceeding the 5% account-risk cap.
