# Combine broken support and gap resistance for short entries/sizing

## Discovery Summary

C065 states that violated support becomes resistance in a downtrend, while EN069 says a price gap also acts as resistance and recommends selling rallies into the gap with a stop above it. When a prior support level and a gap coincide, the combined resistance zone gives a more objective short-entry trigger and stop placement. That stop distance then determines position size for a fixed-risk trade, tying Murphy's S/R rules to risk management.

## Trading Implication

In a downtrend, wait for a rally into a zone where broken prior support overlaps a price gap; sell into that zone with a stop above it, and size the position so the stop distance equals the planned account risk.
