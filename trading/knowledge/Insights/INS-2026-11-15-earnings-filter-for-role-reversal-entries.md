# Earnings Filter for Role-Reversal Entries

## Discovery Summary

The role-reversal principle across support/resistance (C065, E005) and speedlines (R029) depends on significant penetration, but E005 admits this is subjective. Adding a mandatory pre-trade earnings calendar check acts as a binary filter: if earnings are within 7 days, the role-reversal signal is invalidated or deferred, reducing exposure to gap risk that can disrupt the technical edge.

## Trading Implication

Before entering any trade based on prior support becoming resistance or a broken speedline, verify the next earnings date; if within 7 days, skip the trade or wait until after the announcement.
