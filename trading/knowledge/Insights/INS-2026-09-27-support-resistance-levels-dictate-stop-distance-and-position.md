# Support/Resistance Levels Dictate Stop Distance and Position Size

## Discovery Summary

The concept of prior support becoming resistance (C065) and price gaps as support/resistance (EN069) provide specific technical levels for stop placement. Risk guideline RG035 mandates that stops be placed at valid technical levels, and that stop distance then determines position size given a fixed maximum risk (e.g., 5% of account). Integrating these notes yields a systematic method: identify key levels from violated support/resistance or gaps, set stops there, and calculate position size using the money management rule. This sequence directly applies Murphy's principles across the technical and risk management domains.

## Trading Implication

A trader should first identify potential support/resistance levels from prior violated levels and gaps, then place protective stops at those levels, and finally size the position based on the stop distance and maximum allowable risk per trade.
