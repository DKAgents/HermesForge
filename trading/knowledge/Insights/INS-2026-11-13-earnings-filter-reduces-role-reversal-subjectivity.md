# Earnings Filter Reduces Role-Reversal Subjectivity

## Discovery Summary

E005-subjectivity-in-defining-significant-penetration admits that determining a 'significant' penetration is subjective, while C065-previous-support-as-future-resistance-in-downtrend and R029-speed-resistance-lines-role-reversal both rely on that role-reversal principle to generate entries. The seed rule adds an external, objective binary filter: if earnings are within 7 days, the penetration may be earnings-driven and therefore untrustworthy, so the role-reversal trade is skipped. This turns a subjective technical decision into a concrete pre-trade rule with a fundamental risk override.

## Trading Implication

Before entering any role-reversal trade from broken support/resistance or speedlines, check the earnings calendar; if the company reports within 7 days, stand aside and wait for a post-earnings retest or clean reversal signal.
