# Intermarket Chain with MA Crossovers

## Discovery Summary

Murphy's intermarket chain (commodities → bonds → stocks) can be applied to the double crossover (10/50) and triple crossover (4/9/18) methods. A crossover signal on a leading asset may precede a similar crossover on the next asset, allowing traders to anticipate trend changes. For example, a triple crossover buy on commodities could foreshadow a double crossover buy on stocks.

## Trading Implication

Monitor crossover signals on commodities and bonds as leading indicators for stock crossover signals. Enter stock positions only after confirming the intermarket sequence with a crossover on the preceding asset.
