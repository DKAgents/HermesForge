# Triple-Source Convergence Narrows Ideal Pullback Entry Zone

## Discovery Summary

Three independent frameworks converge on a specific retracement zone for trend entries. Dow Theory (C050) establishes the 1/3 to 2/3 retracement range for secondary corrections. Murphy's entry rule (EN068) narrows this to 40–60% as the actionable entry zone. Fibonacci retracement levels (N085) then add precision by identifying 38%, 50%, and 62% as the specific price levels within that zone — with trend strength determining which end applies (38% in strong trends, 62% in weaker ones). Together, these three sources create a tiered filter: wait for a 40–60% pullback, then execute at the nearest Fibonacci level within that range.

## Trading Implication

In an uptrend, enter long only when price pulls back to the 40–60% retracement zone AND touches a Fibonacci level (38%, 50%, or 62%); use trend strength to bias toward the 38–50% cluster in strong trends and 50–62% in weaker ones, avoiding entries outside this confirmed overlap zone.
