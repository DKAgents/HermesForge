# Volume Confirmation Aligns with False Breakout Filter

## Discovery Summary

EN008 and N013 both stress that valid upside breakouts require volume expansion, while light-volume breakouts risk being bull traps. C324 frames volume as a confirmation factor alongside price. Together, they imply a rule: before entering a long on pattern completion, require rising volume; if volume is absent, treat the breakout as suspect and reduce or avoid position size.

## Trading Implication

Traders should not enter a long at pattern completion unless volume is expanding; if volume is light, either stand aside or use a smaller position pending heavier volume confirmation.
