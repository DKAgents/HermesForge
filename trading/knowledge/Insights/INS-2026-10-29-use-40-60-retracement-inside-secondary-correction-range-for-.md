# Use 40-60% retracement inside secondary correction range for entries

## Discovery Summary

C050-secondary-trend-retracement-range defines the valid pullback depth for a secondary correction in an uptrend, normally one-third to two-thirds of the preceding advance. EN068-4060-retracement-zones-for-timing-entries narrows that broad zone to the 40-60% retracement area, which aligns with Murphy's ideal entry point for buying pullbacks. The broad secondary range alone is not an entry trigger; the 40-60% subzone provides the additional precision needed for timing entries.

## Trading Implication

In an uptrend, wait for a pullback to reach the 40-60% retracement zone of the prior advance before entering long, rather than buying early on shallow dips. Use the secondary correction range as validation that the pullback is normal and the 40-60% zone as the execution window.
