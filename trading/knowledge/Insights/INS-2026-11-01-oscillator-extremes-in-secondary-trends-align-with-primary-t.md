# Oscillator Extremes in Secondary Trends Align With Primary Trend

## Discovery Summary

EN041's oscillator entry rule buys oversold only in uptrends and sells overbought only in downtrends; C366 explains why: secondary trends are the counter-primary-trend corrections that create those oscillator extremes. Using N186 for breadth, an oversold/overbought reading should not be treated as a primary reversal signal but as an entry opportunity in the direction of the primary trend. In especially strong trends, when oscillator extremes may not print, the zero-line crossover variant in EN041 is the fallback trigger.

## Trading Implication

Before acting on an oscillator signal, label the current move as a secondary trend against a primary trend; trade only in the primary direction when that secondary extreme appears, and defer to the zero-line crossover in strong trends instead of forcing a counter-trend trade.
