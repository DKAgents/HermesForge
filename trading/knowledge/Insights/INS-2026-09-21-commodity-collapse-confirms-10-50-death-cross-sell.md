# Commodity Collapse Confirms 10/50 Death Cross Sell

## Discovery Summary

The 10/50-day moving average crossover (EN028, N039) is an intermediate-term trend signal for stocks. E040 identifies that a commodity price plunge is a leading stress indicator for commodity-exporting nations (Australia, Canada, Mexico, Russia), which often precedes or coincides with equity downtrends. A trader can confirm a bearish 10/50 crossover signal in these export-heavy equity markets by checking whether commodity prices (e.g., CRB index or specific commodity futures) are also falling. This intermarket confirmation increases the reliability of the crossover sell signal, aligning with Murphy's intermarket chain where commodities lead bonds and stocks.

## Trading Implication

When a 10-day moving average crosses below the 50-day on a commodity-exporting country's equity index, traders should only take the short/sell signal if commodity prices are concurrently in a downtrend. If commodities are rising, treat the crossover with caution or stand aside until both confirm.
