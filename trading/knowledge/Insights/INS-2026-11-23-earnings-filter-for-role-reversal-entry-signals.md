# Earnings Filter for Role-Reversal Entry Signals

## Discovery Summary

Role-reversal entries—previous support becoming resistance in downtrends (C065) and broken speedlines reversing roles (R029)—depend on judging whether a penetration is significant, a process Murphy admits is subjective (E005, p. 83). An earnings release within 7 days introduces event risk that can generate unreliable price moves, so adding a mandatory earnings-calendar filter removes the least trustworthy role-reversal signals. This turns a subjective 'significant penetration' judgment into a binary, objective pre-trade check.

## Trading Implication

Before taking any role-reversal trade, check the earnings calendar; if earnings are within 7 days, stand aside until after the announcement and confirm the penetration on post-earnings price action.
