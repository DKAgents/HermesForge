# Earnings Calendar Filter for Role-Reversal Entries

## Discovery Summary

Murphy's role-reversal rules (E005, C065, R029) rely on price penetration and are subjective; an earnings announcement can cause a gap through a support/resistance level, producing a false signal. Adding a mandatory pre-trade check that earnings are not within 7 days filters out high-risk entries where the reversal signal is likely to be noise. This connects technical analysis role-reversal to event-driven risk management.

## Trading Implication

Before taking any role-reversal trade (support-turned-resistance or speedline break), verify the stock has no earnings within 7 days; if it does, stand aside until after the event.
