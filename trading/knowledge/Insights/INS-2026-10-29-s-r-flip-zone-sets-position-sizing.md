# S/R Flip Zone Sets Position Sizing

## Discovery Summary

C065 (Previous Support as Future Resistance in Downtrend) identifies a specific resistance level after a break. C064 (Previous Peaks as Future Support in Uptrend) identifies specific support after a break. Together, they confirm the Murphy rule that a trader can place a stop-loss just beyond the flipped S/R level, which directly determines the risk per share and thus maximum position size for a given account risk.

## Trading Implication

When entering a trade based on a support/resistance flip, place your stop-loss just beyond the flipped level and size your position so that the stop distance equals your maximum allowable loss per share.
