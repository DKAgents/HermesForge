# CRB/Bond Ratio Triggers Sector Rotation with RS Filter

## Discovery Summary

The note 'Sector Rotation Based on CRB/Bond Ratio' provides directional rotation triggers based on the ratio's rising or falling trend. Adding 'Relative Strength Analysis for Sector Rotation' creates a filter: instead of rotating blindly when the CRB/Bond ratio changes, traders should only rotate into sectors that also show relative strength against the market. This prevents buying weak sectors that happen to be in the right inflation/deflation category.

## Trading Implication

When the CRB/Bond ratio rises, do not automatically buy all gold/oil/cyclical sectors; instead, select only those specific sectors or stocks showing positive relative strength against the S&P 500. When the ratio falls, similarly filter defensives by relative strength before rotating.
