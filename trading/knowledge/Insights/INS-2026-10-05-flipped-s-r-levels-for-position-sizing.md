# Flipped S/R levels for position sizing

## Discovery Summary

The note on previous support as future resistance in downtrend (Murphy, p. 83) and the seed question linking support/resistance to stop distance and position size together confirm that violated S/R levels provide precise, logical stop placements. This allows a trader to convert a technical reversal level into a fixed risk distance, enabling consistent position sizing per trade.

## Trading Implication

When trading in the direction of the trend, set your stop just beyond the flipped former support/resistance level and size your position so that the stop distance equals your predetermined risk tolerance.
