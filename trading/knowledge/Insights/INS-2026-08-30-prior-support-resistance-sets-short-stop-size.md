# Prior Support Resistance Sets Short Stop & Size

## Discovery Summary

C065 establishes that in a downtrend, broken support becomes resistance. EN069 shows that price gaps serve as precise support/resistance levels for entry timing and stop placement. RG035 mandates that stops must sit at valid technical levels (above resistance for shorts) and that stop distance directly determines position size via the 10% commitment/5% risk rule. Combining them yields a specific sequence: identify a retest of prior support (now resistance), sell short there, place the stop above that resistance, and size the position by dividing account dollar risk by the stop distance.

## Trading Implication

In a downtrend, sell short on a bounce to a violated support level, set a protective stop just above that level, and calculate position size as (account_risk_dollars) / (entry−stop distance).
