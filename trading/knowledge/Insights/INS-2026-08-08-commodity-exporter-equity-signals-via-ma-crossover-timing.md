# Commodity Exporter Equity Signals via MA Crossover Timing

## Discovery Summary

E040 identifies that commodity-exporting nations (Australia, Canada, Mexico, Russia) face amplified equity stress when commodity prices plunge — making their equity markets particularly sensitive to the intermarket chain Murphy describes. EN028/N039 provide a concrete mechanical entry/exit rule (10/50-day MA crossover) for stocks. The non-obvious connection is that commodity exporter equity indices (e.g., EWA, EWC) should be monitored for 10/50 MA crossover sell signals *specifically when commodity prices are declining*, as the intermarket pressure amplifies the reliability of the crossover signal in these markets versus commodity-importing nations.

## Trading Implication

When broad commodity indices show a sustained downtrend, prioritize 10-day crossing below 50-day sell signals on commodity-exporter equity ETFs (EWA, EWC, EWW, RSX) as high-conviction shorts, treating the commodity decline as a pre-qualifying filter before the MA crossover triggers entry.
