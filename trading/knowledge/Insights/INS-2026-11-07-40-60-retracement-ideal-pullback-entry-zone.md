# 40-60% Retracement: Ideal Pullback Entry Zone

## Discovery Summary

The 'Secondary Trend Retracement Range' concept states that Dow secondary corrections typically retrace one-third to two-thirds of the prior move, with 50% as the most common. Murphy's '40–60% Retracement Zones for Timing Entries' narrows this to a precise 40-60% zone for entries, while the Fibonacci note provides the 38%-62% boundaries for strong and weak trends. The overlap of these sources identifies 50% as the ideal entry point within an uptrend pullback, with 40-60% as the actionable entry band.

## Trading Implication

In an uptrend, wait for a pullback to retrace 40-60% of the prior advance, ideally near 50%, before entering long; avoid entries outside this zone unless trend-strength conditions justify the 38% or 62% extremes.
