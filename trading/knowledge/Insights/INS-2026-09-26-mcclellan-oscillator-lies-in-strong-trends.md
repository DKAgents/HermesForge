# McClellan Oscillator Lies in Strong Trends

## Discovery Summary

McClellan Oscillator (N186) is a market breadth oscillator that can show overbought/oversold signals, but in strongly trending markets these signals can be misleading. EN041 advises buying oversold conditions only in uptrends, which aligns with C366's secondary trends — counter-trend moves within a primary trend. However, the McClellan Oscillator's signals during a powerful primary trend may generate false counter-trend entry signals if not filtered by the primary trend direction.

## Trading Implication

When using the McClellan Oscillator for entry, first confirm the primary trend (e.g., via a 200-day moving average) and only take oversold buy signals in a confirmed uptrend or overbought sell signals in a confirmed downtrend; avoid acting on oscillator extremes during strong trends that may be 'lying' (false signals).
