# S/R Levels Determine Stops and Position Size

## Discovery Summary

C065 establishes that violated support becomes resistance in a downtrend, giving a specific short entry at that level. EN069 adds that price gaps also act as support/resistance, with protective stops placed above gaps in bear moves. Together these Murphy rules provide objective entry and stop levels, and the distance from entry to stop defines the per-share risk that drives position size.

## Trading Implication

In a downtrend, short bounces into prior support-turned-resistance or gap resistance, place the protective stop just above that level, and size the position so the stop distance equals the trader's predefined risk per trade.
