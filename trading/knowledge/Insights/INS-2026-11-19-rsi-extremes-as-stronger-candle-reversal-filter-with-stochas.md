# RSI Extremes as Stronger Candle Reversal Filter with Stochastic Confirmation

## Discovery Summary

R177-Filtered Candle Patterns — Oscillator Alternatives states that any oscillator can filter candle patterns, but the oscillator must be in its presignal overbought/oversold area before a reversal pattern is valid. N165 defines those RSI presignal zones as above 70 or below 30, and notes that divergence in these zones warns of possible reversals. C149 adds that RSI is less volatile than stochastics and reaches extremes less often, so an RSI extreme is a stricter filter, and the best confirmation occurs when RSI and stochastics are simultaneously overbought or oversold.

## Trading Implication

Before trading a candle reversal pattern, require RSI to be overbought for bearish reversals or oversold for bullish reversals; strengthen the setup by requiring stochastics to be simultaneously extreme and watching for RSI divergence.
