## Signal
**The Digital Asset Market CLARITY Act (H.R. 3633) faces a make-or-break Senate vote in September 2026, between the midterm election recess and the home stretch of the 119th Congress:**

1. **The stakes are binary for crypto:** Passage would establish a comprehensive U.S. crypto market structure framework — defining digital assets as commodities under CFTC jurisdiction, creating clear listing standards, and providing a path to regulatory compliance. This is widely seen as the most significant crypto legislation in U.S. history.

2. **The timing is urgent:** With midterm elections in November 2026, the September voting window is likely the last opportunity before the campaign season dominates the congressional calendar. If the CLARITY Act fails in September, it is effectively dead until the new Congress in 2027.

3. **The market impact is asymmetric:** Passage → massive relief rally across crypto (BTC +10-20%, altcoins +20-50% as regulatory uncertainty priced out). Failure → crypto corrects on regulatory overhang (BTC -5-15%, altcoins -20-30%).

4. **The Senate math is tight:** The bill passed Senate Banking Committee 15-9 (bipartisan). Full Senate passage requires 60 votes to overcome a filibuster. The cloture motion is the first test.

5. **The midterm election overlay:** Crypto has become a campaign issue. Pro-crypto candidates in both parties are using the CLARITY Act as a signaling tool. Passage before midterms is a political win for crypto-friendly incumbents.

## Hypothesis
**The CLARITY Act Senate vote is a binary event that will determine the regulatory trajectory for U.S. crypto markets for the next 12+ months. The asymmetric payoff favors being long crypto heading into the vote, with a defined risk stop on failure:**

1. **The asymmetry:** Passage upside (BTC +10-20%) significantly exceeds failure downside (BTC -5-15%) if properly risk-managed. The expected value is positive.
