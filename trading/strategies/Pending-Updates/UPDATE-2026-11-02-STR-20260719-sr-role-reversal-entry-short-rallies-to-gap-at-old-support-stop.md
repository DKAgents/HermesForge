## Insight Summary

C065 (Murphy p. 83) shows that in a downtrend a broken support level flips into resistance on bounces. EN069 adds a specific execution trigger: in a bear move, sell a rally to the lower edge of a price gap or into the gap, with a protective stop above the gap. When the broken support coincides with a gap, the two rules combine into a precise short setup, and the entry-to-stop distance becomes the basis for position sizing.

## Trading Implication

In a downtrend, identify old support zones that are also overhead gaps; short rallies into those gaps, place the stop above the gap, and scale position size so the entry-to-stop distance contains the allowed risk.
