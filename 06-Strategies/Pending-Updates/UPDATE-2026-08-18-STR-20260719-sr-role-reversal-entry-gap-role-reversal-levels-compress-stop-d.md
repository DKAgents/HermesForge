## Insight Summary

C065 establishes that violated support becomes resistance in a downtrend, creating a behaviorally-anchored price ceiling on bounces. EN069 adds that price gaps also function as resistance levels in bear moves, with a specific stop placement rule (above the gap). When a prior support level and an unfilled gap coincide near the same price zone, the two independent resistance signals compress the credible stop distance — a rally into the gap that also tags the prior support-turned-resistance gives a tighter, justified stop (just above the gap), which directly reduces position-sizing risk per the seed logic.

## Trading Implication

In a downtrend, identify short entries where a prior violated support level aligns with an overhead unfilled gap; sell the rally into that confluence zone and place the protective stop just above the gap's upper edge, allowing a tighter stop and proportionally larger position size than either signal alone would justify.
