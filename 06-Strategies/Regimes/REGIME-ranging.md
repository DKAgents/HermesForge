# Regime: Ranging

Price oscillates between support and resistance levels without establishing a sustained trend. Mean-reversion and support/resistance strategies tend to outperform here.

## Strategies That Work Here

- [[STR-20260726-eufearia-cci-reversal|STR-J EUFEARIA CCI]] — CCI mean-reversion at extremes, best in bear/ranging phases
- [[STR-20260719-sr-role-reversal-entry|STR-D S/R Role-Reversal]] — support/resistance role reversal entries

## Strategies That Fail Here

- [[STR-20260728-adaptive-trend|STR-I AdaptiveTrend]] — trend-following struggles in range-bound markets
- [[STR-20260719-macd-histogram-divergence-weekly-assessment|STR-B MACD Divergence]] — divergence signals less reliable without trend

## Key Indicators

- ADX(14) < 20
- Price near 200-day SMA
- Bollinger Bands contracting or flat
- Support/resistance levels holding
