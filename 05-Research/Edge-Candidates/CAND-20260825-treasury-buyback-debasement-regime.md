## Hypothesis
The US Treasury's explicit yield-management intervention (doubling bond buybacks) has activated a **structural dollar debasement regime**:

1. **Historical precedent:** When a G7 central bank/Treasury engages in yield suppression without addressing fiscal fundamentals, the FX market reprices debasement risk into hard assets. The 2020-2021 Fed MMT-era saw BTC rally from $7K to $64K; the 2026 Treasury buyback is a smaller but equivalent signal.

2. **Asymmetric positioning:** Prior to the breakout, BTC was rangebound $58K-$65K for 8+ weeks with declining OI (VanEck: perp OI down 10.6% MoM). The deleveraged base means the rally starts from a structurally healthier position — less leveraged long overhang to unwind.

3. **Follow-through thesis:** If the Treasury continues buyback operations (Sept 9 start date per announcement), the dollar weakness and hard-asset rally could persist 4-8 weeks. The debasement trade typically runs until the Treasury or Fed signals a policy reversal.

4. **Sector rotation:** The regime favors: BTC/ETH > Gold miners (GDX) > Commodity stocks (XLB) > Dollar-sensitive EM. It disadvantages: Financials (lower rates + flatter curve), USD cash, short-vol strategies (VIX may spike if debasement accelerates into a bond crisis).
