# Triple-Confirmed 38-62% Zone as Optimal Pullback Entry

## Discovery Summary

Three separate frameworks converge on the same retracement zone: Dow Theory (C050) identifies 1/3 to 2/3 corrections as secondary trends with 50% being most common; Murphy's entry rule (EN068) specifies 40-60% pullbacks as 'excellent' entry opportunities; and Fibonacci levels (N085) flag 38%, 50%, and 62% as key retracement zones. The overlap region of approximately 38-62% is independently validated by Dow Theory, Murphy's empirical rule, and Fibonacci mathematics — creating a high-confluence entry filter that no single note alone provides.

## Trading Implication

In an established uptrend, wait for pullbacks to enter the 38-62% retracement zone of the prior advance, treating 50% as the highest-probability entry level since it is confirmed by all three frameworks simultaneously; avoid entries outside this zone as they lack multi-framework support.
