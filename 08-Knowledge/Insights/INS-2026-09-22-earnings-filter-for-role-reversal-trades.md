# Earnings filter for role-reversal trades

## Discovery Summary

The notes E005-subjectivity-in-defining-significant-penetration and C065-previous-support-as-future-resistance-in-downtrend highlight the subjective nature of role reversal. The seed proposes adding a mandatory earnings calendar check: if earnings are within 7 days, skip the trade. This filters out high-risk events that can cause abrupt penetrations, reducing the subjectivity and improving trade reliability.

## Trading Implication

Before entering a role-reversal trade (e.g., shorting at former support now resistance in a downtrend), verify the underlying's earnings release date. If within 7 days, avoid the trade to mitigate gap risk.
