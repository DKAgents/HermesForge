# Murphy's 40-60% retracement zone for entries

## Discovery Summary

The Dow theory secondary trend retracement range (C050) of one-third to two-thirds overlaps with Fibonacci retracement levels (N085) of 38%, 50%, and 62%. Murphy's entry rule (EN068) narrows this to a 40–60% zone, providing a more precise, actionable filter for pullback entries within the broader trend context.

## Trading Implication

When entering on pullbacks in an uptrend or bounces in a downtrend, wait for a retracement of exactly 40–60% of the prior move rather than the wider Dow or Fibonacci ranges.
