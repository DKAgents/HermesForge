# Confluence of Role-Reversal and Gap Resistance for Stop Placement

## Discovery Summary

C065 states that prior support becomes resistance in a downtrend; EN069 notes that price gaps act as resistance for timing bear moves. When a downtrend presents both a violated support level and an overlapping gap, they form a combined resistance zone. RG035 requires stops at valid technical levels and ties stop distance to position sizing. The connection creates a filter: use the area where these two resistance factors coincide to set a protective stop above it, then compute position size based on that stop distance.

## Trading Implication

Identify short setups where a broken support level and an overhead gap align; place the protective stop just above the higher resistance, and size the position using the stop distance under the 5% risk rule from RG035.
