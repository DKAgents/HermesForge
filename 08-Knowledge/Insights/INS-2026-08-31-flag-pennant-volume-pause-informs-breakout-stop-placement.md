# Flag/Pennant volume pause informs breakout stop placement

## Discovery Summary

N043 notes flags and pennants pause on very light volume for 1–3 weeks before resuming on heavy volume, placing them at the midpoint of the move. R082 and N013 both require heavy volume at breakout to validate the signal and avoid false breakouts. When volume diverges from price during the trend into the pattern, the light-volume consolidation validates the pattern's health, so stops can be tightened under the pattern's boundary rather than widened.

## Trading Implication

Place a stop just beyond the flag/pennant boundary opposite the trend, and only move it to breakeven or trail after the breakout is confirmed by a heavy-volume surge as specified by R082.
