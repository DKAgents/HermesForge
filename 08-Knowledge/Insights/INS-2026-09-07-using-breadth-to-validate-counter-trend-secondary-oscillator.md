# Using Breadth to Validate Counter-Trend Secondary Oscillator Signals

## Discovery Summary

The oscillator entry strategy (EN041) buys oversold conditions in uptrends and sells overbought in downtrends, but secondary trends (C366) are counter-trend moves lasting weeks to months that can generate false oscillator extremes. The McClellan Oscillator (N186) provides market-level breadth data that can distinguish between a true oversold condition within a primary uptrend versus the start of a broader secondary correction. Using breadth divergence or extreme breadth readings adds a filter layer to confirm whether an oversold/overbought signal aligns with the primary trend or is being undermined by a developing secondary trend.

## Trading Implication

Before entering an oversold buy in an uptrend, confirm the McClellan Oscillator is not making lower lows alongside price or showing extreme negative breadth that would suggest a secondary downtrend is gaining strength rather than offering a dip-buying opportunity.
