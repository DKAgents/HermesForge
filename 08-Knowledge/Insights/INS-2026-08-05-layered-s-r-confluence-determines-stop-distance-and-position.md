# Layered S/R Confluence Determines Stop Distance and Position Size

## Discovery Summary

C065 establishes that violated support becomes resistance on bounces in downtrends, and EN069 adds that unfilled gaps also act as resistance levels where short entries can be timed (sell rally to lower end of gap, stop above gap). RG035 then closes the loop: the distance from entry to that technically-validated resistance stop determines maximum position size under the 5% risk / 10% commitment rule. The non-obvious insight is that using BOTH a prior support-turned-resistance level (C065) AND a nearby gap resistance (EN069) in the same price zone creates a tighter, more defensible stop placement — which, per RG035, allows a proportionally larger position size without violating money management constraints.

## Trading Implication

In a downtrend, when a bounce approaches both a violated support level (now resistance, C065) and the lower end of a gap (EN069) simultaneously, place the short entry and stop just above that confluence zone; the tighter technically-justified stop permits a larger position under the 5% risk rule (RG035), improving capital efficiency without increasing risk.
