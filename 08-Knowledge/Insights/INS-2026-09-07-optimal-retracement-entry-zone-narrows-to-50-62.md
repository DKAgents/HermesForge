# Optimal retracement entry zone narrows to 50-62%

## Discovery Summary

Murphy's secondary trend concept (C050) establishes that corrections typically retrace one-third to two-thirds of the prior move, with 50% as the sweet spot. The Fibonacci levels note (N085) adds a strength filter: strong trends retrace ~38%, weak trends ~62%. The retracement timing rule (EN068) specifies 40-60% as the ideal entry zone. Combined, these suggest that in a confirmed primary uptrend, the 50-62% zone is the highest-probability entry area because it aligns with the 'most common' retracement depth while still respecting the 60% upper bound of the actionable range.

## Trading Implication

When entering long on pullbacks in a strong primary uptrend, target entries at the 50-62% retracement zone rather than the wider 40-60% band—this filters out shallower pullbacks that may be trend-continuation traps and concentrates capital on the historically most reliable retracement depth.
