# RSI Extremes as Better Candle Pattern Filter

## Discovery Summary

C149 notes RSI is less volatile and reaches extremes less often than stochastics, making simultaneous oscillator extremes highly confirmatory. R177 states any oscillator can filter candle patterns, requiring overbought/oversold presignals. By combining these, RSI's overbought/oversold levels (above 70/below 30, per N165) become a superior filter for candle reversal patterns because its rarity reduces false signals compared to using a more volatile oscillator like stochastics.

## Trading Implication

Use RSI rather than stochastics as your primary oscillator to filter candle reversal patterns—only enter on a reversal pattern when RSI is clearly overbought (>70) or oversold (<30). This filters out many false signals from more frequent stochastic extremes.
