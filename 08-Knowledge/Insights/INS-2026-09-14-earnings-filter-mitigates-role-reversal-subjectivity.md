# Earnings filter mitigates role-reversal subjectivity

## Discovery Summary

The subjectivity in defining significant penetration (E005) is a known weakness for role-reversal signals like previous support becoming resistance (C065) or speed lines reversing roles (R029). Adding a mandatory pre-trade earnings calendar check (as per seed) filters out trades within 7 days of earnings, reducing the impact of subjective penetration judgments during high-volatility periods.

## Trading Implication

Traders should implement a binary filter: if earnings are within 7 days, skip any role-reversal entry based on support/resistance or speedline breaks.
