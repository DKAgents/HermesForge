# Earnings Filter Resolves Role-Reversal Subjectivity Across All S/R Forms

## Discovery Summary

E005 identifies that 'significant penetration' for role-reversal is inherently subjective (Murphy, p. 83), creating ambiguous entry signals. C065 and R029 both describe role-reversal mechanics — for price-based support/resistance and speed resistance lines respectively — but neither resolves when a penetration is actionable. The seed condition (earnings within 7 days = no trade) provides a binary, objective filter that sidesteps the subjectivity problem: if the catalyst environment is unstable, the role-reversal signal — regardless of how 'significant' the penetration appears — is disqualified before subjectivity even becomes an issue.

## Trading Implication

Before acting on any role-reversal entry (whether from violated support levels per C065 or broken speedlines per R029), apply the earnings binary filter first: if earnings are within 7 days, skip the trade entirely, eliminating the need to adjudicate penetration significance in a high-volatility, unpredictable environment.
