# Subjectivity undermines role-reversal rules

## Discovery Summary

The subjectivity in defining significant penetration (E005) directly affects the reliability of support/resistance role reversal (C065) and speedline role reversal (R029). Without an objective criterion, traders may misidentify false breaks. This highlights the need for a quantified filter to validate role-reversal signals.

## Trading Implication

Traders should adopt a consistent, objective rule for determining significant penetration (e.g., a 3% or 1 ATR close beyond the level) before acting on role-reversal setups, reducing false signals.
