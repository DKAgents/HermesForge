# Confluence of gap and broken support tightens stops, sizes positions

## Discovery Summary

C065 states prior support becomes resistance in a downtrend (Murphy p.83). EN069 adds that price gaps also act as resistance for timing short entries. When these resistance zones overlap, RG035’s rule to place stops above technical resistance and size based on stop distance allows a tighter, technically valid stop. The conjunction of the two resistance factors yields a more precise stop level, directly affecting position sizing.

## Trading Implication

In a downtrend, short rallies into a zone where a prior broken support and a price gap coincide; place a protective stop just above the overlapping resistance and use the smaller stop distance to allocate a larger position within the same dollar risk limit.
