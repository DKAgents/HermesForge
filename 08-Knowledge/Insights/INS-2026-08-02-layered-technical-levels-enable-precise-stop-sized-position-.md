# Layered Technical Levels Enable Precise Stop-Sized Position Sizing

## Discovery Summary

C065 and EN069 identify two distinct, confluent resistance sources in a downtrend — violated support levels (C065, Murphy p.83) and unfilled price gaps (EN069) — both of which can serve as the technical anchor for stop placement. RG035 completes the sequence by requiring that stops be placed at valid technical levels above resistance for shorts, then back-calculating position size from the distance to that stop within a 5% max-risk rule. When a violated support level and a price gap coincide overhead, they create a high-confidence resistance cluster, allowing the trader to define a tight, technically justified stop just above that zone and maximize position size within money management constraints.

## Trading Implication

In a downtrend, identify where a violated support level and an overhead gap coincide to form a resistance cluster; place your short entry stop just above that cluster and use the resulting stop distance to calculate maximum allowable position size per RG035's 5% risk rule — tighter confluence means a tighter stop and a larger position.
