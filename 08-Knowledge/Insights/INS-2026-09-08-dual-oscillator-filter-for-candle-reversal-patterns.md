# Dual Oscillator Filter for Candle Reversal Patterns

## Discovery Summary

R177 states any oscillator can filter candle patterns when in overbought/oversold territory. C149 reveals that RSI is less volatile than stochastics and the best signals occur when both are simultaneously extreme. This adds a dual-confirmation condition: a candle reversal pattern is most reliable when both RSI and stochastics are in their respective overbought/oversold zones, not just one oscillator.

## Trading Implication

Before acting on a reversal candle pattern, a trader should verify that both RSI and stochastics are in overbought or oversold territory simultaneously to increase signal reliability.
