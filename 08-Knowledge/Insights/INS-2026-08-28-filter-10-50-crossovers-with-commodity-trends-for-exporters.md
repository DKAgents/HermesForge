# Filter 10/50 Crossovers with Commodity Trends for Exporters

## Discovery Summary

The 10/50-day moving average crossover in N039 and EN028 provides stock buy/sell signals, but E040 notes that falling commodity prices damage commodity-exporting countries such as Australia, Canada, Mexico, and Russia and lead their equity markets. This suggests the 10/50 crossover alone may give late or unreliable signals in those markets when commodities are in a downtrend. Commodity price trends can therefore act as a regime filter for 10/50 crossover signals in commodity-exporter equity markets.

## Trading Implication

Before acting on a 10/50 bullish crossover in Australia, Canada, Mexico, or Russia equity indices/ETFs, check the commodity trend; avoid or delay buy signals when commodity prices are falling and prefer crossover signals aligned with stable or rising commodities.
