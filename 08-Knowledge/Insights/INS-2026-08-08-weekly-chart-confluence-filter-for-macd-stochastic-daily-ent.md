# Weekly Chart Confluence Filter for MACD/Stochastic Daily Entries

## Discovery Summary

R142 establishes that MACD and Stochastics crossover signals on daily charts should only be taken in the direction confirmed by weekly chart signals. N044 adds that the 10/40-week moving average relationship defines the primary trend direction on weekly charts. C130 provides a third weekly-timeframe tool — price channel breakouts — that can serve as an independent weekly trend confirmation. Together, these three weekly-timeframe methods (MA crossover, price channel, and weekly MACD/Stochastic direction) form a layered weekly trend filter before acting on daily crossover signals.

## Trading Implication

Before taking a MACD or Stochastic crossover signal on a daily chart, confirm trend alignment using at least one weekly-timeframe tool: the 10/40-week MA relationship (N044) or a weekly price channel breakout direction (C130); only enter in the direction that all weekly filters agree on.
