# Oscillator entries in secondary corrections

## Discovery Summary

The McClellan oscillator's overbought/oversold readings can be used with the EN041 rule: in a primary uptrend, buy during oversold conditions typically occurring during secondary (counter-trend) corrections. This aligns with the concept of secondary trends providing entry points in the direction of the primary trend.

## Trading Implication

Use the McClellan oscillator to time entries during secondary corrections: wait for oversold readings in a primary uptrend before initiating long positions, and use overbought readings in primary downtrends for shorts.
