# Triple Convergence: 38-62% Zone Validated by Three Frameworks

## Discovery Summary

All three notes independently converge on the 38–62% retracement zone as the high-probability entry range, but from different theoretical origins: Dow Theory (C050) identifies 1/3 to 2/3 corrections as normal secondary trend behavior; Murphy's trading rule (EN068) operationalizes 40–60% as the actionable entry band; and Fibonacci analysis (N085) provides the mathematical rationale with 38.2%, 50%, and 61.8% as key levels. The non-obvious insight is that the 50% level sits at the intersection of all three frameworks simultaneously — Dow's most common retracement, Murphy's midpoint of the 40–60% zone, and a Fibonacci-derived ratio — making it the single highest-conviction sub-level within the zone.

## Trading Implication

In an uptrend, prioritize limit orders near the 50% retracement of the prior advance, treating the 38–62% band as the full entry window; entries outside this band (under 38% or over 62%) lack multi-framework support and should be avoided or sized smaller.
