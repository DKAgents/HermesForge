# 40-60% Pullback Zone as Confluent Entry Filter

## Discovery Summary

Dow's secondary trend retracement range of one-third to two-thirds (C050) overlaps with Fibonacci retracement levels of 38%, 50%, and 62% (N085). Murphy's entry rule explicitly identifies the 40-60% pullback zone as the ideal timing for entries in uptrends (EN068). The convergence of these three frameworks around the 50% level makes the 40-60% zone a high-confluence entry filter.

## Trading Implication

Use the 50% retracement as the prime entry target, with 38% marking the minimum pullback in strong trends and 62% the maximum in weaker trends, to time long entries on uptrend pullbacks within the 40-60% zone.
