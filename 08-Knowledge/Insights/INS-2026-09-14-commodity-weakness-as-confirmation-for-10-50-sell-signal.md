# Commodity Weakness as Confirmation for 10/50 Sell Signal

## Discovery Summary

The 10/50 crossover rule (N039, EN028) gives a sell signal when the 10-day MA crosses below the 50-day MA. The intermarket insight from E040 suggests that commodity price trends act as a leading indicator for commodity-exporting economies. Therefore, a bearish crossover in a stock from a commodity-exporting country (e.g., Australia, Canada) is stronger if commodity prices are also falling, adding a confluence filter to the technical signal.

## Trading Implication

When a 10/50 sell signal occurs in a commodity-exporting stock, check the trend of a broad commodity index (e.g., CRB). If commodities are also in a downtrend, increase position size or execute the short earlier; if commodities are rising, treat the sell signal with less conviction.
