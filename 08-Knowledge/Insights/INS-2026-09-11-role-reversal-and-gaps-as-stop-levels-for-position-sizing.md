# Role Reversal and Gaps as Stop Levels for Position Sizing

## Discovery Summary

Murphy's role reversal concept (C065) and gap support/resistance rule (EN069) supply specific, valid technical levels for stop placement that satisfy the risk guideline RG035's requirement to place stops at technical points. By identifying previous support turned resistance or gap edges, a trader obtains a precise stop distance, which then directly determines position size under the 5% risk and 10% commitment rules outlined in RG035.

## Trading Implication

When shorting in a downtrend, place stops just above the previous support level that has flipped to resistance (C065) or above a bearish gap's lower edge (EN069), then calculate position size by risking no more than 5% of allocated capital, as described in RG035.
