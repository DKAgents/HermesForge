# Dynamic Retracement Entry Zones by Trend Strength

## Discovery Summary

The 40-60% retracement zone from EN068 provides a broad entry range, but N085's Fibonacci framework adds a strength-dependent filter: in strong trends, focus on the lower end near 38-50%, while in weaker trends, extend patience to the 62% level. C050's Dow Theory context confirms these ranges fall within the normal secondary trend retracement bounds of one-third to two-thirds. Together they convert a static entry zone into a dynamic one adjusted to trend strength.

## Trading Implication

Assess trend strength before timing pullback entries — in strong uptrends, enter near the 38-50% retracement of the prior advance; in weaker trends, wait for deeper 50-62% retracements to improve risk/reward.
