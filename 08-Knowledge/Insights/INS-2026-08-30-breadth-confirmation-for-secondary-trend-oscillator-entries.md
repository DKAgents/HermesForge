# Breadth Confirmation for Secondary Trend Oscillator Entries

## Discovery Summary

Oscillator entry rule EN041 advises buying oversold in an uptrend, but secondary trends (C366) can cause prolonged counter-move corrections that mimic minor oversold dips. The McClellan Oscillator (N186) measures broad market breadth, and its oversold levels and direction can differentiate between a brief pullback and a deeper secondary downtrend. Requiring a breadth thrust from the McClellan Oscillator when an individual setup triggers adds a filter that reduces early entries into an unfinished secondary correction.

## Trading Implication

When an oversold buy signal appears in an uptrend, wait for the McClellan Oscillator to cross above zero or turn up from its own oversold reading before entering, to avoid entering mid-correction in a secondary trend.
