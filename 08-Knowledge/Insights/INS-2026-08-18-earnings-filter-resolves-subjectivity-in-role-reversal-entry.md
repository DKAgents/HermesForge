# Earnings Filter Resolves Subjectivity in Role-Reversal Entry Timing

## Discovery Summary

E005 acknowledges that 'significant penetration' for role-reversal (support-to-resistance or resistance-to-support) is inherently subjective, with no universal benchmark. C065 and R029 both describe role-reversal mechanics across price levels and speedlines, creating multiple potential entry signals whose quality is hard to objectively assess. The seed question introduces an earnings calendar binary filter: if earnings are within 7 days, the role-reversal entry is skipped entirely. This external, objective binary condition directly compensates for the subjectivity problem in E005 by eliminating a class of trades where volatility from earnings could produce false role-reversal confirmations or whipsaw penetrations that appear significant but are event-driven rather than technically valid.

## Trading Implication

Before acting on any role-reversal signal (whether at a price support/resistance level per C065 or a broken speedline per R029), a trader must first check the earnings calendar — if earnings are due within 7 days, the trade is skipped regardless of how convincing the penetration appears, removing one source of subjective error identified in E005.
