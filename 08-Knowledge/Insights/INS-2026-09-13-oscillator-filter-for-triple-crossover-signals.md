# Oscillator Filter for Triple Crossover Signals

## Discovery Summary

The double-crossover oscillator concept (C128) can be applied to the 4-9-18 day triple crossover method (N037, R323) by measuring the spread between two of the three averages—such as the 4-day and 18-day—as a momentum gauge. This transforms the simple crossover rule into a conditional signal that requires the spread oscillator to align with the crossover direction, helping avoid false signals in volatile or directionless markets.

## Trading Implication

Before entering a trade on a 4-9-18 crossover, confirm that the 4-18 spread oscillator is also moving in the trade's direction and not showing overextended readings, filtering out whipsaws.
