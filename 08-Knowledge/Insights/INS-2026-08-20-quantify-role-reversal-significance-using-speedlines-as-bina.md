# Quantify Role-Reversal Significance Using Speedlines as Binary Filter

## Discovery Summary

E005 identifies the core weakness in role-reversal trading: 'significant penetration' is subjective with no universal definition (Murphy, p. 83). C065 confirms the mechanism — violated support becomes resistance in downtrends (Murphy, p. 83) — but inherits the same subjectivity problem. R029 offers a partial resolution: speedlines provide a structural, rule-based framework where the 2/3 line breaking to the 1/3 line is a defined, objective event. By requiring that a role-reversal entry (C065) only be taken when price action also confirms a speedline break (R029), traders replace subjective 'significance' judgment with a geometric, calculable threshold.

## Trading Implication

When trading role-reversal setups from C065, require that the penetration also breaches the relevant speedline (2/3 or 1/3 line from R029) to qualify as 'significant' per E005 — entries that lack speedline confirmation should be skipped or sized down as ambiguous. This converts the acknowledged subjectivity in E005 into a binary, pre-defined condition.
