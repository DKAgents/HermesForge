# Gap + Role-Reversal Zones Define Optimal Stop Placement and Position Sizing

## Discovery Summary

C065 establishes that violated support becomes resistance in downtrends, and EN069 specifies that gaps in a bear move act as resistance at their lower end — both identify the same type of technical resistance ceiling on bounces. RG035 mandates that stops must be placed at valid technical levels (above resistance for shorts) and that stop distance directly controls position size via the 5% max risk rule. The non-obvious connection is that combining gap-based resistance (EN069) with role-reversal resistance (C065) at the same price zone creates a confluent, high-confidence resistance level — which in turn justifies tighter stop placement above that zone, allowing a larger position size within RG035's money management constraints.

## Trading Implication

When a downtrend bounce rallies into a price zone where a prior violated support and a gap's lower end coincide, place a short entry there with a stop just above the confluence zone; the tighter, technically-justified stop permits a larger position size under the 5% max risk rule without violating money management limits.
