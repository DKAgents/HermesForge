# McClellan vs secondary trend: oscillator false signals

## Discovery Summary

McClellan Oscillator (N186) measures market breadth overbought/oversold, but EN041 warns that oscillators give false signals in strong trends. Secondary trends (C366) provide the counter-trend move that makes oscillator-based entries valid by aligning the oversold/overbought condition with the intermediate correction within the primary trend.

## Trading Implication

Only take McClellan Oscillator oversold buys in an uptrend when price is in a secondary reaction (counter-trend dip), and overbought sells in a downtrend during secondary rallies—avoid entry when the oscillator signal coincides with the primary trend's extreme.
