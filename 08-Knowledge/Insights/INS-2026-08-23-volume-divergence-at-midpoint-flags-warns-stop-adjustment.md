# Volume Divergence at Midpoint Flags Warns Stop Adjustment

## Discovery Summary

N043 establishes that flags and pennants form at approximately the midpoint of a move, with volume drying up during consolidation then surging at breakout. R082 mandates that valid breakouts require heavy volume confirmation. C050 adds that secondary corrections retrace 1/3 to 2/3 of prior moves. When a flag/pennant breakout occurs WITHOUT the required volume surge (R082 violation), the pattern may actually be a secondary correction rather than continuation, and the expected remaining move distance — estimated from the midpoint rule in N043 — shrinks, warranting tighter stops.

## Trading Implication

If a flag or pennant breaks out on below-average volume, treat the measured-move target as unreliable and tighten stops to no more than the 1/3 retracement level of the flagpole (per C050 retracement norms), rather than trailing to the full projected target.
