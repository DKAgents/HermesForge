# Volume-Confirmed Breakouts Need Tighter Stops

## Discovery Summary

R082 and N013 establish that valid breakouts require heavy volume, while light-volume breakouts are suspect and may reverse. N043 adds that flags/pennants form on light volume during consolidation, then resume on heavy volume. Therefore, a breakout from a flag/pennant on light volume is a false-signal warning, and stops should be tightened or exited immediately, especially since the flagpole's momentum often defines the expected move.

## Trading Implication

When trading flag/pennant breakouts, require a volume surge at breakout. If volume is light, treat the breakout as false, tighten the stop to the pattern's boundary or exit, and consider fading the move if price then drops on heavy volume.
