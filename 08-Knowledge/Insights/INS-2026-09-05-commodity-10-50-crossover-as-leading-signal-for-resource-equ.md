# Commodity 10/50 Crossover as Leading Signal for Resource Equity Markets

## Discovery Summary

The 10/50-day moving average crossover method (N039, EN028) is typically used on individual stocks, but applying it to a broad commodity price index creates a leading macro filter for equity markets of commodity-exporting nations. E040 notes that plunging commodity prices damage Australia, Canada, Mexico, and Russia; a bearish 10/50 crossover on commodities can anticipate economic stress and subsequent sell signals in those countries' stock indices. This aligns with Murphy's intermarket chain, where commodity weakness flows through to equities of resource-dependent economies.

## Trading Implication

Apply the 10/50-day crossover to a commodity index (e.g., CRB). When the 10-day crosses below the 50-day, treat it as an early warning to reduce or avoid long positions in equities of Australia, Canada, Mexico, and Russia; a bullish crossover supports initiating or holding those positions.
