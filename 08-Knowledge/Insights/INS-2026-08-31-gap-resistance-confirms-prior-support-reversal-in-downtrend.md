# Gap resistance confirms prior support reversal in downtrend

## Discovery Summary

C065 establishes that prior support becomes resistance in a downtrend as trapped buyers sell on rallies. EN069 specifies that price gaps can serve as support or resistance for timing entries, with sell entries placed at the lower end of a gap. When a downtrend gap aligns with a violated support level, the gap provides a precise, rule-based entry zone on a rally back to that former support, with a stop above the gap's upper boundary defining risk for position sizing.

## Trading Implication

When a prior support level is breached in a downtrend and a gap exists near that level, sell rallies into the lower end of the gap rather than at the support level alone, and place the protective stop above the gap's upper boundary to tighten risk and improve position sizing.
