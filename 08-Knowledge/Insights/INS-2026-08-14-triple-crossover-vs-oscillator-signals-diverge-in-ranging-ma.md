# Triple Crossover vs Oscillator Signals Diverge in Ranging Markets

## Discovery Summary

C128 notes that double-crossover MA differences function as oscillators (like MACD), which inherently capture momentum and divergence. R323 and N037 describe the triple crossover method (4-9-18 day) as a trend-following signal system. The seed question about regime failure points to a non-obvious split: in ranging/volatile markets, the triple crossover (R323/N037) generates whipsaw signals because it is purely trend-following, while the oscillator interpretation of double MA differences (C128) can still provide useful mean-reversion signals by detecting convergence/divergence rather than directional crossover.

## Trading Implication

In ranging or high-volatility regimes, suppress or reduce position sizing on 4-9-18 triple crossover signals (R323/N037) and instead rely on the oscillator-mode interpretation of MA differences (C128/MACD-style) to identify overbought/oversold extremes rather than trend entries.
