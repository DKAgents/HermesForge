# 10/50 Crossover on Commodities Filters Commodity-Exporter Stocks

## Discovery Summary

The double crossover method using 10-day and 50-day moving averages (N039, EN028) can be applied to a broad commodity price index rather than individual stocks. According to the edge condition on commodity exporters (E040), falling commodity prices lead economic stress in nations like Australia, Canada, Mexico, and Russia. By monitoring the 10/50 crossover on commodities, a trader gains a leading intermarket filter for timing equity positions in those countries—consistent with Murphy's commodities→stocks chain.

## Trading Implication

Only take long signals in commodity-exporting country stock indices when a commodity benchmark's 10-day MA is above the 50-day MA; exit or go short when it crosses below.
