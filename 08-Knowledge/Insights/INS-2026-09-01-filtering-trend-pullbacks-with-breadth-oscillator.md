# Filtering Trend Pullbacks with Breadth Oscillator

## Discovery Summary

Secondary trends are counter-trend corrections lasting weeks to months (C366). The oscillator entry rule advises buying oversold in an uptrend (EN041). The McClellan Oscillator (N186) measures broad market breadth and identifies overbought/oversold conditions. When the primary trend is up, a secondary pullback becomes a better entry point if the McClellan Oscillator is also oversold, confirming the pullback is broad-based rather than a narrow sector rotation, thereby reducing the risk of an oscillator whipsaw in a strong trend.

## Trading Implication

Require the McClellan Oscillator to be oversold before buying an oversold condition in an uptrend per EN041, using the breadth reading to filter out false secondary trend pullbacks.
