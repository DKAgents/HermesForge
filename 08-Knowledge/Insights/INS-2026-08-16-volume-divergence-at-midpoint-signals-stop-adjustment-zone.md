# Volume Divergence at Midpoint Signals Stop Adjustment Zone

## Discovery Summary

Flags and pennants (N043) occur at approximately the midpoint of a move, with volume contracting during consolidation and surging on breakout — directly aligning with R082's requirement that valid breakouts need heavy volume. C050 establishes that secondary corrections retrace 1/3 to 2/3 of prior moves. Together, if volume fails to surge on the flag/pennant breakout (diverging from price), the pattern may actually be a secondary correction retracement rather than a continuation, meaning the 50% retracement level from C050 becomes the critical stop reference rather than the flagpole low.

## Trading Implication

When a suspected flag or pennant breakout occurs on weak volume (violating R082), treat the move as a potential secondary correction and place stops at the 50% retracement level of the prior flagpole move rather than tight below the consolidation low.
