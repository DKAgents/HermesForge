# Breadth oscillator for secondary trend entries

## Discovery Summary

The secondary trends concept (C366) defines counter-trend moves within a primary trend. The oscillator entry strategy (EN041) advises buying oversold conditions in an uptrend, which naturally aligns with entering during a secondary correction. Using the McClellan Oscillator (N186) as the breadth-based oscillator provides a market-wide oversold signal, confirming that the secondary correction is sufficiently widespread, thus avoiding false signals in strong trends.

## Trading Implication

In a primary uptrend, wait for a secondary correction confirmed by a McClellan Oscillator oversold reading, then initiate long positions per EN041.
