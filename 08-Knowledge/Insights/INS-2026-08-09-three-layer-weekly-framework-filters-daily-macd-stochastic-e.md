# Three-Layer Weekly Framework Filters Daily MACD/Stochastic Entries

## Discovery Summary

R142 establishes that MACD and Stochastics crossover signals on daily charts must be filtered through weekly chart trend direction first. N044 provides the structural weekly trend framework — using 10/40-week MAs to define primary trend and support levels. C130 adds a channel breakout confirmation layer, noting weekly price channels are among the most successful trend-following devices. Together, these three notes construct a multi-layer weekly confirmation stack: weekly MA alignment (N044) + weekly price channel breakout (C130) must both confirm before acting on daily MACD/Stochastic crossovers (R142).

## Trading Implication

Only take daily MACD or Stochastic crossover signals when: (1) price is above the 40-week MA in a bull setup, and (2) price has broken above its weekly price channel — requiring two weekly-timeframe confirmations before any daily entry trigger is valid.
