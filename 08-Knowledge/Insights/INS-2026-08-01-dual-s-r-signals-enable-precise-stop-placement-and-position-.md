# Dual S/R Signals Enable Precise Stop Placement and Position Sizing

## Discovery Summary

C065 establishes that violated support becomes resistance in downtrends, and EN069 adds that price gaps also function as S/R levels for timing entries. Together they provide two distinct, technically-grounded resistance levels for short entries. RG035 then completes the sequence: once resistance is identified (whether via role-reversal from C065 or gap from EN069), the stop distance above that resistance determines maximum position size under the 5% risk / 10% commitment rule.

## Trading Implication

On a short trade, identify the nearest overhead resistance level (role-reversed support per C065 or gap lower end per EN069), place the protective stop just above it, then calculate position size so that the stop distance does not exceed 5% of total account equity per RG035 — never adjust the technical stop to fit a desired position size.
