# 40–60% zone unifies Dow and Fibonacci retracement logic

## Discovery Summary

Murphy’s 40–60% retracement entry zone (EN068-4060-retracement-zones-for-timing-entries) directly aligns with both Dow’s observation that secondary trends retrace one‑third to two‑thirds (C050) and Fibonacci’s key 38% and 62% levels (N085). The Fibonacci note further conditions this zone: in strong trends retracements are typically shallow (near 38%), while in weaker trends they run deeper (near 62%), explaining why the 40–60% band works as a practical catch‑all for entries.

## Trading Implication

Rather than waiting for an exact Fibonacci level, place entry orders inside the 40–60% retracement zone of the prior impulse, expecting pullbacks to cluster near 40% in strong trends and near 60% in weaker trends.
