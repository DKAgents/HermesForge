# Earnings calendar filter resolves support/resistance penetration subjectivity

## Discovery Summary

The seed question proposes adding a mandatory 7-day earnings binary filter before entering role-reversal trades. E005 notes that defining 'significant penetration' for support/resistance role reversal is inherently subjective, while C065 and R029 describe how violated levels become resistance. By requiring no earnings within 7 days, the filter reduces false signals from earnings-driven volatility that can cause temporary, non-significant penetrations, thereby partially resolving the subjectivity problem noted by Murphy on p. 83.

## Trading Implication

Before taking any role-reversal entry (e.g., shorting a rally to prior support turned resistance), check the earnings calendar; if the stock has earnings within 7 days, skip the trade to avoid whipsaws from event-driven penetrations that may not meet the 'significant' threshold.
