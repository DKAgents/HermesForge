# Gap + Violated Support Creates Dual-Confirmed Resistance for Sizing

## Discovery Summary

C065 establishes that violated support flips to resistance due to trapped buyers selling into rallies. EN069 adds that price gaps also act as resistance on bear-market rallies, with entries triggered at the gap's lower end and stops above it. When a violated support level coincides with an unfilled gap overhead, both mechanisms independently reinforce the same resistance zone, creating a higher-conviction short entry. RG035 then completes the chain: that tighter, technically-validated resistance cluster allows a closer stop above the dual-confirmed level, which under the 5% max risk rule permits a proportionally larger position size.

## Trading Implication

When a downtrend bounce approaches a zone where both a violated support level and a price gap converge, sell at the lower end of the gap with a stop above it; because the stop distance is compressed by dual confirmation, position size can be increased within RG035's 5% risk limit without violating money management rules.
