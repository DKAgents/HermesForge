# Gap and Role-Reversal Levels Define Stop Distance and Position Size

## Discovery Summary

C065 establishes that violated support becomes resistance in downtrends, and EN069 adds that gaps also function as support/resistance zones for precise entry timing (buy dip to upper gap edge, sell rally to lower gap edge). RG035 then completes the sequence: once a technical level is identified — whether a role-reversed support or a gap boundary — the distance from entry to that level determines the stop, and the stop distance combined with the 5% max risk rule ($5,000 on a $100,000 account) mechanically determines position size. The non-obvious connection is that gap-defined and role-reversal-defined resistance levels in downtrends are not just entry signals but the specific technical anchors that feed directly into the RG035 position-sizing formula.

## Trading Implication

In a downtrend, sell a rally into a prior violated support level or into the lower end of a gap, place the protective stop just above that level, then calculate maximum position size by dividing the 5% account risk limit ($5,000 on $100K) by the per-share distance to that stop — never defaulting to an arbitrary stop distance.
