# Refining Retracement Zones with Trend-Strength Context

## Discovery Summary

Murphy's general 40–60% retracement zone (EN068) for entries is common, but Fibonacci levels (N085) add a critical condition: the specific 38%, 50%, or 62% retracement you should target depends on trend strength. In strong uptrends, pullbacks typically stop near 38%; in weak trends, they reach 62%. This aligns with Dow Theory's secondary trend retracement range of one-third to two-thirds (C050), but the Fibonacci breakdown tells you which end of that range to use given current market conditions.

## Trading Implication

Instead of blindly entering anywhere in the 40–60% zone, assess trend strength first: target entries near the 38% retracement in strong trends and wait for the 62% level in weak trends, using the 50% level as your baseline when trend strength is ambiguous.
