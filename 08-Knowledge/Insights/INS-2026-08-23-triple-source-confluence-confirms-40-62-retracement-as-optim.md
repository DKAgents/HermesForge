# Triple-Source Confluence Confirms 40-62% Retracement as Optimal Entry Zone

## Discovery Summary

Three independent frameworks converge on the same retracement zone: Dow Theory (C050) states secondary corrections retrace one-third to two-thirds (33-66%) of the prior move; Murphy's entry rule (EN068) specifies 40-60% pullbacks as 'excellent' long entry opportunities; Fibonacci retracements (N085) identify 38%, 50%, and 62% as key levels, with 38% marking strong trends and 62% marking weaker ones. The 40-60% entry zone from EN068 is bracketed and validated by both the Dow secondary trend retracement range and the Fibonacci cluster, creating a high-confluence entry window that is theoretically grounded across multiple independent systems.

## Trading Implication

In an uptrend, wait for pullbacks into the 40-62% retracement zone and look for confirmation (e.g., reversal candle, volume shift) specifically at 38%, 50%, or 62% Fibonacci levels within that band; entries outside this window (below 33% or above 66%) lack multi-system support and should be avoided or sized smaller.
