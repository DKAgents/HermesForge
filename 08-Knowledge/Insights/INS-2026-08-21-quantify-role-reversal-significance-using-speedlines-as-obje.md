# Quantify Role-Reversal Significance Using Speedlines as Objective Filter

## Discovery Summary

E005 identifies a known weakness in role-reversal trading: 'significant penetration' is subjective with no universally precise definition (Murphy, p. 83). C065 describes the behavioral mechanism behind support-turned-resistance in downtrends (Murphy, p. 83), but still depends on that same subjective threshold. R029 offers a partial solution: speedlines provide a rule-based, geometric framework for confirming role reversals — a break of the 2/3 speedline followed by a rally that fails at that line constitutes an objective, non-subjective confirmation that the level has truly reversed roles.

## Trading Implication

When applying the role-reversal entry rule from C065, traders should require that the violated support level also aligns with or is confirmed by a broken speedline acting as resistance (per R029), replacing subjective 'significance' judgment with a geometric threshold derived from the prior trend's slope.
