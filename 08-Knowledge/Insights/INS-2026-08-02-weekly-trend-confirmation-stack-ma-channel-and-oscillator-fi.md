# Weekly Trend Confirmation Stack: MA, Channel, and Oscillator Filters

## Discovery Summary

R142 establishes that MACD and Stochastics daily crossovers should only be acted upon when aligned with the weekly trend. N044 provides a concrete weekly trend definition via 10/40-week MAs, where price above the 40-week MA signals bull trend. C130 adds a complementary trend confirmation tool — the weekly price channel breakout — that research shows produces superior results. Together, these three notes form a layered weekly trend confirmation stack: a trader can require weekly MA alignment (N044), weekly channel position (C130), and weekly oscillator direction (R142) before acting on any daily MACD or Stochastics crossover signal.

## Trading Implication

Before entering on a daily MACD or Stochastics crossover, confirm: (1) price is above the 40-week MA, (2) price is within or breaking out of a bullish weekly price channel, and (3) the weekly MACD/Stochastics is itself in a bullish posture — only then act on the daily signal.
