# 50% Retracement as Ideal Pullback Entry Zone

## Discovery Summary

The rule EN068-4060 explicitly states that 40-60% retracements in an uptrend provide excellent long entries, while the Fibonacci note N085 highlights 50% as a key level (and notes 38-62% as typical bounds). The Dow concept C050 also places common corrections near 50% (one-third to two-thirds range). Together, these three sources converge on 50% as the optimal entry point for pullbacks in a trending market.

## Trading Implication

Place limit orders for long entries at the 50% retracement level of the prior advance in an uptrend, using 38% and 62% as invalidation or adjustment thresholds. For shorts, mirror this on bounces.
