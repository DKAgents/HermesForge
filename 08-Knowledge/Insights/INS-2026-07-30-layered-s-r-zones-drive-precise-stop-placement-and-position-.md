# Layered S/R Zones Drive Precise Stop Placement and Position Sizing

## Discovery Summary

C065 and EN069 identify two distinct technical mechanisms that generate resistance levels in downtrends: violated support reversals (Murphy p.83) and unfilled price gaps. RG035 then mandates that stops must be placed at valid technical levels above resistance for shorts, with position size scaled inversely to stop distance. The non-obvious connection is that combining both resistance sources — a gap's lower end AND a prior violated support — creates a confluent resistance zone, which simultaneously justifies a tighter stop and therefore permits a larger position size under the 5% max risk rule.

## Trading Implication

In a downtrend, when a price gap's lower end and a prior violated support level coincide, sell the rally into that confluence zone with a stop just above it — the tighter, technically-validated stop allows a proportionally larger position under the 10% commitment / 5% risk framework from RG035.
