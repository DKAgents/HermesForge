# Three-Layer Weekly Filter System for Daily Trade Entries

## Discovery Summary

R142 establishes that MACD and Stochastics daily crossover signals must be filtered by weekly chart direction first. N044 provides a concrete weekly trend definition: price above the 40-week MA signals bull trend, below signals bear trend. C130 adds a third weekly filter layer via price channel breakouts, which research shows produce superior results. Together these three notes define a complete weekly trend confirmation stack before any daily signal is acted upon.

## Trading Implication

A trader should require at least two of three weekly confirmations (MACD/Stochastics weekly crossover direction, price above/below 40-week MA, and weekly channel breakout status) before taking a daily MACD or Stochastics crossover signal in that direction — never trade daily crossovers against the composite weekly trend.
