# Volume confirms flag/pennant breakout validity

## Discovery Summary

N043 specifies that flags and pennants should pause on very light volume then resume on a burst of trading activity. R082 and N013 together establish that valid breakouts require heavy volume, while light-volume breakouts often signal false moves. This creates a specific confirmation filter: the volume surge at a flag/pennant breakout is not just descriptive—it is a necessary condition for entry validity.

## Trading Implication

Only enter a flag or pennant breakout if volume expands clearly above the pattern's formation levels; treat a breakout on mediocre or declining volume as a no-trade or fade signal.
