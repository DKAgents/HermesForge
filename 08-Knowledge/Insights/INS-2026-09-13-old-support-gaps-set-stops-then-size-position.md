# Old Support/Gaps Set Stops, Then Size Position

## Discovery Summary

C065 establishes that violated support becomes resistance in a downtrend, and EN069 says bear-move rallies into gap resistance can be sold with stops above the gap. RG035 then requires stops at valid technical levels and ties position size to stop distance. The sequence is: use prior support-turned-resistance or the lower edge of a gap as the short entry/resistance zone, place the protective stop above it, and let that stop distance determine share size under the max-risk rule.

## Trading Implication

In a downtrend, short rallies into prior support turned resistance or into the lower end of a price gap, place the stop just above that level, and size the position as risk budget divided by stop distance.
