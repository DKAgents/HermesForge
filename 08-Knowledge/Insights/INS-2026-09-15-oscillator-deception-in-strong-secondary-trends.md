# Oscillator Deception in Strong Secondary Trends

## Discovery Summary

The 'Oscillator Entry Strategy in Trending Markets' (EN041) advises buying oversold conditions in an uptrend, but 'Secondary Trends' (C366) reveals that counter-trend moves can persist for weeks to months. The McClellan Oscillator (N186) measures market breadth, which can show false oversold readings during a secondary correction within a primary uptrend, leading to premature entries. The edge condition that oscillators lie in strong trends is resolved by requiring confirmation that the secondary trend has exhausted before acting on oscillator signals.

## Trading Implication

Do not take an oversold buy signal in a primary uptrend until breadth indicators like the McClellan Oscillator show a clear divergence from price or the secondary trend has exceeded its typical duration, avoiding false entries during ongoing corrections.
