# S/R Stop Distance Determines Position Size

## Discovery Summary

In a downtrend, former support acts as resistance (C065). A rally to that resistance provides a short entry, with a stop above the level (EN069). The stop distance then feeds into the money management rule in RG035, where the stop must be at a valid technical level and the position size is adjusted based on that distance to respect the 5% risk limit.

## Trading Implication

When shorting into a resistance level that was prior support, place the protective stop just above that level and calculate position size using the stop distance and your maximum dollar risk per trade.
