# Volume Divergence in Flags Signals Stop Tightening Opportunity

## Discovery Summary

N043 establishes that flags and pennants form on 'very light volume' during consolidation, while R082 and N013 both require heavy volume at breakout for signal validity. Together, these three notes create a two-stage volume decision rule: if volume fails to surge at the flag/pennant breakout, the breakout is likely false per N013, and the position should be treated as a potential bull trap. The seed question about volume divergence maps directly onto this: when price resumes trend but volume does NOT confirm (stays light at the breakout of the continuation pattern), stops should be tightened rather than trailed normally.

## Trading Implication

When trading flag or pennant breakouts, if volume does not surge above consolidation-phase levels at the breakout candle, tighten stops to just below the breakout level immediately rather than using standard trailing stops — the light-volume breakout is a high-probability false signal per N013.
