# Commodity Crossover as Leading Signal for Exporters

## Discovery Summary

The double crossover method using 10/50 moving averages, typically applied to stock indices, can be applied to commodity prices to anticipate moves in commodity-exporting nations' equity markets. As noted in E040, plunging commodities damage countries like Australia, Canada, Mexico, and Russia. Therefore, a bearish crossover in a key commodity index could serve as a leading filter to avoid or short positions in those countries' stock ETFs, while a bullish crossover could signal entry opportunities.

## Trading Implication

A trader should monitor the 10/50 MA crossover on a broad commodity index as a timing filter for long or short positions in equity markets of commodity-exporting countries, using the crossover to confirm the direction of commodity trends before acting on country-specific stock signals.
