# Filter Fibonacci with 40-60% zone

## Discovery Summary

The Dow Theory concept (C050) defines secondary trend retracements anywhere from 33–66%, while Fibonacci retracements (N085) identify specific levels at 38%, 50%, and 62%. Murphy's rule (EN068-4060) narrows the ideal entry window to the 40–60% retracement zone, effectively filtering out the 38% and 62% Fibonacci levels as less reliable for timing entries during pullbacks. The 50% level sits at the center of this 40–60% zone, offering a precise target within the broader retracement range.

## Trading Implication

Overlay Fibonacci retracements on the prior advance and only take long entries when price pulls back into the 40–60% zone—preferably near the 50% level—while ignoring shallow 38% retracements in strong trends and deeper 62% retracements that suggest trend weakness.
