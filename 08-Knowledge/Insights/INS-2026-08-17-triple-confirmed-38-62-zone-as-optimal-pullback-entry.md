# Triple-Confirmed 38-62% Zone as Optimal Pullback Entry

## Discovery Summary

Three independent frameworks converge on the same retracement zone: Dow Theory (C050) defines secondary corrections as retracing one-third to two-thirds (33-67%) of prior moves; Murphy's entry rule (EN068) specifies 40-60% pullbacks as optimal long entries; Fibonacci levels (N085) place key support at 38%, 50%, and 62%. The overlap between all three frameworks is the 40-62% zone, with 50% as the single level confirmed by all three simultaneously. A pullback reaching exactly 50% satisfies Dow's correction range, Murphy's entry rule, and Fibonacci's most universal level.

## Trading Implication

In an uptrend, prioritize long entries when price retraces to 50% of the prior advance — this level is confirmed by Dow Theory, Murphy's 40-60% rule, and Fibonacci simultaneously; treat 38% and 62% as the outer boundaries of the high-probability entry window, with 62% also serving as the maximum retracement before trend integrity is questioned.
