# Using Speedlines to Objectify Support/Break Significance

## Discovery Summary

E005 notes the inherent subjectivity in defining a 'significant' penetration when support becomes resistance. R029 describes speed resistance lines, which reverse roles after a break with a specific, measured level. By applying the role-reversal principle from C065 (previous support becoming resistance) to speedlines, the broken line itself provides an objective benchmark for 'significant'—if price fails to reclaim it, the break is confirmed, resolving the ambiguity.

## Trading Implication

In a downtrend, draw the 2/3 speedline; once broken, wait for a rally back to that line. Treat a failure to close above it as a precise short entry trigger, eliminating guesswork about whether a prior support break was 'significant'.
