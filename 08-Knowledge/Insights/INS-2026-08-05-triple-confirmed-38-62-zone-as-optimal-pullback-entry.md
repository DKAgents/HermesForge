# Triple-Confirmed 38-62% Zone as Optimal Pullback Entry

## Discovery Summary

Three independent frameworks converge on the same retracement zone: Dow Theory (C050) establishes 1/3 to 2/3 retracement as the secondary trend correction range; Murphy's entry rule (EN068) specifies 40-60% pullbacks as optimal entry zones; and Fibonacci levels (N085) identify 38%, 50%, and 62% as key support levels. The overlap between all three — approximately 38-62% — creates a high-confidence entry window validated by classical Dow Theory, practitioner trading rules, and mathematical Fibonacci ratios simultaneously.

## Trading Implication

In an uptrend, only enter long on pullbacks when price reaches the 38-62% retracement zone of the prior advance, as this is the only zone confirmed by all three frameworks; pullbacks shallower than 38% or deeper than 62% fall outside at least one validation system and should be treated with lower confidence or avoided.
