# Earnings Filter Resolves Role-Reversal Subjectivity Across Multiple Contexts

## Discovery Summary

E005 identifies that 'significant penetration' for role-reversal is inherently subjective (Murphy, p. 83), which creates entry ambiguity. C065 and R029 both describe role-reversal mechanics in downtrends and speedlines respectively — all three share the same subjectivity weakness at the point of acting on the reversal. The seed question introduces an earnings calendar binary filter that, when appended to role-reversal entry criteria, partially resolves the subjectivity problem by eliminating one class of false penetrations: those driven by pre-earnings volatility rather than genuine structural breaks. A 7-day earnings exclusion window provides a concrete, objective threshold that substitutes for the absent 'significance' benchmark Murphy acknowledges is lacking.

## Trading Implication

Before entering any role-reversal trade — whether at a violated support-turned-resistance level (C065) or a broken speedline (R029) — check if earnings fall within 7 days; if so, skip the trade entirely, as the penetration cannot be confirmed as structurally significant versus earnings-noise-driven.
