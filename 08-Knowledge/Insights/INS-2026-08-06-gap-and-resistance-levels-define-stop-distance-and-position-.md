# Gap and Resistance Levels Define Stop Distance and Position Size

## Discovery Summary

C065 establishes that violated support becomes resistance in downtrends, and EN069 adds a more precise entry mechanism — sell a rally into the lower end of a gap acting as resistance. RG035 then completes the chain: once the technical stop level is identified (above the gap/resistance per EN069 and C065), position size is calculated by dividing max dollar risk (5% of account) by the distance to that stop. Together, these three notes create a full sequence: identify resistance (gap or prior support), place stop above it, then derive position size from the stop distance.

## Trading Implication

In a downtrend, when price rallies into a prior violated support or the lower end of a gap, sell short with a stop placed just above that resistance level, then calculate position size so total risk does not exceed 5% of account equity on the trade.
