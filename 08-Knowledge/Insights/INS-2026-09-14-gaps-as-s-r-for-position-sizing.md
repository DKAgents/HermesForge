# Gaps as S/R for Position Sizing

## Discovery Summary

C065 explains that previous support becomes resistance in downtrends, while EN069 specifies that price gaps act as support or resistance for timing entries. RG035 combines technical stop levels with money management: stop distance (set at valid S/R) determines position size. Together, they show that a gap in a downtrend (gap acting as resistance) can be used as the exact technical level for a protective stop (above the gap), which then directly drives position size via the 5% risk rule — creating a precise, actionable framework for sizing short trades.

## Trading Implication

In a downtrend, place a short entry on a rally to a price gap that was previously support (now resistance), set the stop just above the gap's upper end, and size the position so that the stop distance accounts for no more than 5% of account equity at risk.
