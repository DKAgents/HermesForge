# Volume Divergence at Midpoint Flags Signals Stop Adjustment Zones

## Discovery Summary

Flags and pennants (N043) occur at approximately the midpoint of a market move, which aligns with the 50% secondary trend retracement range described in C050. R082 mandates that breakouts from consolidation patterns require heavy volume to be valid. When volume diverges from price during the flag/pennant formation — meaning volume does not surge on the breakout as required by R082 — the trader has a signal that the expected continuation to the second half of the move may not materialize, warranting tighter stop placement near the original flagpole base rather than the typical post-breakout stop.

## Trading Implication

When a flag or pennant breakout occurs on below-average volume (violating R082), tighten stops to just below the consolidation low rather than the standard post-breakout placement, since the midpoint thesis (N043) and retracement boundaries (C050) suggest elevated failure risk in the second leg.
