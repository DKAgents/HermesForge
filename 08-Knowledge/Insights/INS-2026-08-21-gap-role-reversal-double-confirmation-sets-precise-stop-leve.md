# Gap + Role-Reversal Double Confirmation Sets Precise Stop Levels

## Discovery Summary

C065 establishes that violated support becomes resistance in a downtrend, while EN069 specifies that gaps also act as support/resistance levels with precise entry zones (upper/lower end of the gap). When a prior support level that has flipped to resistance also coincides with an unfilled gap, the two mechanisms reinforce each other, creating a high-confidence resistance zone with a clearly defined stop placement rule (above the gap per EN069). This layering of Murphy's role-reversal principle with his gap-timing rule produces a tighter, more defensible stop distance than either rule alone.

## Trading Implication

In a downtrend, when a rally approaches both a former support level (now resistance per C065) AND the lower end of an overhead gap, initiate or add to the short position at that confluence zone, placing the protective stop just above the gap — the dual confirmation narrows the stop distance and improves the reward-to-risk ratio for position sizing.
