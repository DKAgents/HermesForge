# Weekly Price Channel Filters Daily MACD/Stochastics

## Discovery Summary

Murphy advises using weekly signals as trend filters for MACD and Stochastics crossover systems (R142) to avoid trading against the primary trend. Independently, the weekly rule price channel is described as a simpler, research-backed superior alternative to moving averages for trend following (C130). By combining these, the weekly price channel breakout direction can replace the weekly MACD/Stochastics indicator reading as the trend filter for daily MACD/Stochastics entries, potentially improving robustness and simplicity.

## Trading Implication

Define the weekly trend with a price channel (e.g., 4-week high-low breakout) and restrict daily MACD or Stochastics crossover signals to trades aligned with that channel direction.
