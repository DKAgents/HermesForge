# 50% Retracement: Murphy's Ideal Entry Point

## Discovery Summary

The Dow theory concept (C050) states secondary retracements commonly reach 50%. Murphy's rule (EN068-4060) identifies 40-60% retracements as ideal entry zones. Fibonacci retracements (N085) include 50% as a key level. Together, they converge on the 50% retracement as a specific high-probability entry point within the broader zone.

## Trading Implication

When entering pullbacks in uptrends, target the 50% Fibonacci retracement level for precise entries, as it is validated by Dow, Murphy, and Fibonacci frameworks. Adjust expectations based on trend strength (e.g., 38% in strong trends) to avoid missing entries.
