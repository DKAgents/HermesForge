# Weekly Channel Breakout as MACD/Stochastic Trend Filter

## Discovery Summary

R142 establishes that MACD and Stochastics daily signals should be filtered by weekly chart trend direction. C130 identifies the weekly price channel (weekly rule) as a superior, simpler trend-following alternative to moving averages. N044 provides the moving average framework (10/40 week) traditionally used for primary trend. Combining these: the weekly rule from C130 can replace or complement the weekly trend assessment required by R142, providing a binary breakout/breakdown filter for daily MACD/Stochastic entries.

## Trading Implication

Before taking a daily MACD or Stochastic crossover signal, confirm the weekly price channel direction (C130 breakout = uptrend, breakdown = downtrend) and only act on daily signals aligned with that channel direction. If weekly channel is neutral/flat, defer to N044's 40-week moving average as tiebreaker.
