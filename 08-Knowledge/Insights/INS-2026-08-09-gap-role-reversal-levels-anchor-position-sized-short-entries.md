# Gap + Role-Reversal Levels Anchor Position-Sized Short Entries

## Discovery Summary

C065 establishes that violated support becomes resistance on bounces in a downtrend (Murphy p.83). EN069 adds that gaps in a bear move also act as resistance, with a tactical entry rule: sell a rally to the lower end of the gap with a stop above it. RG035 then requires that this technically-derived stop distance (above the gap or role-reversal resistance) must also satisfy money management constraints — a 5% max risk on total capital limits position size. Together, the three notes form a sequential decision chain: identify the resistance level (role-reversal or gap), place the stop above it, then back-calculate position size from the stop distance.

## Trading Implication

In a downtrend, when price rallies into a prior support-turned-resistance level that coincides with a gap's lower end, sell there with a stop just above the gap, then size the position so the stop distance represents no more than 5% of total capital — do not increase stop distance to allow a larger position.
