# Dow retracement ranges map to Fibonacci entry zones

## Discovery Summary

Dow's secondary trend retracement range of one-third to two-thirds (C050) directly overlaps with Fibonacci retracement levels of 38%, 50%, and 62% (N085). Murphy's 40-60% retracement zone for timing entries (EN068) sits entirely within Dow's stated range, with the 50% level being both the most common Dow retracement and a core Fibonacci ratio. This multi-framework convergence around the 50% retracement zone creates a high-confidence entry area validated by both classical Dow Theory and modern technical analysis.

## Trading Implication

When entering on pullbacks in an uptrend, prioritize the 50% retracement level as it represents the convergence of Dow's most common retracement, Fibonacci's 50% ratio, and the center of Murphy's 40-60% optimal entry zone.
