# Triple Convergence: Dow, Murphy, Fibonacci Define 38-62% Entry Zone

## Discovery Summary

Three independent frameworks converge on the same retracement zone: Dow Theory (C050) identifies one-third to two-thirds (33-67%) as the secondary correction range; Murphy's entry rule (EN068) specifies 40-60% pullbacks as ideal entry points; and Fibonacci retracements (N085) place key levels at 38%, 50%, and 62%. The overlap of all three — approximately 38-62% — creates a high-confidence entry zone validated by Dow, Murphy, and Fibonacci simultaneously. Notably, N085 adds a trend-strength filter: strong trends should hold the 38% level, while only weaker trends reach 62%.

## Trading Implication

In an uptrend, prioritize long entries when price pulls back to 38-50% of the prior advance (confirming a strong trend); treat 62% as a last-opportunity entry and a structural warning level — a breach below two-thirds invalidates the Dow secondary correction framework and suggests primary trend reversal.
