# Oscillator entries require secondary trend confirmation

## Discovery Summary

The oscillator entry strategy (EN041) advises buying oversold in uptrends, which effectively targets secondary corrections (C366). However, in very strong primary trends, oscillators can remain overbought/oversold, causing false signals. Thus, the strategy should only be applied when a genuine secondary trend (lasting weeks to months) is confirmed, not on shallow pullbacks. The McClellan oscillator (N186) is an example of a breadth oscillator susceptible to this pitfall.

## Trading Implication

Before using an oscillator to enter a counter-trend trade, verify that the pullback meets the duration and retracement criteria of a secondary trend to filter out false signals in strongly trending markets.
