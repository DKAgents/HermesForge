# Prior Support/Gap as Resistance Sets Stop & Size

## Discovery Summary

Murphy's principle that prior support becomes resistance in a downtrend (C065) and that price gaps act as resistance levels for bear moves (EN069) define the valid technical levels required by risk rule RG035 for stop placement. RG035 then uses the distance from entry to that technical stop to determine position size via the 5% maximum risk constraint, creating a direct workflow from pattern identification through stop location to position sizing.

## Trading Implication

In a downtrend, sell a rally to the previous support-turned-resistance or to the lower end of a gap, place a protective stop just above that level, and size the position so that the distance to the stop risks no more than 5% of the allocated capital.
