# Volume Divergence at Midpoint Flags Signals Stop Adjustment Zone

## Discovery Summary

Flag and pennant patterns (N043) occur at approximately the midpoint of a market move, meaning the prior flagpole represents roughly one-half of the total expected move. R082 requires that breakout from these patterns be accompanied by heavy volume to be valid. C050 establishes that secondary corrections retrace one-third to two-thirds of the prior move. When volume fails to surge on a flag/pennant breakout as required by R082, and the correction is approaching the two-thirds retracement level per C050, this convergence signals elevated risk — the continuation thesis is weakening and stops should be tightened toward the two-thirds retracement level rather than held at the conventional pattern boundary.

## Trading Implication

If a flag or pennant breakout occurs without the required volume surge (R082 violation), and the prior correction has already retraced 50-66% of the flagpole (C050 danger zone), tighten stops to just below the two-thirds retracement level rather than the standard pattern low, as the continuation setup is structurally compromised.
