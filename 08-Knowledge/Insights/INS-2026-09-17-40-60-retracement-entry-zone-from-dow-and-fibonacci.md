# 40-60% Retracement Entry Zone from Dow and Fibonacci

## Discovery Summary

The Dow Theory's secondary trend retracement range (33-66%, common 50%) and Fibonacci retracement levels (38%, 50%, 62%) are broad. Murphy's rule (EN068) narrows the ideal entry zone to 40-60% retracement, which sits within both the Dow range and the Fibonacci cluster, providing a precise trigger for pullback entries in the direction of the primary trend.

## Trading Implication

In an uptrend, enter long only on pullbacks that retrace 40-60% of the prior advance; in a downtrend, short bounces retracing 40-60%. Use this zone to filter entries instead of the wider 33-66% or Fibonacci levels alone.
