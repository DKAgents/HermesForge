# Use Breadth Oscillator to Confirm Secondary Trend Entries

## Discovery Summary

EN041 establishes that oscillators should be used with the primary trend (buy oversold in uptrends, sell overbought in downtrends), but oscillators lie in strong trends by staying persistently overbought/oversold. C366 defines secondary trends as counter-primary corrections lasting weeks to months — precisely the pullbacks where EN041's 'buy oversold' signal would trigger. N186's McClellan Oscillator adds a market-breadth dimension: because it measures broad advance/decline conditions rather than price momentum alone, it can distinguish genuine secondary-trend exhaustion (broad market oversold) from a strong trend where individual oscillators are merely lagging, reducing false entries on counter-trend trades.

## Trading Implication

Before acting on an individual-security oscillator's oversold signal during a primary uptrend pullback (secondary trend), require confirmation that the McClellan Oscillator also shows broad market oversold conditions, filtering out entries during momentum continuations where price oscillators are unreliable.
