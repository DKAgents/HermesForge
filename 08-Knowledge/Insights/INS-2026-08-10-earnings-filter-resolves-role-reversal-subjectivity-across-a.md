# Earnings Filter Resolves Role-Reversal Subjectivity Across All Trendline Types

## Discovery Summary

E005 acknowledges that 'significant penetration' for role-reversal is inherently subjective (Murphy, p. 83), creating ambiguity at entry. C065 and R029 both depend on this same subjective threshold — whether a support break or speedline break is 'real' enough to trade the reversed role. The seed question introduces a binary, objective earnings calendar filter: if earnings fall within 7 days, the role-reversal entry is skipped entirely. This is non-obvious because it converts an otherwise unresolvable subjectivity problem (how significant is the penetration?) into a conditional one — even if the penetration passes a trader's personal threshold, the earnings proximity voids the trade, eliminating one major source of false role-reversals driven by event volatility rather than structural change.

## Trading Implication

Before acting on any role-reversal signal — whether from a violated support level (C065) or a broken speedline (R029) — check the earnings calendar; if earnings are within 7 days, skip the trade regardless of how convincing the penetration looks, removing event-noise ambiguity from an already subjective rule.
