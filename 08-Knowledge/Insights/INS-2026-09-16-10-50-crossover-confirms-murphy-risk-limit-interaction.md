# 10/50 Crossover Confirms Murphy Risk Limit Interaction

## Discovery Summary

The seed question asks whether Murphy's 10-15% per market limit conflicts with HermesForge 1% position sizing. The notes describe the 10/50-day moving average crossover as a trend-following signal that lags but reduces whipsaws. Since the crossover is known to generate false signals in choppy markets, position sizing rules become critical. Murphy's risk limit is a stop-loss percent, while HermesForge 1% appears to be a position-size rule based on account equity—these are complementary, not conflicting, as the crossover signal would trigger entries that the 1% rule sizes and the 10-15% limit would be a maximum loss cap per trade.

## Trading Implication

Traders should apply the 10/50 crossover for entries/exits, but size positions using the stricter of the two risk rules (likely HermesForge 1% for position size) and set a hard stop at Murphy's 10-15% loss limit to protect against whipsaw damage.
