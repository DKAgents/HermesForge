# Earnings Filter Resolves Subjectivity in Support-Resistance Penetration

## Discovery Summary

The subjectivity in defining 'significant penetration' (E005-subjectivity-in-defining-significant-penetration) weakens the role-reversal signals from C065-previous-support-as-future-resistance-in-downtrend and R029-speed-resistance-lines-role-reversal. The seed note adds a concrete binary filter: checking for earnings within 7 days before acting on any penetration-based reversal signal. This resolves the ambiguity by vetoing signals that might be driven by earnings-related volatility rather than genuine role-reversal dynamics.

## Trading Implication

Before entering any trade based on support-resistance or speedline role-reversal, check the company's earnings calendar. If earnings fall within 7 days, skip the trade entirely, as the penetration may be unreliable and prone to false signals.
