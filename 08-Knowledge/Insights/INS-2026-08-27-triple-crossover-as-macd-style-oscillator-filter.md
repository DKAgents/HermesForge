# Triple Crossover as MACD-style Oscillator Filter

## Discovery Summary

C128 notes that a double crossover can be viewed as an oscillator, with MACD as the prime example. R323 describes the triple crossover method using short, medium, and long averages. N037 specifies the 4-9-18 day variant. By subtracting the 18-day from the 4-day average, you create an oscillator line, and the 9-day can serve as the signal line — essentially converting the triple crossover into a MACD-like setup that adds momentum confirmation to the crossover signals.

## Trading Implication

Take triple crossover buy signals only when the 4-18 spread is above the 9-day signal line and rising; ignore signals when the oscillator is negative or declining to filter whipsaws.
