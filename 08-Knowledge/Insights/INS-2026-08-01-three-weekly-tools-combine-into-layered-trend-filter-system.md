# Three Weekly Tools Combine Into Layered Trend Filter System

## Discovery Summary

R142 establishes that MACD and Stochastics daily crossover signals must be filtered by weekly chart direction. N044 adds that the 10/40-week moving average relationship defines the primary trend on weekly charts, with the 40-week MA acting as bull market support. C130 introduces the weekly price channel as a superior trend-following alternative. Together, these three weekly-based tools create a layered confirmation system: weekly price channel defines the trend structure, 10/40-week MAs confirm the primary trend direction, and only then should MACD/Stochastics daily crossovers be traded in alignment.

## Trading Implication

Before acting on any daily MACD or Stochastics crossover, a trader should require: (1) price above the weekly channel breakout level, (2) 10-week MA above 40-week MA on weekly chart — only then trade daily bullish crossovers; reverse for shorts.
