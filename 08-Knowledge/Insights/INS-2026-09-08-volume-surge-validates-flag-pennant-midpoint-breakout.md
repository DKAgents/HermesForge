# Volume Surge Validates Flag/Pennant Midpoint Breakout

## Discovery Summary

The flag/pennant pattern in N043 requires a 'burst of trading activity' for trend resumption, while rule R082 generalizes that all price pattern breakouts demand heavy volume for validity. Indicator note N013 provides the specific filter: a light-volume breakout from these consolidations signals a likely bull trap, and if that light-volume breakout is followed by a decline on heavy volume, it is a negative combination confirming the pattern's failure. Since N043 notes these patterns form at the 'midpoint of the market move,' the volume validation occurs at a specific, measurable price level, making the entry and invalidation point highly precise.

## Trading Implication

Enter only on a flag/pennant breakout accompanied by a clear volume surge; if the breakout occurs on light volume, immediately set a stop below the pattern or flagpole midpoint, as a subsequent heavy-volume decline would invalidate the setup entirely.
