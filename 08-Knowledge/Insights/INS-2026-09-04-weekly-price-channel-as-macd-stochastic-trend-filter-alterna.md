# Weekly Price Channel as MACD/Stochastic Trend Filter Alternative

## Discovery Summary

Rule R142 specifies using weekly MACD and Stochastics as trend filters for their daily counterparts, while C130 and N044 present the Weekly Rule price channel and long-term moving averages (10/40 week) as proven trend-following devices. The non-obvious connection is that the weekly price channel breakout status can serve as a supplementary or confirmatory trend filter for daily MACD/Stochastic signals, offering a simpler, less computationally intensive alternative to evaluating weekly MACD/Stochastics themselves. When the weekly price channel is bullish (price above channel mid or breaking out upward), it validates long daily MACD/Stochastic crosses; bearish weekly channels confirm short signals.

## Trading Implication

Before taking a daily MACD or Stochastic crossover signal, check whether the weekly price channel (Weekly Rule) is aligned—only go long when price is breaking out to new weekly highs, and only go short when breaking to new weekly lows. This provides a mechanical trend filter that may be easier to enforce objectively than interpreting weekly MACD/Stochastic crossovers.
