# Refine entry retracements with trend strength

## Discovery Summary

C050 (Secondary Trend Retracement Range) says typical retracements are 1/3 to 2/3, common 50%. EN068 (40–60% Retracement Zones) recommends entries in the 40-60% band. N085 (Fibonacci 38%,50%,62%) adds that in strong trends the minimum retracement is usually 38%, and in weak trends the maximum is 62%. This implies the 40-60% zone is a useful approximation, but trend strength should adjust the entry: strong trends may pull back only to ~38% (below the 40% floor of EN068), while weak trends may retrace to ~62% (above the 60% ceiling).

## Trading Implication

When entering on pullbacks in uptrends, assess trend strength: for strong trends, enter near the 38% Fibonacci retracement rather than waiting for 40%; for weaker trends, allow pullbacks to 62% instead of limiting to 60%.
