# Confluent Gap and Prior Support Resistance Tightens Short Stops

## Discovery Summary

C065 shows that in a downtrend, broken support becomes resistance on bounces. EN069 adds that in bear moves, the lower end of price gaps is also resistance, with protective stops above the gap. RG035 states that a closer protective stop at a valid technical resistance allows larger position size within the same risk budget. Together they define a short setup: sell rallies into prior support-turned-resistance or bear-gap resistance and place the stop just above that technical zone.

## Trading Implication

In a downtrend, wait for price to rally into a prior broken support or gap resistance; place a protective stop above that technical level and size the position based on the stop distance under the 5% risk rule.
