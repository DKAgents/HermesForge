# Volume confirms gap breakout validity

## Discovery Summary

The pattern note on gap types and the concept note define breakaway, runaway, and exhaustion gaps but omit volume criteria. Rule R082 states that all price pattern breakouts must be accompanied by heavy volume. Since breakaway and runaway gaps function as breakouts from consolidation or trend continuation, applying the volume rule distinguishes genuine signals from common or exhaustion gaps.

## Trading Implication

Require above-average volume on a gap to trade it as a breakaway or runaway gap; treat low-volume gaps as common gaps or potential exhaustion signals.
