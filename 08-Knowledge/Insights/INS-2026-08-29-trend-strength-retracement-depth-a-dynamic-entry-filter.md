# Trend-Strength & Retracement Depth: A Dynamic Entry Filter

## Discovery Summary

Murphy's 'Secondary Trend Retracement Range' (C050) establishes the general one-third to two-thirds retracement norm, while the 40-60% rule (EN068) refines optimal entry timing. Fibonacci retracements (N085) add a critical condition: the depth of the pullback signals trend strength — shallow 38% retracements indicate strong trends, deeper 62% retracements signal weak trends. This transforms static retracement zones into a dynamic filter where the specific level tested dictates position sizing or entry conviction.

## Trading Implication

Instead of blindly entering on any 40-60% pullback, a trader should favor 38% retracement entries in strong trends with full size, and treat 62% retracement entries with reduced size or tighter stops, as per the Fibonacci-based trend strength signal in N085.
