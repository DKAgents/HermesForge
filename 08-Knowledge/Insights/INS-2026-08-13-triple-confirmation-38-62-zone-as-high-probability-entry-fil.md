# Triple Confirmation: 38-62% Zone as High-Probability Entry Filter

## Discovery Summary

All three notes converge on the same retracement zone but from independent theoretical frameworks: Dow Theory (C050) identifies one-third to two-thirds corrections as secondary trends, Murphy's trading rule (EN068) specifies 40-60% pullbacks as optimal entry zones, and Fibonacci analysis (N085) marks 38%, 50%, and 62% as key retracement levels. The 40-60% entry zone from EN068 is bracketed almost exactly by the Fibonacci 38% and 62% levels from N085, and falls entirely within Dow's one-third to two-thirds secondary correction range from C050. This triple convergence—Dow Theory, Murphy's empirical rule, and Fibonacci mathematics—independently validating the same zone is non-obvious because the three frameworks originate from entirely different analytical traditions.

## Trading Implication

In an uptrend, only enter long positions on pullbacks when price has retraced between 38% and 62% of the prior advance, with the 50% level serving as the highest-conviction entry point where all three frameworks simultaneously agree; treat retracements shallower than 38% or deeper than 62% as outside the high-probability entry window.
