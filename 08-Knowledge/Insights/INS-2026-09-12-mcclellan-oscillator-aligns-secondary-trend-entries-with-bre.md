# McClellan Oscillator aligns secondary trend entries with breadth extremes

## Discovery Summary

C366-secondary-trends describes corrections lasting weeks to months within the primary trend. EN041-oscillator-entry-strategy-in-trending-markets advises buying oversold levels in uptrends and selling overbought levels in downtrends, which corresponds to trading those secondary corrections. N186-mcclellan-oscillator provides a broad-market breadth measure of overbought/oversold extremes. The non-obvious connection is using the McClellan Oscillator's broad-market oversold/overbought readings as a confirming filter for secondary trend extremes, ensuring the counter-trend move is a genuine breadth-driven correction before entering in the primary trend direction.

## Trading Implication

In a confirmed bull market, only execute oversold buy entries from the oscillator strategy when the McClellan Oscillator also reaches oversold territory, indicating a broad-market secondary pullback rather than a minor blip. Conversely, short only overbought readings in bear markets when McClellan Oscillator confirms overbought extremes.
