# Volume confirmation for gap breakouts

## Discovery Summary

Combining 'Price Gaps Types' (N150) with 'Breakouts Must Be Accompanied by Heavy Volume' (R082) reveals that breakaway and runaway gaps, which signal new or continuing trends, require volume confirmation to be valid. Without heavy volume, a gap might be a common gap or false breakout, reducing its predictive power.

## Trading Implication

Only trade breakaway or runaway gaps if accompanied by significantly above-average volume; otherwise treat them as common gaps and avoid trading.
