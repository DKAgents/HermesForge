# Trend Strength Refines Retracement Entry Zones

## Discovery Summary

The Dow-derived secondary trend retracement range (33-66%, most common 50%) from C050 and the Fibonacci retracement levels (38%, 50%, 62%) from N085 bracket Murphy's entry zone (40-60%) from EN068. N085 adds that strong trends see minimum retracements near 38% and weak trends maximum near 62%, allowing the 40-60% zone to be split: enter long on pullbacks to 40-50% in strong trends, and 50-60% in weak trends, rather than treating the zone uniformly.

## Trading Implication

Before entering a pullback, determine trend strength; target 40-50% retracement for strong uptrends and 50-60% for weak uptrends to align entry with the most probable retracement depth.
