# Triple-Source Confluence Narrows Ideal Pullback Entry Zone

## Discovery Summary

Three independent frameworks converge on a specific retracement zone: Dow Theory (C050) defines secondary corrections as retracing 1/3 to 2/3 of prior moves; Murphy's entry rule (EN068) targets the 40–60% pullback zone for long entries; and Fibonacci levels (N085) identify 38%, 50%, and 62% as key retracement anchors. The 50% level appears in all three frameworks simultaneously, while the 38–62% Fibonacci band nearly perfectly brackets the 40–60% entry rule, which itself sits within Dow's 1/3 to 2/3 corrective range. This triple convergence is not obvious because the three notes come from different analytical traditions (Dow Theory, practical entry rules, and Fibonacci mathematics).

## Trading Implication

A trader should prioritize long entries specifically when price pulls back to the 50% retracement level within a confirmed uptrend, treating 38–50% as the high-probability entry zone in strong trends (per N085's 'minimum retracement in strong trends') and 50–62% as valid but signaling trend weakness; entries outside this band lack multi-framework support.
