# ADX-Filtered Keltner Breakouts at 50% Retracements

## Discovery Summary

E036 establishes that rising ADX favors moving-average-based indicators, making Keltner Channels (N190) more reliable in trending markets. C050 notes that secondary trends typically retrace 33–66%, most commonly 50%, of the prior move. Combining these: when ADX is rising, a pullback to the 50% retracement level, often coinciding with the Keltner midline, provides a high-probability entry point upon a breakout beyond the channel in the trend direction.

## Trading Implication

First confirm ADX > 25 and rising, then after a secondary pullback to roughly 50% retracement within the Keltner Channels, enter only on a breakout above (or below) the band aligned with the primary trend.
