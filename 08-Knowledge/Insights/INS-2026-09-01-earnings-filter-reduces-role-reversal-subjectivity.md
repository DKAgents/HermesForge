# Earnings Filter Reduces Role Reversal Subjectivity

## Discovery Summary

The inherent subjectivity in defining a 'significant penetration' for support/resistance role reversal (E005) can be mitigated by adding a mandatory binary filter: check for earnings within 7 days before entering a role-reversal trade. The role reversal principles described in C065 (previous support becomes resistance in downtrend) and R029 (speedlines reverse roles) still apply, but using the earnings filter avoids periods where price volatility can create misleading penetrations, making the significance determination more objective.

## Trading Implication

Before executing any trade based on a support/resistance or speedline role reversal, check the earnings calendar. If the company has earnings in the next 7 days, skip the trade to avoid false signals from erratic price action.
