# Volume & Price Filters to Avoid Bull Traps

## Discovery Summary

Volume as a Filter for False Breakouts (N013) and Filters for Confirming Breakouts (R052) together provide a more robust method to identify Bull Traps (N028). A valid upside breakout requires both heavy volume (N013) and a price close beyond resistance (e.g., 1% or two-day penetration per R052); a light-volume breakout that later reverses on heavy volume signals a false breakout.

## Trading Implication

Enter breakouts only when heavy volume accompanies a price close above resistance using R052 filters; ignore or set tighter stops on light-volume breakouts to avoid bull traps.
