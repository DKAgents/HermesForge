# Fibonacci + Dow + Murphy Converge on 38–62% Entry Zone

## Discovery Summary

Three independent frameworks — Dow Theory (C050), Murphy's trading rule (EN068), and Fibonacci retracements (N085) — all converge on the 38–62% retracement range as the high-probability entry zone in a trending market. Dow identifies 1/3 to 2/3 as the secondary correction range; Murphy's rule EN068 specifically narrows this to 40–60% for actionable entries; Fibonacci retracements at 38%, 50%, and 62% provide discrete price levels within that same band. The non-obvious insight is that these three systems were derived independently yet map almost exactly onto each other, which functionally creates a triple-confirmation framework for any pullback entry.

## Trading Implication

In an uptrend, wait for a pullback to reach the 38–62% retracement zone and look for a confluence of a Fibonacci level (38%, 50%, or 62%) aligning with the 40–60% Murphy rule before entering long; use the 62–66% level as a hard invalidation threshold per both Dow's two-thirds limit and Fibonacci's maximum retracement in a weak trend.
