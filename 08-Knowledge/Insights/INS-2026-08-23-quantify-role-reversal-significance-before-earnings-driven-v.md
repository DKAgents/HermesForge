# Quantify Role-Reversal Significance Before Earnings-Driven Volatility

## Discovery Summary

E005 establishes that 'significant penetration' of support/resistance is inherently subjective (Murphy, p. 83), while C065 and R029 confirm that role-reversal applies across both price levels and speedlines. The seed question introduces a binary earnings calendar filter. The non-obvious connection is that the subjectivity problem in E005 becomes especially dangerous near earnings events: a penetration that appears 'significant' under normal conditions may be rendered ambiguous or false by earnings-driven gaps and volatility spikes, compounding the baseline subjectivity with an event-risk multiplier. Using a 7-day earnings filter as a hard binary gate addresses E005's acknowledged weakness in a specific, actionable context rather than leaving significance thresholds purely discretionary.

## Trading Implication

Before applying any role-reversal entry from C065 or R029, a trader should first check whether earnings fall within 7 days; if so, skip the trade entirely because the subjective 'significance' threshold described in E005 cannot be reliably calibrated against earnings-driven price distortions.
