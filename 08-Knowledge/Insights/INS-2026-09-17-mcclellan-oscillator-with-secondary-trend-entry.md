# McClellan Oscillator with Secondary Trend Entry

## Discovery Summary

The McClellan Oscillator (N186) identifies overbought/oversold conditions in the broad market. EN041 advises buying oversold conditions only in an uptrend, but C366 notes secondary trends are counter-trend moves within a primary trend. This creates a condition: use the McClellan Oscillator's oversold readings during a secondary dip in a primary uptrend to enter with the primary trend, avoiding false signals during strong trend moves.

## Trading Implication

In a primary uptrend confirmed by price or other indicators, buy when the McClellan Oscillator shows an oversold reading during a secondary correction, not when the oscillator is overbought.
