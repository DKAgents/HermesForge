# Weekly channels as alternative MACD/Stochastic trend filter

## Discovery Summary

R142 establishes that weekly trend must be evaluated before acting on daily MACD and Stochastics crossovers to avoid counter-trend trades. C130 provides a concrete, simpler mechanism to determine that weekly trend: the weekly price channel (weekly rule) serves as a trend-following alternative to moving averages with demonstrated superior results. Together, they imply a trader could replace discretionary weekly trend assessment with a mechanical weekly channel breakout filter before executing daily MACD or Stochastics signals.

## Trading Implication

A trader should only act on daily MACD or Stochastic crossover signals when price is above the weekly price channel (long only) or below it (short only), using the weekly rule as a mechanical trend filter instead of subjective interpretation.
