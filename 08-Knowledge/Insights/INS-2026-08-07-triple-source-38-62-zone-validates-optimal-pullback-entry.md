# Triple-Source 38-62% Zone Validates Optimal Pullback Entry

## Discovery Summary

Three independent frameworks converge on the same retracement zone: Dow Theory (C050) identifies 1/3 to 2/3 as the secondary correction range, Murphy's trading rule (EN068) specifies 40-60% as the ideal entry zone in uptrends, and Fibonacci retracements (N085) anchor 38%, 50%, and 62% as key levels. The non-obvious insight is that the 40-60% entry rule from EN068 is not arbitrary — it is simultaneously validated by Dow's classical secondary trend limits and by Fibonacci mathematics, meaning all three systems overlap in the 38-62% band and most tightly in the 50% area.

## Trading Implication

A trader should prioritize long entries on pullbacks only when price retraces into the 38-62% zone of the prior advance, with the 50% level as the highest-conviction entry point; pullbacks shallower than 38% or deeper than 62% should trigger caution or position-size reduction as they fall outside the consensus zone.
