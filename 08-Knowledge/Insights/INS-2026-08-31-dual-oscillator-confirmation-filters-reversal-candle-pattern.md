# Dual-oscillator confirmation filters reversal candle patterns

## Discovery Summary

Note R177 states any oscillator can filter candle patterns when in its overbought/oversold area. Note C149 specifies the best signals occur when both RSI and stochastics are simultaneously overbought or oversold, because RSI is less volatile and reaches extremes less frequently. Note N165 provides RSI's specific overbought (>70) and oversold (<30) levels. The non-obvious synthesis: a reversal candle pattern is most reliable not just when one oscillator is extreme, but when both RSI and stochastics are in their respective extreme zones simultaneously, combining the dual-oscillator confirmation with the single-oscillator pattern filter rule.

## Trading Implication

Before entering a trade on a reversal candle pattern, confirm that RSI (14) is above 70 or below 30 AND stochastics is also in its respective overbought/oversold territory. This dual condition screens out weaker signals and improves trade timing.
