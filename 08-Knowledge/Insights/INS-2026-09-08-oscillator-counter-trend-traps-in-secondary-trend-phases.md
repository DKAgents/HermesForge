# Oscillator counter-trend traps in secondary trend phases

## Discovery Summary

EN041 prescribes buying oversold in uptrends and selling overbought in downtrends, but doesn't specify which trend timeframe. C366 introduces secondary trends — intermediate counter-moves lasting weeks to months. Applying EN041's oscillator entry rules during a secondary counter-trend (e.g., buying an oversold reading within what appears to be a primary uptrend but is actually a secondary downtrend) creates a false signal because the operative trend at the tradeable timeframe is actually bearish.

## Trading Implication

Before executing an oscillator-based entry per EN041, confirm the trend on at least two timeframes to ensure you aren't trading against a secondary trend masquerading as a minor pullback. If an oversold signal occurs but the intermediate trend (per C366) has turned counter to the primary, delay entry until the primary trend resumes or the secondary trend confirms reversal.
