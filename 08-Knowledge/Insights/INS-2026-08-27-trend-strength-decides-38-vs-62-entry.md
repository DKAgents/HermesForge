# Trend Strength Decides 38% vs 62% Entry

## Discovery Summary

Dow Theory's secondary trend retracement range of one-third to two-thirds (C050) provides the macro framework. Murphy's rule (EN068-4060) identifies the 40–60% zone as the ideal entry area. Fibonacci levels (N085) add a condition: strong trends typically see minimum retracements around 38%, while weaker trends extend to the 62% level. This allows traders to refine entries within Murphy's zone based on trend strength.

## Trading Implication

Before a pullback, evaluate trend strength. In a strong uptrend, place long limit orders near the 38% Fibonacci retracement. In a weaker uptrend, target the 50–62% area for entry.
