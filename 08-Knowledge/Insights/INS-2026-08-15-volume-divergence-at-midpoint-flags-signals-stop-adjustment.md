# Volume Divergence at Midpoint Flags Signals Stop Adjustment

## Discovery Summary

N043 establishes that flags and pennants occur at roughly the midpoint of a move and resume on heavy volume bursts. R082 mandates that breakouts require heavy volume to be valid. C050 defines secondary retracements as 1/3 to 2/3 of prior move. When a flag/pennant breakout occurs near the 50% retracement level (C050's most common correction) but WITHOUT the requisite volume surge (R082), the pattern's midpoint assumption in N043 is undermined — suggesting the move may be exhausting rather than continuing, warranting tighter stops rather than trend-following entries.

## Trading Implication

If a flag or pennant breaks out near the 50% retracement level without accompanying heavy volume, treat it as a potential trend exhaustion signal and tighten stops to just below the breakout level rather than projecting a full flagpole extension target.
