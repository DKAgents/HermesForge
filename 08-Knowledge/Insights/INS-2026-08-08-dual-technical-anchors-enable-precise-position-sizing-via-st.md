# Dual Technical Anchors Enable Precise Position Sizing via Stop Distance

## Discovery Summary

C065 and EN069 together provide two distinct technical mechanisms for locating resistance in a downtrend: violated support levels (Murphy p.83) and price gaps acting as resistance on rallies. RG035 then converts these technically-anchored resistance levels into a mechanical position-sizing calculation — the distance from entry to the resistance-based stop determines how large a position can be taken within the 5% risk / 10% commitment constraints. The non-obvious insight is that gaps provide a tighter, more precise resistance anchor than a prior support zone, which may be wide; a tighter gap-based stop therefore permits a larger position under RG035's framework.

## Trading Implication

In a downtrend, prefer entering short at a rally into a gap (EN069) over a rally to a violated support zone (C065) when the gap is narrower — the tighter technical stop increases allowable position size under the 5% risk rule (RG035) without sacrificing validity of the stop level.
