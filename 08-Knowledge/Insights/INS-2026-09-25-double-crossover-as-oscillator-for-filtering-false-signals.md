# Double Crossover as Oscillator for Filtering False Signals

## Discovery Summary

Note C128 explains that the double crossover method (10/50-day MA) can be viewed as an oscillator, similar to MACD. Instead of trading every crossover, a trader could require the oscillator (difference of the two MAs) to exceed a threshold before entering, reducing whipsaws in choppy markets.

## Trading Implication

Before acting on a 10/50 crossover signal, first confirm that the spread between the two moving averages has moved beyond a minimum distance (e.g., 1–2 standard deviations) to avoid false starts.
