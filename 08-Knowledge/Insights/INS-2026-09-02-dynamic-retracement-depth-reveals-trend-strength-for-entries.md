# Dynamic retracement depth reveals trend strength for entries

## Discovery Summary

Murphy's 40-60% retracement rule (EN068) provides a generic entry zone, but Fibonacci levels (N085) add a condition: in strong trends, expect only 38% retracement before resumption; in weaker trends, retracements extend to 62%. Combining these with Dow Theory's one-third to two-thirds secondary retracement range (C050) reveals that the depth of retracement within the 40-60% zone actually diagnoses trend health—a shallow 38% pullback signals trend strength and higher-probability continuation, while a deeper 62% retracement warns of potential trend failure.

## Trading Implication

Before entering on a pullback to the 40-60% zone, measure whether price held at 38% (strong trend—size up or enter aggressively) or sliced through to 62% (weakening trend—reduce size, tighten stops, or wait for confirmation). If retracement exceeds two-thirds (66%), Dow Theory suggests the primary trend may be reversing, not correcting.
