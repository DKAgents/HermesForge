# Support/Resistance Levels Drive Stop Placement and Position Sizing

## Discovery Summary

C065 (previous support as future resistance in downtrend) and EN069 (price gaps as support/resistance) provide concrete technical levels for stop placement. RG035 (Combining Technical Factors with Money Management for Stop Placement) then dictates that protective stops must be placed at these valid technical levels (above resistance for shorts) and that the stop distance determines position size based on a fixed risk limit. This creates a sequential workflow: identify technical resistance from prior support or gaps, sell into that zone, set a stop just above it, and size the position so the stop distance multiplied by position size equals the maximum acceptable loss.

## Trading Implication

In a downtrend, locate previous support levels and gap lower edges as resistance; sell a rally to that level with a stop placed above it, then size the trade so that the stop distance keeps risk within your money management limit.
