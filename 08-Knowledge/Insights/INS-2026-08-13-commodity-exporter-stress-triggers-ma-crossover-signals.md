# Commodity Exporter Stress Triggers MA Crossover Signals

## Discovery Summary

E040 identifies commodity-exporting nations (Australia, Canada, Mexico, Russia) as particularly vulnerable to commodity price plunges, creating predictable equity market stress. EN028 and N039 define a specific, mechanical entry/exit rule (10-day crossing below 50-day = sell) for stocks. The intermarket chain from the seed question means a commodity plunge serves as an early warning that these nations' equity markets will likely trigger bearish MA crossover signals — giving the trader lead time to prepare short or exit positions before the crossover confirms.

## Trading Implication

When commodity prices show a sustained plunge, pre-screen equity ETFs or indices of Australia (EWA), Canada (EWC), Mexico (EWW), and Russia for proximity to a 10/50-day bearish crossover, and treat any such crossover as a high-conviction sell signal rather than a routine one.
