# Commodity trend filters for 10/50 crossover on exporter stocks

## Discovery Summary

The 10/50-day moving average crossover from N039 and EN028 generates buy/sell signals for stocks. When applied to equity indices of commodity-exporting countries (Australia, Canada, etc.), E040 notes that plunging commodity prices are especially damaging. Combining the crossover signal with a declining commodity price trend adds a confirmatory filter—traders can avoid bullish crossovers in commodity-exporter stocks when commodities are in a downtrend, or seek bearish crossovers for higher conviction short entries.

## Trading Implication

Before trading a 10/50 crossover signal on stocks or ETFs from commodity-exporting nations, confirm the direction of the commodity price trend. Favor bearish crossovers (sell signals) when commodity prices are falling, and be cautious with bullish crossovers unless commodity prices are stable or rising.
