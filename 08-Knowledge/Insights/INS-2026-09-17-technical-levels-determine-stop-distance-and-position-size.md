# Technical Levels Determine Stop Distance and Position Size

## Discovery Summary

C065 (previous support as resistance in downtrend) and EN069 (price gaps as support/resistance) define technical levels for stop placement. RG035 (combining technical factors with money management) mandates stops at valid technical levels and provides a 5% maximum risk rule. Together, they create a sequence: identify a support/resistance level from C065 or EN069, set a stop beyond it, then use the 5% risk and 10% commitment limits to compute position size.

## Trading Implication

For a long entry in a downtrend, place a stop below the prior support-turned-resistance or a gap; then calculate position size so that the stop distance times shares equals 5% of account equity.
