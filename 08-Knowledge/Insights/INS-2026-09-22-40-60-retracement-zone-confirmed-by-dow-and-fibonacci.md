# 40-60% Retracement Zone Confirmed by Dow and Fibonacci

## Discovery Summary

Dow theory's typical 50% retracement (from C050-secondary-trend-retracement-range) falls within Murphy's 40-60% entry zone (from EN068-4060-retracement-zones-for-timing-entries) and matches the key Fibonacci level of 50% (from N085-fibonacci-percentage-retracements-38-50-62). This convergence supports using the 40-60% retracement range as a reliable entry zone for pullbacks in trends.

## Trading Implication

Enter long on pullbacks retracing 40-60% of the prior advance in an uptrend, prioritizing the 50% level as the ideal entry point; apply the same logic inversely for short entries in downtrends.
