# Volume Divergence Exposes False Breakouts Near Retracement Zones

## Discovery Summary

R082 establishes that breakouts require heavy volume to be valid, while N043 specifies that flags and pennants form on very light volume before resuming on a burst of activity. C050 notes that secondary corrections retrace one-third to two-thirds of the prior move. When a flag/pennant breakout occurs near the 50% retracement level (C050) but lacks the required volume surge (R082), the probability of a failed continuation is elevated — the flagpole midpoint characteristic (N043 point 4) coinciding with the Dow retracement zone creates a testable confluence zone where volume confirmation becomes especially critical.

## Trading Implication

When a flag or pennant breaks out near a 33-67% retracement of the prior swing, require explicit volume expansion above the consolidation average before entering; if volume fails to surge at that breakout, tighten the initial stop to the near side of the consolidation range rather than the far side.
