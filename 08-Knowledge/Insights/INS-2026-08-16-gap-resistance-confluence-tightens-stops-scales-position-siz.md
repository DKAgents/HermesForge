# Gap+Resistance Confluence Tightens Stops, Scales Position Size

## Discovery Summary

C065 establishes that violated support becomes resistance on bounces in a downtrend. EN069 specifies that in a bear move, gaps also act as resistance ceilings — sell a rally to the lower end of the gap with a stop above the gap. When a prior support level (C065) and a gap resistance zone (EN069) coincide on a bounce, the technical resistance is doubly confirmed, allowing a tighter, more defensible stop just above both levels. RG035 then prescribes that a tighter stop permits a larger position size within the 5% risk constraint on a $10,000 commitment.

## Trading Implication

In a downtrend, when a rally reaches a level that is simultaneously a prior violated support AND the lower end of a price gap, enter short with a stop just above that confluence zone — the tighter stop justified by dual technical confirmation allows a proportionally larger position size under RG035's money management framework.
