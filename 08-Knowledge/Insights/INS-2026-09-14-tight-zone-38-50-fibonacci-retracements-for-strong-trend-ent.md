# Tight Zone: 38-50% Fibonacci Retracements for Strong Trend Entries

## Discovery Summary

The Fibonacci retracement note (N085) specifies that strong trends retrace a minimum of 38% and maximum of 62%, while the rule EN068-4060 suggests 40-60% for entries. The secondary trend retracement range (C050) echoes this with one-third to two-thirds (33-66%) but notes the most common 50%. Together, they confirm that the 'sweet spot' for high-probability entries, especially in strong trends, narrows to the 38-50% zone (Fibonacci-derived), not the broader 40-60% or 33-66% ranges. This convergence creates a specific actionable filter: in an uptrend, wait for a pullback to 38-50% (especially near 50%) before buying, avoiding the 62% level which signals trend weakness.

## Trading Implication

Use Fibonacci retracement levels to time entries only in the 38-50% range of the prior advance, with 50% as the ideal entry point. Avoid entries at retracements beyond 62%, as they indicate the trend may be failing.
