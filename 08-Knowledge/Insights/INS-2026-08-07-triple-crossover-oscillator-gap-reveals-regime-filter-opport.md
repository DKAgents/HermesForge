# Triple Crossover Oscillator Gap Reveals Regime Filter Opportunity

## Discovery Summary

C128 establishes that moving average differences can be viewed as oscillators, while N037 and R323 describe the triple crossover method (4-9-18 day). The non-obvious connection is that the spread between the 4-9 pair and the 9-18 pair in the triple crossover system can itself be treated as a dual oscillator, where the magnitude of each spread reveals whether the market is trending or ranging. When both oscillator spreads are narrow and oscillating around zero, the market is ranging and triple crossover signals (R323) will generate excessive whipsaws.

## Trading Implication

A trader using the 4-9-18 triple crossover (N037) should calculate both the 4-minus-9 and 9-minus-18 spreads as oscillators; only take crossover signals when at least one spread is expanding directionally, suppressing trades when both spreads are compressed and oscillating, indicating a ranging regime.
