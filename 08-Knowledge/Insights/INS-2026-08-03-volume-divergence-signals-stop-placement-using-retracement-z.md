# Volume Divergence Signals Stop Placement Using Retracement Zones

## Discovery Summary

Flags and pennants (N043) require both light volume during consolidation AND a volume surge at breakout (R082) to be valid continuation signals. When volume diverges from this expected pattern — e.g., breakout occurs without heavy volume — the pattern's midpoint characteristic (N043 point 4) combined with Dow's secondary retracement range of 33-67% (C050) provides a structural framework for tightening stops. If a breakout lacks confirming volume per R082, the flag/pennant midpoint implies price has already traveled ~50% of the move, meaning a failed breakout could retrace the entire second half, warranting stops placed at the 50% retracement of the flagpole rather than just below the consolidation low.

## Trading Implication

When a flag or pennant breakout occurs on below-average volume (violating R082), tighten stops to the 50% retracement level of the flagpole rather than the standard consolidation boundary, since the midpoint characteristic means failure risk is amplified and potential loss is larger than it appears.
