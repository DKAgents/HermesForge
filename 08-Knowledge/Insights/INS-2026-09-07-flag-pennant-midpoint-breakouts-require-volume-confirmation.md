# Flag/Pennant Midpoint Breakouts Require Volume Confirmation

## Discovery Summary

The flag and pennant summary (N043) notes these patterns form at 'about the midpoint of the market move' on 'very light volume' before resuming on a 'burst of trading activity.' The breakout rules (R082) mandate that 'heavier trading activity' validate any breakout signal, while the volume filter note (N013) specifically warns that light-volume upside breakouts are likely bull traps. Together, these imply that a flag or pennant breakout lacking heavy volume invalidates the midpoint measurement objective and signals a likely false continuation.

## Trading Implication

When trading flag/pennant patterns, only enter on a breakout accompanied by a clear volume surge; if the breakout occurs on light volume, treat the pattern as suspect and refrain from projecting the measured move to the midpoint target.
