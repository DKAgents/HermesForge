# Triple Convergence: Dow, Murphy, Fibonacci Define 38-62% Entry Zone

## Discovery Summary

Three independent frameworks — Dow Theory (C050, 1/3 to 2/3 retracement), Murphy's entry rule (EN068, 40-60% pullback zones), and Fibonacci retracements (N085, 38%, 50%, 62%) — all converge on the same 38-62% retracement band. This is non-obvious because the Dow Theory fraction (1/3=33% to 2/3=66%) appears to slightly exceed the others, but Murphy's EN068 specifically narrows the actionable entry window to 40-60%, which sits precisely within the Fibonacci 38-62% range. The convergence of three distinct methodologies on overlapping zones provides probabilistic confirmation that this band represents genuine institutional support/resistance rather than a single system's artifact.

## Trading Implication

In an uptrend, place limit buy orders specifically in the 40-50% retracement zone (where Murphy's rule and the Fibonacci 50% level overlap most densely), and treat 62% as a hard invalidation threshold beyond which trend strength may be deteriorating per N085.
