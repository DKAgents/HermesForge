# Volume Divergence at Midpoint Flags Signals Premature Stop Tightening

## Discovery Summary

Flags and pennants (N043) occur at approximately the midpoint of a market move with characteristically light volume during consolidation — this light volume is EXPECTED and normal, not a divergence warning. R082 states breakouts require heavy volume to be valid. Together, these notes clarify that during the flag/pennant formation phase, volume should be light (do not tighten stops on light consolidation volume), but if the subsequent breakout lacks heavy volume, the trend resumption signal is invalid and stops should be tightened or position exited. C050 adds that if price retraces 50-67% of the flagpole rather than resuming, the pattern has likely failed.

## Trading Implication

During a flag or pennant consolidation, do not tighten stops due to light volume — it is expected behavior; only tighten stops if the breakout from the pattern occurs on weak volume (violating R082) or if price retraces more than two-thirds of the flagpole (per C050's retracement range), signaling pattern failure rather than continuation.
