# Support/Resistance Role-Reversal Defines Stop Distance and Position Size

## Discovery Summary

C065 establishes that prior support, once broken in a downtrend, becomes resistance. RG035 ties stop placement to those technical levels (above resistance for shorts) and links stop distance to position sizing. Together, they create a sequence: identify the role-reversed level, place the stop there per RG035, then calculate position size based on the distance from entry to that level.

## Trading Implication

When shorting a bounce in a downtrend, place the protective stop just above the violated support level now acting as resistance, then adjust position size so the dollar risk from entry to that stop does not exceed the 5% max account risk rule.
