# Weekly Rule Complements MACD/Stochastics Trend Filtering

## Discovery Summary

R142 establishes that weekly MACD/Stochastics signals should be used to filter daily entries, preventing counter-trend trades. N044 adds that long-term moving averages (e.g., 10/40-week) track the primary trend on weekly charts, providing another confirmation method. C130 introduces the weekly price channel (weekly rule) as an alternative trend-following device, which can replace or confirm the weekly moving average filter. Together, a trader can use the weekly rule (price channel) as a concrete, systematic trend filter in place of, or in addition to, weekly MACD/Stochastics, strengthening the weekly trend bias before taking daily signals.

## Trading Implication

Before acting on any daily MACD or Stochastics crossover, confirm the weekly trend using either the weekly price channel (e.g., price above the 4-week high) or the 10/40-week moving average alignment. If the weekly filter conflicts, stand aside or wait for alignment.
