# Dual S/R Structures Define Stop Placement and Position Size

## Discovery Summary

C065 establishes that violated support becomes resistance in downtrends, while EN069 establishes that gaps also act as support/resistance with explicit stop placement rules (stop above/below the gap). Together, these two S/R structures — role-reversed levels and gap boundaries — provide two independent reference points for defining stop distance on the same trade. The seed question connects this directly to position sizing: tighter stops from gap-defined S/R permit larger size, while the ambiguity of a role-reversed level (which may span a wider zone) forces a wider stop and smaller position.

## Trading Implication

In a downtrend, when selling a rally, prefer entries where a price gap's lower end and a prior violated support level converge — the gap provides a precise stop level above the gap, keeping stop distance tight and allowing larger position size than using the role-reversed level alone.
