# ADX-Validated Keltner with Retracement Entries

## Discovery Summary

ADX-Based Indicator Selection (E036) recommends moving averages when ADX is rising (trending market). Keltner Channels (N190) use an EMA and ATR, making them suitable trend-following tools in that regime. Secondary Trend Retracement Range (C050) from Dow theory suggests corrections retrace 1/3 to 2/3, often 50%; combining these, a trader can use ADX to confirm the trend, then enter on pullbacks to the Keltner middle line or after a breakout once the retracement depth aligns with typical correction levels.

## Trading Implication

In trending markets (rising ADX), wait for a secondary retracement to 33-66% of the prior move, then enter on a Keltner channel breakout or bounce from the middle line to improve entry timing and reduce whipsaws.
