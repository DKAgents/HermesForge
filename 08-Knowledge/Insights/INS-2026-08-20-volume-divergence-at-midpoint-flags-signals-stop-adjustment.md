# Volume Divergence at Midpoint Flags Signals Stop Adjustment

## Discovery Summary

Flags and pennants (N043) occur at approximately the midpoint of a move and require a volume burst at breakout to confirm trend resumption (R082). When volume fails to surge at the flag/pennant breakout, this divergence signals the pattern may be failing. The secondary trend retracement range (C050) of 33-67% provides a structural framework: if the flagpole represents roughly half the total move, a failed breakout without volume confirmation suggests a deeper retracement correction may be underway, warranting tighter stops near the 50% retracement of the prior flagpole move.

## Trading Implication

When a flag or pennant breakout occurs on below-average volume (violating R082), tighten stops to the 50% retracement of the flagpole (C050) rather than using the standard post-breakout stop, since the lack of volume confirmation increases the probability of a secondary correction rather than trend continuation.
