# Oscillator Lies in Trending Secondary Corrections

## Discovery Summary

EN041's oscillator entry strategy advises buying oversold in uptrends and selling overbought in downtrends, but C366 reveals that secondary trends lasting weeks to months create counter-primary-trend moves that mimic these exact conditions. An oversold reading during a primary uptrend could actually be a developing secondary downtrend correction, making the signal premature. The McClellan Oscillator (N186) adds breadth context — a weak oversold bounce in breadth during a primary uptrend flags a secondary correction rather than a re-entry point.

## Trading Implication

Before executing EN041's oversold-in-uptrend entry, confirm that the oversold condition isn't the start of a secondary correction by requiring breadth confirmation via the McClellan Oscillator to be reversing from an oversold extreme, not just hitting it.
