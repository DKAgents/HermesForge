# Weekly Price Channel Filters Daily MACD/Stochastics Crossovers

## Discovery Summary

R142 states that weekly signals should act as trend filters for MACD and Stochastics crossover systems before daily signals are taken. C130 offers the weekly price channel as a less time-consuming trend-following alternative to moving averages, while N044 specifies 10- and 40-week moving averages for tracking the primary trend. These notes combine into a concrete multi-timeframe rule: define the weekly trend with a price channel or long-term weekly moving average, then require daily MACD/Stochastics crossovers to align with that trend.

## Trading Implication

A trader should determine the weekly trend using a 40-week moving average or weekly price channel and only take daily MACD or Stochastics crossover signals in that direction.
