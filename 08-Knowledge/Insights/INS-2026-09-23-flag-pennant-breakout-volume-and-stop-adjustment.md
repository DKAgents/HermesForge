# Flag/Pennant Breakout Volume and Stop Adjustment

## Discovery Summary

Flag and pennant patterns (N043) require a volume surge at breakout to confirm validity (R082). Light-volume breakouts are likely false signals (N013). When a flag or pennant breaks out on low volume, volume diverges from the expected heavy volume, indicating a false breakout. Therefore, a trader should adjust stops by placing a stop-loss just below the pattern (or above for downtrend) to protect against the false move, or avoid entering altogether.

## Trading Implication

Upon observing a flag or pennant breakout on low volume, do not enter a new position; if already in a position, tighten stops to just below the flag/pennant consolidation or exit.
