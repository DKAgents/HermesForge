# Earnings Calendar Filter for Role-Reversal Entries

## Discovery Summary

C065-previous-support-as-future-resistance-in-downtrend and R029-speed-resistance-lines-role-reversal rely on the assumption that broken support/resistance levels will hold as new opposite roles, but E005-subjectivity-in-defining-significant-penetration exposes the ambiguity in confirming these breaks. The seed note introduces a concrete, non-price-based binary filter: if earnings are within 7 days, the role-reversal setup is invalidated or deferred. This directly addresses the subjectivity of 'significant penetration' by replacing a discretionary price filter with a time-based earnings risk filter, aligning with known fundamental event risk.

## Trading Implication

Before entering any role-reversal trade (support-to-resistance or speedline break), check the earnings calendar; if earnings are within 7 days, either skip the setup or wait until after the announcement to avoid heightened gap risk. This turns the subjective penetration rule into a more mechanical entry criteria.
