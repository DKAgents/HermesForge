# Volume Divergence at Midpoint Flags Signals Stop Adjustment

## Discovery Summary

Flags and pennants (N043) occur at approximately the midpoint of a move with characteristically light consolidation volume followed by a burst at resumption. R082 requires that breakouts be confirmed by heavy volume to be valid. When a flag/pennant breakout occurs WITHOUT the expected volume surge, this divergence — per R082 — invalidates the signal. C050 adds that the midpoint location (~50% retracement zone) is precisely where secondary corrections end, meaning a failed volume confirmation at this midpoint breakout suggests the move may be a secondary correction rather than a continuation, warranting tighter stops or outright exit.

## Trading Implication

If a flag or pennant breakout lacks the required volume surge (R082), treat the position as suspect at the midpoint level (C050) and tighten stops to just below the consolidation low rather than using a wider trend-based stop.
