# Volume Divergence at Midpoint Flags Reveals Stop Zones

## Discovery Summary

N043 establishes that flags/pennants form at approximately the midpoint of a move on very light volume, while R082 requires heavy volume at breakout for signal validity. C050 adds that secondary corrections retrace 1/3 to 2/3 of the prior move. When a flag/pennant breakout occurs WITHOUT the required volume surge (per R082), the trader should suspect the pattern is not at the midpoint but rather a secondary correction, meaning price may retrace 33-67% of the flagpole — providing a specific stop placement zone below the 33% retracement level of the flagpole.

## Trading Implication

If a flag/pennant breakout lacks confirming volume, treat the flagpole as a potentially complete move rather than a midpoint, and place stops below the one-third retracement of the flagpole rather than below the consolidation low.
