# Volume Confirmation Required for Flag/Pennant Pattern Breakouts

## Discovery Summary

Note N043 specifies that flag and pennant patterns resume trend on 'a burst of trading activity,' while Rule R082 and Note N013 both mandate that valid breakouts require heavy volume to avoid false signals. The connection adds a concrete validation rule: a flag or pennant breakout lacking a volume surge should be treated as suspect per the false breakout filter described in N013, where light-volume breakouts often precede bull traps.

## Trading Implication

When trading a flag or pennant breakout, delay entry until volume expands significantly above the pattern's quiet formation levels; if the breakout occurs on light volume, skip the trade or wait for a heavy-volume pullback that confirms the signal is false before considering counter-entries.
