# Previous support and gaps for stop placement

## Discovery Summary

C065 explains that prior support becomes resistance in a downtrend, and EN069 shows that price gaps act as support/resistance. These technical levels satisfy RG035's requirement that protective stops be placed at valid technical levels. By using these levels, a trader can set stops precisely and then size positions according to money management rules.

## Trading Implication

In a downtrend, sell into the prior support level (now resistance) or the lower end of a gap, placing a stop just above; for an uptrend, buy a dip to a gap's upper end and stop below. Compute stop distance to determine position size based on max risk.
