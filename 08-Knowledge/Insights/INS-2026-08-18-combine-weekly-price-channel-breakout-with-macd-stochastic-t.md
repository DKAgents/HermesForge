# Combine Weekly Price Channel Breakout with MACD/Stochastic Trend Filter

## Discovery Summary

R142 establishes that weekly chart signals should act as trend filters before acting on daily MACD and Stochastics crossovers. C130 independently identifies the weekly price channel (weekly rule) as a superior, simpler trend-following device compared to moving averages. The non-obvious connection is that the weekly price channel from C130 can serve as the specific weekly trend-confirmation mechanism called for in R142, providing a concrete, rules-based implementation of the multi-timeframe filter principle. Rather than subjectively evaluating weekly MACD/Stochastics, a trader can use an objective price channel breakout on the weekly chart to define trend direction.

## Trading Implication

A trader should only take daily MACD or Stochastics crossover signals in the direction of the prevailing weekly price channel breakout, skipping counter-trend daily signals until a new weekly channel breakout occurs in the opposite direction.
