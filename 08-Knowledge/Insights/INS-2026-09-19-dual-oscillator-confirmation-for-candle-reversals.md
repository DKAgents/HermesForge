# Dual Oscillator Confirmation for Candle Reversals

## Discovery Summary

C149 notes that RSI is less volatile than stochastics and best signals occur when both are simultaneously overbought or oversold. R177 states any oscillator can filter candle patterns, requiring presignal territory. Combining these, a trader can require both RSI (per N165 levels 70/30) and stochastics to be in overbought/oversold territory before accepting a reversal candle pattern, increasing reliability.

## Trading Implication

Only trade a candle reversal pattern (e.g., hammer, shooting star) when both RSI and stochastics are in overbought (above 70/80) or oversold (below 30/20) territory simultaneously.
