# Volume Divergence in Trends: Tighten Stops on Weak Breakouts

## Discovery Summary

N013 (Volume as a Filter for False Breakouts) and R082 (Breakouts Must Be Accompanied by Heavy Volume) establish that valid breakouts need heavy volume, while light-volume breakouts are suspect. N043 (Flag and Pennant) adds that continuation patterns require a volume surge to resume the trend. Thus, when price in a trend advances on declining volume, it resembles a light-volume breakout, which is likely false and warrants defensive stop placement.

## Trading Implication

During an ongoing trend, if a new price extreme forms on noticeably lighter volume, tighten the stop to just beyond the most recent swing or pattern boundary, or take partial profits to protect against a false continuation.
