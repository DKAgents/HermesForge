# Gap + Role-Reversal Levels Define Precise Sized Short Entries

## Discovery Summary

C065 establishes that violated support becomes resistance in downtrends (Murphy p.83), while EN069 specifies that in a bear move, gaps also act as resistance — sell a rally to the lower end of the gap with a stop above it. When a prior support level and a downward gap coincide or cluster near the same price zone, two independent resistance signals converge, creating a high-confidence short entry level. RG035 then closes the loop: once that technically-derived resistance defines the stop placement (above the gap/role-reversal level), position size is calculated to limit total risk to 5% of account capital.

## Trading Implication

In a downtrend, identify price zones where a violated support level and an unfilled gap overhead cluster together; sell rallies into that zone, place the stop just above it, then use RG035's 5%-of-account risk formula to size the position precisely around that stop distance.
