# Speedlines Reduce Subjectivity in Role Reversal

## Discovery Summary

The subjectivity in defining 'significant penetration' for support/resistance role reversal (E005) can be addressed by Speed Resistance Lines (R029). By treating a break of a speedline (e.g., the 2/3 line) as the threshold for significance, a trader transforms a vague judgment into a mechanical, trendline-based rule. This leverages the role reversal principle (C065) but ties it to an objective geometric benchmark directly from speedline theory.

## Trading Implication

Replace discretionary 'significant penetration' calls with a speedline break rule: once a speedline is broken, expect it to reverse roles on pullbacks; only act on role reversal if price confirms the break with a retest.
