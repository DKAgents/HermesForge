# Breadth Oscillator Filters Pullback Entries

## Discovery Summary

Secondary trends (C366) are counter-trend corrections lasting weeks to months. The oscillator entry rule (EN041) advises buying oversold in uptrends, but oscillators often lie in strong trends, giving premature signals. The McClellan Oscillator (N186) measures market breadth and can confirm whether an oversold reading during a secondary downtrend still occurs within a healthy primary uptrend, filtering out false oscillator reversal signals.

## Trading Implication

In a primary uptrend, wait for a secondary correction and only enter long when the McClellan Oscillator reaches oversold territory (e.g., deeply negative), rather than relying on price-based momentum oscillators that can decompose during strong trends.
