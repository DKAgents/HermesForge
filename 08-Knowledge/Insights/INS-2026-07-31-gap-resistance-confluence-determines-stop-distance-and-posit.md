# Gap + Resistance Confluence Determines Stop Distance and Position Size

## Discovery Summary

C065 establishes that violated support becomes resistance in downtrends, EN069 adds that price gaps also act as resistance on bear-market rallies, and RG035 specifies that stops must be placed at valid technical levels — above resistance for shorts — with position size calculated from stop distance. The non-obvious connection is that when a price gap and a prior-support-turned-resistance level coincide on a rally, they create a high-confidence resistance cluster for stop placement, which in turn permits a tighter, technically-validated stop and thus a larger position size under the 5% risk rule.

## Trading Implication

In a downtrend, sell rallies that reach the convergence of a price gap's lower end and a former support level (now resistance), placing the protective stop just above that cluster; the tighter stop justified by this dual-confirmation allows a proportionally larger position under the 10% commitment / 5% risk framework from RG035.
