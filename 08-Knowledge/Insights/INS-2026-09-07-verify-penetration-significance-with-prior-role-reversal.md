# Verify penetration significance with prior role reversal

## Discovery Summary

The subjectivity issue raised in E005 — determining what constitutes a 'significant' penetration for role reversal — can be mitigated by applying the concrete role-reversal logic from C065 and R029. If a prior support level in a downtrend is breached and subsequently acts as resistance on a bounce (per C065) or if a broken speedline confirms its new role as resistance (per R029), that post-facto behavior provides objective evidence that the initial penetration was indeed 'significant'. This transforms a subjective, real-time judgment into a confirmable condition.

## Trading Implication

Instead of trying to judge penetration significance in real-time, wait for the first subsequent bounce that confirms the level has reversed roles (e.g., old support rejects price as new resistance). Use that confirmation as the entry filter rather than the breakout moment itself.
