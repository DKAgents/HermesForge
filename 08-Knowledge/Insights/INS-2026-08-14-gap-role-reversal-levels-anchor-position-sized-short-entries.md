# Gap + Role-Reversal Levels Anchor Position-Sized Short Entries

## Discovery Summary

C065 establishes that violated support becomes resistance in downtrends (Murphy p.83). EN069 adds that gaps in a bear move also act as resistance, with entries triggered on rallies into the gap and stops placed just above it. RG035 closes the loop: the distance between the short entry (at the gap/role-reversal resistance level) and the stop above it determines the maximum position size via the 5% risk rule on a $100,000 account. Together, the three notes form a sequenced decision chain: identify role-reversal resistance or gap resistance → enter short on the rally → set stop just above that level → size the position so the stop distance equals no more than 5% of account.

## Trading Implication

When a prior support level is violated and price rallies back into that level or into an unfilled gap beneath it, initiate a short with a stop just above the resistance zone, then calculate position size so that stop distance represents at most 5% of total account equity, never exceeding 10% capital commitment.
