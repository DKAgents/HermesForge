# Volume Confirmation as Pre-Entry Filter Reduces False Breakout Risk

## Discovery Summary

EN008 establishes that pattern completion requires noticeable volume increase, while N013 specifies the directional asymmetry: heavy volume validates upside breakouts, light volume signals bull traps. C324's definition of confirmation as 'as many market factors as possible agreeing' provides the conceptual framework that ties these together — volume is not merely a secondary indicator but a required confirming factor before entry is justified. The non-obvious connection is that these three notes together produce a binary pre-entry gate: no volume expansion at breakout = no entry, regardless of how clean the pattern looks.

## Trading Implication

A trader should require explicitly above-average volume at the breakout candle close before entering any long reversal pattern trade; if volume is light on the breakout, wait for either a retest with volume confirmation or skip the trade entirely to avoid bull traps.
