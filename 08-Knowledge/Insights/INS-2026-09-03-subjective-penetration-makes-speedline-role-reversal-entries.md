# Subjective penetration makes speedline role-reversal entries unreliable

## Discovery Summary

E005 establishes that 'significant penetration' is inherently subjective with no universal definition, yet R029 and C065 both rely on clear penetration thresholds for role-reversal signals. Speed resistance lines (R029) depend on unambiguous breaks for entry, but the subjectivity noted in E005 means two traders could interpret the same 2/3 line break differently — one entering, one waiting. This gap creates false signals unless an objective filter is applied.

## Trading Implication

Before acting on any speedline or support/resistance role-reversal signal, require a confirming objective criterion (e.g., a % close beyond the level or a time filter) to remove the subjectivity identified in E005.
