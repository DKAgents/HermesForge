# Earnings Filter Resolves Role-Reversal Subjectivity Across All Trendline Types

## Discovery Summary

E005 acknowledges that 'significant penetration' for role-reversal is inherently subjective (Murphy, p. 83), which undermines reliable entry signals from both C065's support-becomes-resistance principle and R029's speedline role-reversal rule. The seed question introduces a binary earnings calendar filter as a mechanism to reduce false role-reversal signals — earnings events create artificial penetrations that may not reflect genuine supply/demand shifts, directly exploiting the subjectivity weakness identified in E005. Applying this filter to both horizontal S/R levels (C065) and diagonal speedlines (R029) creates a unified pre-trade gate across multiple trendline-based role-reversal setups.

## Trading Implication

Before acting on any role-reversal entry — whether from a violated horizontal support level (C065) or a broken speedline (R029) — a trader must check if earnings are within 7 days; if so, skip the trade entirely to avoid misclassifying an earnings-driven penetration as a technically significant one.
