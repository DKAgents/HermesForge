# Weekly Price Channel as MACD/Stochastics Filter

## Discovery Summary

R142 mandates evaluating MACD and Stochastics on weekly charts to filter daily signals. C130 promotes the weekly price channel as a simpler trend-following alternative, while N044 notes long-term weekly moving averages track the primary trend. This implies a trader can substitute the weekly price channel (or a 40‑week MA) for the weekly indicator crossover as the directional gate, reducing complexity while still respecting Murphy's multi‑timeframe principle.

## Trading Implication

Take daily MACD or Stochastics buy signals only when price closes above a rising 4‑week price channel high (or above a rising 40‑week MA); ignore sell signals unless below.
