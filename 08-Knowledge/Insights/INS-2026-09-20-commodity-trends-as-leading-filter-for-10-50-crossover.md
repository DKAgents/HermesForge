# Commodity Trends as Leading Filter for 10/50 Crossover

## Discovery Summary

The 10/50-day moving average crossover (N039 and EN028) is a standalone technical signal for stock trend shifts. E040 shows that commodity price plunges damage commodity-exporting economies. This creates a filter: when commodities are in a confirmed downtrend, long signals from the 10/50 crossover on stocks of commodity-exporting countries are less reliable. Conversely, shorts may be favored.

## Trading Implication

Before taking long crossover signals, check whether commodity prices are falling (e.g., below 10/50 MA). If so, avoid longs or consider shorting the equity indices of commodity exporters.
