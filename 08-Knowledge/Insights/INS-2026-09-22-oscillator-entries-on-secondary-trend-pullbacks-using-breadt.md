# Oscillator entries on secondary trend pullbacks using breadth

## Discovery Summary

Secondary trends (C366) are counter-trend moves within a primary trend. The oscillator entry strategy (EN041) recommends buying oversold conditions in an uptrend, which typically occur during secondary corrections. The McClellan oscillator (N186) provides a market breadth measure of overbought/oversold conditions. Combining these, a trader can use the McClellan oscillator to confirm that a secondary correction has reached an oversold extreme before entering with the primary trend, but must be cautious because strong trends can cause oscillators to lie, producing false signals.

## Trading Implication

Use the McClellan oscillator to identify oversold readings during secondary corrections in an uptrend, but wait for the oscillator to turn up from oversold before entering to avoid catching a falling knife in a strong trend.
