# Subjective penetration undermines role-reversal reliability

## Discovery Summary

E005 explicitly acknowledges that defining a 'significant' penetration for support/resistance role reversal is subjective, lacking universal precision. This subjectivity directly undermines the actionable application of role-reversal concepts like C065 (previous support becomes resistance) and R029 (speedline role reversal), because a trader cannot objectively confirm if a breakout is valid enough to expect the flip to hold.

## Trading Implication

A trader should require additional objective confirmation beyond a simple penetration—such as a close beyond the level or a minimum percentage move—before treating a broken support or resistance as having reversed roles.
