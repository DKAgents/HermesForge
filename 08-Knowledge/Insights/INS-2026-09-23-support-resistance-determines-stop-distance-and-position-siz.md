# Support/Resistance Determines Stop Distance and Position Size

## Discovery Summary

C065 and EN069 prescribe specific technical levels (violated support in downtrends as resistance, price gaps as support) for entry and stop placement. RG035 then mandates that protective stops must be placed at these valid technical levels, and the distance from entry to stop determines maximum position size based on a fixed risk per trade (e.g., 5% of account). This sequence creates a direct link between technical analysis and money management.

## Trading Implication

When trading short in a downtrend, set stop just above the former support level (now resistance) identified in C065; when buying a dip to a gap, place stop below the gap per EN069. Use the resulting stop distance to compute position size so that the total risk equals the pre-set maximum (e.g., 5% of account).
