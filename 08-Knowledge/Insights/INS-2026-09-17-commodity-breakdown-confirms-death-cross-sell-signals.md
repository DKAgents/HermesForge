# Commodity Breakdown Confirms Death Cross Sell Signals

## Discovery Summary

The 10/50-day moving average crossover (N039, EN028) provides a technical sell signal for stock indexes, but its reliability is enhanced when aligned with the intermarket stress signal from E040: a plunge in commodity prices damages commodity-exporting economies (Australia, Canada, Mexico, Russia) and their equity markets. Since commodity prices often lead these markets, a commodity breakdown can confirm or even precede a bearish 10/50 crossover in exporter equity indexes, turning a standalone technical signal into a higher-probability trade.

## Trading Implication

When trading the 10/50 crossover on commodity-exporter equity ETFs (e.g., EWC for Canada, EWA for Australia), only take short or sell signals if commodity prices (e.g., CRB index or oil/copper) are also falling; avoid or fade long crossover signals when commodities are in a downtrend.
