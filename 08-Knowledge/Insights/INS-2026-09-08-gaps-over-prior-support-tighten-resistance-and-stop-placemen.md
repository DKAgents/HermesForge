# Gaps Over Prior Support Tighten Resistance and Stop Placement

## Discovery Summary

In a downtrend, a broken support becomes resistance (C065). When a price gap aligns with that former support, EN069's rule confirms it as a reinforced resistance level. Placing a protective stop just above this combined gap/resistance zone satisfies RG035's technical validity requirement; the shorter stop distance then permits a larger position size while staying within the 5% total risk limit.

## Trading Implication

When shorting a pullback to a prior support now acting as resistance, look for a nearby price gap to reinforce the level, set the stop just above the gap, and size the position larger based on the tighter stop.
