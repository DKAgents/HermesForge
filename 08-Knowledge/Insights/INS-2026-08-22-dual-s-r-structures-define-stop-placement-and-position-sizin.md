# Dual S/R Structures Define Stop Placement and Position Sizing

## Discovery Summary

C065 establishes that violated support becomes resistance in downtrends, while EN069 establishes that gaps act as support/resistance for timing entries with explicit stop placement rules (stop below gap for longs, above gap for shorts). Together, they create a layered S/R framework: in a downtrend, a trader selling a rally can use both the flipped support-as-resistance level (C065, Murphy p.83) AND the gap's lower/upper end (EN069) as confluent resistance zones, with the gap's boundary providing the precise stop distance for position sizing calculations.

## Trading Implication

In a downtrend, when a bounce approaches a prior violated support level that also coincides with a gap boundary, sell at that confluence zone with a stop just above the gap — this dual confirmation tightens stop distance and improves position size without sacrificing edge.
