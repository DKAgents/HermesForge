# Using Speedlines to Objectify Support/Resistance Penetration

## Discovery Summary

The subjectivity in defining 'significant penetration' for role reversal (E005) can be mitigated by applying the role-reversal rule from speed resistance lines (R029). A prior support level that becomes resistance (C065) can be validated as truly reversed when a break of a speedline (e.g., the 2/3 line) confirms the significance, replacing arbitrary judgment with an objective price-derived criterion.

## Trading Implication

Before entering a short on a bounce to former support-turned-resistance, confirm that the corresponding speedline (drawn from the prior downtrend) has also been broken, adding an objective filter to the role-reversal entry.
