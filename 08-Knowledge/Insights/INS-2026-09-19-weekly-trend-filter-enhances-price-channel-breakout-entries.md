# Weekly Trend Filter Enhances Price Channel Breakout Entries

## Discovery Summary

The weekly rule (price channel) from C130 identifies trend-following breakouts on the weekly chart, while R142 advises using weekly signals to filter daily MACD/Stochastics entries. Combined, a trader can use the weekly price channel as a higher-timeframe trend filter: only take daily MACD/Stochastics signals aligned with the weekly channel direction. Long-term moving averages from N044 (e.g., 10/40-week) reinforce the weekly trend context, supporting Murphy's multi-timeframe principle.

## Trading Implication

Before entering daily MACD or Stochastics signals, confirm the weekly price channel direction (e.g., price above weekly channel low for longs) and optionally the 40-week MA slope. Take only long signals when weekly channel is up, short signals when down.
