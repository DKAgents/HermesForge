# Triple Crossover Oscillator Gap Reveals Regime Filter Opportunity

## Discovery Summary

C128 establishes that MA differences can be viewed as oscillators revealing momentum, while R323 and N037 define triple crossover signals using 4-9-18 day MAs. The non-obvious connection is that the spread between all three MAs in the 4-9-18 system can be measured as a dual oscillator (4-9 diff and 9-18 diff) to detect whether the market is trending or ranging — when both oscillators are compressed near zero simultaneously, the triple crossover system is likely in a whipsaw-prone regime and signals should be filtered or ignored.

## Trading Implication

A trader using the 4-9-18 triple crossover system should calculate the absolute spread between the 4-9 and 9-18 MA pairs as oscillators; when both spreads are compressed below a threshold, suppress crossover signals until at least one spread expands, indicating a trending regime has resumed.
