# Three Sources Converge on 38–62% Retracement Entry Zone

## Discovery Summary

All three notes independently arrive at the same retracement entry zone through different analytical frameworks: Dow Theory (C050) defines secondary corrections as retracing one-third to two-thirds (~33–67%) of the prior move; Murphy's trading rule (EN068) narrows this to 40–60% as the actionable entry zone; and Fibonacci analysis (N085) identifies 38%, 50%, and 62% as the key retracement levels within that same range. The convergence is non-obvious because each source derives the zone from a distinct methodology — classical Dow theory, empirical trading rules, and mathematical Fibonacci ratios — yet they all cluster around the same 38–62% band.

## Trading Implication

A trader should only initiate pullback entries in uptrends when price retraces into the 38–62% zone AND a specific Fibonacci level (38%, 50%, or 62%) aligns within Murphy's 40–60% preferred range — treating the 50% level as the highest-probability single entry point since it is cited explicitly by all three frameworks.
