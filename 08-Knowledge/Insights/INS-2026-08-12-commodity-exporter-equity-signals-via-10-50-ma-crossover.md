# Commodity Exporter Equity Signals via 10/50 MA Crossover

## Discovery Summary

E040 identifies commodity-exporting nations (Australia, Canada, Mexico, Russia) as especially sensitive to commodity price plunges, making their equity markets leading stress indicators within Murphy's intermarket chain. EN028 and N039 provide a concrete mechanical entry/exit rule — the 10/50-day MA crossover — that can be applied specifically to these commodity-exporter equity indices. The intermarket sequence (commodities → bonds → stocks) means a commodity price plunge in E040 should precede equity deterioration in these nations, giving traders advance warning to watch for the 10-day crossing below the 50-day in those specific markets as confirmation of the move.

## Trading Implication

When commodity prices plunge significantly, immediately place the equity indices of Australia, Canada, Mexico, and Russia on a watchlist and initiate short positions when their respective 10-day MAs cross below their 50-day MAs; do not wait for broader market signals since these markets lead the stress cycle.
