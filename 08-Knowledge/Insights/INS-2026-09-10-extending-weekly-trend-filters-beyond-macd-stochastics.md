# Extending Weekly Trend Filters Beyond MACD/Stochastics

## Discovery Summary

R142 states Murphy's principle that weekly signals should serve as trend filters for daily MACD and Stochastics crossovers. C130 identifies the weekly price channel as a simple, proven trend-following alternative, and N044 confirms that long-term moving averages like the 40-week MA track the primary trend on weekly charts. These notes together suggest that the filtering principle can be extended beyond weekly MACD/Stochastics to using the weekly price channel direction or a 40-week MA slope as the trend filter for daily entries.

## Trading Implication

Before acting on a daily MACD or Stochastics crossover, confirm that price is above a rising 40-week MA or has broken out to a new weekly channel high, and only take signals in the direction of that weekly trend.
