# Dual Technical Anchors Enable Precise Position Sizing via Stop Distance

## Discovery Summary

C065 and EN069 together provide two distinct technical levels — violated support-turned-resistance (Murphy p.83) and price gaps — that can serve as precise stop placement anchors in downtrends. RG035 then operationalizes these anchors: once the stop distance is defined by either the gap boundary or the prior support-resistance flip, position size is mechanically determined by the 5% maximum risk rule on the total account. The non-obvious connection is that these two independent technical methods (gap resistance and support-role-reversal) converge to define the same stop zone above price in a downtrend, reducing ambiguity in stop placement and therefore producing a more reliable input for the position-sizing formula in RG035.

## Trading Implication

In a downtrend, identify both the nearest violated support level (now resistance) and the lower boundary of any overhead gap; place the protective stop above whichever is closer, then use RG035's 5% risk rule to size the position based on that exact stop distance — smaller stop distance permits larger size, larger distance mandates smaller size.
