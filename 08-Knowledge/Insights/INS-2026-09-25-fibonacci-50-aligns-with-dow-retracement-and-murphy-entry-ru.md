# Fibonacci 50% aligns with Dow retracement and Murphy entry rule

## Discovery Summary

The Dow concept (C050) notes secondary trends retrace 1/3 to 2/3 with 50% most common. Murphy's rule (EN068-4060) specifies 40-60% retracements as excellent entry zones. The Fibonacci indicator (N085) provides precise levels 38%, 50%, 62%. Together, they define an actionable entry zone: in strong trends target 38-50%, in weak trends 50-62%, with the 50% level as the optimal entry point.

## Trading Implication

In an uptrend, wait for a pullback to the 50% Fibonacci retracement of the prior move, and enter long if the retracement falls within the 40-60% range; adjust to 38% in strong trends or 62% in weaker trends.
