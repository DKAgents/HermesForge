# Weekly Price Channel as Trend Filter for MACD/Stochastics

## Discovery Summary

Murphy's principle (R142) mandates using weekly MACD and Stochastics signals to filter daily entries. The weekly price channel (C130) is a simpler, often superior trend-following tool, while long-term moving averages (N044) also define primary trends. Combining these, a trader can replace the weekly MACD/Stochastics filter with a weekly price channel breakout (e.g., 4-week high/low), providing a robust and less time-consuming trend filter for daily crossover signals.

## Trading Implication

For daily MACD or Stochastics crossovers, only take long signals if price is above the weekly price channel's recent high, and short signals if below the recent low, using the weekly rule as the trend filter instead of weekly indicator readings.
