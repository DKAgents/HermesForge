# Earnings Filter for Role-Reversal Entries

## Discovery Summary

The subjectivity in defining significant penetration (E005) weakens the role-reversal principle in both support/resistance (C065) and speedlines (R029). Adding a mandatory pre-trade earnings calendar check—skipping entries within 7 days of earnings—provides an objective binary filter that mitigates this subjectivity and reduces false breakouts from earnings-driven volatility.

## Trading Implication

Before accepting any role-reversal trade based on prior support/resistance or speedline breaks, verify the stock's earnings date; if within 7 days, cancel the entry and wait until after the event.
