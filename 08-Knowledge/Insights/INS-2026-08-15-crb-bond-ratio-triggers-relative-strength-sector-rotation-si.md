# CRB/Bond Ratio Triggers Relative Strength Sector Rotation Signal

## Discovery Summary

R249 provides a specific intermarket trigger (CRB/Bond ratio direction) that tells investors WHEN to rotate sectors, while N112 and C340 provide the analytical tool (relative strength analysis) to identify WHICH specific stocks or sub-sectors within those broad categories are strongest. The CRB/Bond ratio acts as the macro filter, and relative strength analysis refines the entry to the best-performing securities within the favored sector group. Together they form a two-stage decision process: macro regime identification followed by relative strength ranking for stock selection.

## Trading Implication

When the CRB/Bond ratio is rising, use relative strength analysis to rank individual stocks within gold, oil, and cyclical sectors — allocate to the top relative strength performers within those groups rather than broad sector exposure. When the ratio falls, apply relative strength ranking within utilities, financials, and consumer staples to identify the strongest defensive names.
