# Triple Convergence: Dow, Fibonacci, and Murphy Define Optimal Entry Zone

## Discovery Summary

Three independent frameworks converge on the same retracement zone for entries. C050 (Dow Theory) identifies 33-66% as the secondary correction range with 50% most common. N085 (Fibonacci) places key retracement levels at 38%, 50%, and 62%, with trend strength determining which extreme applies. EN068 (Murphy's rule) operationalizes this into a specific entry rule: the 40-60% zone is actionable for longs in uptrends and shorts in downtrends. The non-obvious insight is that the 40-60% Murphy entry zone is not arbitrary — it is simultaneously validated by Dow's empirical observation of secondary trends AND the mathematical Fibonacci sequence, making entries in this zone triply confirmed.

## Trading Implication

In an uptrend, only initiate or add long positions when price pulls back to the 40-62% retracement zone of the prior advance, treating 38% as a strong-trend minimum target and 62% as the weak-trend maximum; entries outside this zone lack multi-framework confluence and carry higher failure risk.
