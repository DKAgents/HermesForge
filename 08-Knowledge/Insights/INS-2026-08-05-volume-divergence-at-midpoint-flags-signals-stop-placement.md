# Volume Divergence at Midpoint Flags Signals Stop Placement

## Discovery Summary

N043 establishes that flags and pennants form at approximately the midpoint of a move on light volume, while R082 requires heavy volume at breakout for signal validity. C050 notes that secondary corrections retrace one-third to two-thirds of prior moves. When volume diverges during a flag/pennant (fails to surge at breakout), the pattern may instead be a secondary correction retracing 33-50% of the flagpole, suggesting stops should be tightened to just below the 50% retracement of the flagpole rather than below the full pattern low.

## Trading Implication

If a breakout from a flag or pennant lacks the required volume surge per R082, treat the structure as a potential secondary correction per C050 and tighten stops to the 50% retracement level of the flagpole rather than the conventional pattern boundary stop.
