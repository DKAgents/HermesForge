# Violated Gap Support Becomes Resistance for Stop & Sizing

## Discovery Summary

C065 states that in a downtrend, violated support flips to resistance. EN069 treats gaps as support (after upmove) or resistance (after downmove), with stops placed beyond the gap. Combining these, if a gap that supported an uptrend is broken, it becomes a resistance level per C065, offering a technical stop placement above that level for short entries. RG035 then dictates position sizing based on the distance to that stop, keeping total risk within a 5% of the $100k account limit.

## Trading Implication

When a previous gap support is violated, sell into a rally toward its lower edge with a stop placed above that former support-turned-resistance, and size the position so that the stop-distance risk aligns with the 5% rule.
