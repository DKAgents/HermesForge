# Triple Convergence: 38-62% Retracement Zone for Trend Entries

## Discovery Summary

Three independent frameworks converge on the same retracement zone for entries: Dow Theory (C050) identifies 1/3 to 2/3 retracements as secondary corrections within primary trends; Murphy's entry rule (EN068) specifies 40-60% pullbacks as ideal entry zones; Fibonacci levels (N085) place key ratios at 38%, 50%, and 62%. The overlap between these three systems — roughly 38-62% — creates a high-confidence entry window validated by Dow Theory structure, Murphy's empirical rule, and Fibonacci mathematics simultaneously.

## Trading Implication

In a confirmed primary uptrend, wait for pullbacks to the 38-62% retracement zone of the prior advance before entering long; the closer the retracement clusters near 50%, the stronger the convergence across all three frameworks, providing a concrete entry trigger with a defined invalidation level beyond 62-66%.
