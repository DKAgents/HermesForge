# Counter-Trend Oscillator Signals Inside Secondary Trends

## Discovery Summary

The EN041 rule to buy oversold oscillators in uptrends can be refined using C366's secondary trends: in a primary uptrend, a selloff to oversold is often the start of a secondary correction, not a reversal. The McClellan Oscillator (N186) provides a breadth-based oversold reading that can time entries during these secondary pullbacks, aligning with Murphy's counter-trend oscillator strategy.

## Trading Implication

In a primary uptrend, wait for the McClellan Oscillator to reach an oversold extreme during a secondary decline, then buy as the oscillator crosses back above its zero line—confirming the secondary correction is ending. In a primary downtrend, mirror this for short entries during secondary rallies.
