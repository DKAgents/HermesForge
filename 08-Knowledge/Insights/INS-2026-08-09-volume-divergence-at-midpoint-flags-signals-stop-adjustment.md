# Volume Divergence at Midpoint Flags Signals Stop Adjustment

## Discovery Summary

N043 establishes that flags and pennants form at approximately the midpoint of a market move on very light volume, while R082 mandates that breakouts require heavy volume to be valid. C050 adds that secondary corrections retrace 1/3 to 2/3 of prior moves. When volume diverges from price during a continuation pattern (fails to surge on breakout from flag/pennant), the breakout fails R082's validity test — and since flags occur at the midpoint, the remaining expected move is approximately equal to the flagpole. A failed-volume breakout therefore signals the pattern may actually be a secondary correction retracing into the 33-67% zone, warranting tighter stop placement near the 50% retracement level rather than below the pattern low.

## Trading Implication

If a flag or pennant breakout occurs without a volume surge (violating R082), treat the move as a potential secondary correction and tighten stops to the 50% retracement level of the prior flagpole rather than using the standard below-pattern-low stop placement.
