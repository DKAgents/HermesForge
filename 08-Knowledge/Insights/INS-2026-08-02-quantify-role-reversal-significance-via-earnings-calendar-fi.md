# Quantify Role-Reversal Significance via Earnings Calendar Filter

## Discovery Summary

E005 identifies that 'significant penetration' for role-reversal is inherently subjective (Murphy, p. 83), while C065 and R029 confirm the principle applies across both price levels and speedlines. The seed question introduces an earnings calendar check as a binary filter. Because earnings events cause abnormal volatility that can produce false penetrations — mimicking 'significant' breaks without structural market conviction — adding a 7-day earnings exclusion rule directly addresses E005's acknowledged subjectivity by removing the highest-noise environment where spurious role-reversals are most likely to be misidentified.

## Trading Implication

Before entering any trade based on a support-to-resistance or resistance-to-support role-reversal signal (whether from price levels per C065 or speedlines per R029), check the earnings calendar: if the underlying has earnings within 7 days, skip the trade entirely to avoid counting earnings-driven volatility spikes as structurally significant penetrations.
