# Crossover Systems Fail in Ranging Markets: Add Regime Filter

## Discovery Summary

The double and triple crossover methods (C128, R323, N037) generate trend-following signals that are prone to whipsaws in ranging or volatile markets. The seed question highlights that these patterns fail more often under such regimes, implying a need for a regime filter (e.g., ADX or volatility-based) to confirm trend strength before acting on crossover signals.

## Trading Implication

Before taking a crossover signal, check for a trending regime (e.g., ADX above threshold or low volatility compression); in ranging or high-volatility chop, stand aside or reduce position size.
