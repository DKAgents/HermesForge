# Commodity 10/50 Crossover Predicts Exporter Equity Weakness

## Discovery Summary

The 10/50 moving average crossover method (from N039 and EN028) is typically applied to stocks. However, note E040 explains that a plunge in commodity prices damages commodity-exporting countries like Australia, Canada, Mexico, Russia. By applying the same 10/50 crossover to a broad commodity index (e.g., CRB), a bearish crossover becomes a leading filter for anticipating equity downtrends in those export-heavy nations, enabling timing of long/short decisions on country ETFs.

## Trading Implication

When the commodity index's 10-day MA crosses below its 50-day MA, reduce long exposure or add hedges to commodity-exporting country ETFs (e.g., EWA, EWC). Conversely, a bullish crossover can confirm entry into those markets.
