# Volume divergence triggers stop adjustment

## Discovery Summary

N013-Volume-as-a-Filter-for-False-Breakouts notes that a decline on heavy volume after a light-volume breakout signals a false breakout. R082-Breakouts-Must-Be-Accompanied-by-Heavy-Volume emphasizes that valid breakouts require heavy volume. N043-Flag-and-Pennant-Summary-Characteristics states that during the consolidation phase volume drops off, and the trend resumes on a volume burst. When volume diverges from price (e.g., a continuation move without rising volume), it warns the trend may be weakening, and the trader should tighten stops or take partial profits rather than rely on the prior pattern.

## Trading Implication

When a flag/pennant breakout occurs on light volume, or a trending move continues with declining volume, immediately tighten the stop-loss to the breakout level or recent swing point to protect against a false move.
