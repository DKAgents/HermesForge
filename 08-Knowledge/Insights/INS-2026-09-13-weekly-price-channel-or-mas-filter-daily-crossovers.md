# Weekly Price Channel or MAs Filter Daily Crossovers

## Discovery Summary

R142 establishes that weekly signals should act as trend filters for daily MACD and Stochastics crossovers, preventing counter-trend trades. C130 offers the weekly price channel as a proven alternative to moving averages for defining that weekly trend, while N044 gives a specific 10/40-week moving-average structure for primary trend. Together these notes define an objective multi-timeframe filter: use a weekly price-channel breakout or 10/40-week MA alignment to gate daily MACD/Stochastics signals.

## Trading Implication

Before acting on any daily MACD or Stochastics crossover, first require the weekly chart to confirm trend via a weekly price-channel breakout or price above/rising 40-week MA; take only daily signals in that weekly trend direction.
