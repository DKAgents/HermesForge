# Volume confirms flag/pennant breakouts

## Discovery Summary

Notes N043, R082, and N013 collectively reinforce that valid breakouts from consolidation patterns like flags and pennants require heavy volume. N043 describes light volume during the pause and a burst on breakout; R082 and N013 state that breakouts must be accompanied by heavy volume to be real. This confirms a standard risk rule for pattern trading.

## Trading Implication

Only enter trades on flag/pennant breakouts that occur with a clear volume surge; if volume is low, treat the breakout as suspect and tighten stops or avoid the trade.
