# Triple crossover with MACD oscillator filter

## Discovery Summary

The Triple Crossover Method (R323) with specific 4-9-18 periods (N037) generates signals based on moving average crosses, but these can be unreliable in ranging markets. The Moving Averages as Oscillators via Double Crossover concept (C128), which underlies MACD, provides an oscillator that can confirm trend strength. By requiring the MACD oscillator to be in the direction of the crossover (e.g., above zero for buy signals), traders can filter out false breakout signals in range-bound conditions.

## Trading Implication

Apply a MACD oscillator filter (e.g., histogram sign) to the 4-9-18 triple crossover signals; only take buy signals when the MACD is positive and sell signals when negative.
