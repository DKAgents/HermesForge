# Support/Resistance stops determine position sizing

## Discovery Summary

C065 shows how violated support becomes resistance in a downtrend, while EN069 explains using price gaps as support/resistance to place stops just beyond them. RG035 then mandates that stops must be at such valid technical levels and that stop distance directly determines position size through the % risk rule. Thus, the specific level identified by these concepts (prior support or gap) fixes the stop distance, which then calculates the exact position size allowed by the risk budget.

## Trading Implication

Set protective stops just beyond the prior support or gap edges, then size the position by dividing the account’s maximum risk dollar amount by that stop distance.
