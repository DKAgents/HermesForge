# Ideal Entry at 50% Retracement Confirmed by Fib and Dow

## Discovery Summary

C050 notes that secondary trend corrections commonly retrace 50% of the prior move. EN068-4060 specifies the 40-60% retracement zone as optimal for timing entries. N085 identifies 50% as a key Fibonacci retracement and adds that strong trends see minimum 38% retracements while weak trends see maximum 62%. Together, these notes pinpoint the 50% level as the ideal default entry within the 40-60% zone, with trend strength providing actionable adjustment.

## Trading Implication

Place limit buy orders at the 50% retracement of the prior advance for pullback entries; in exceptionally strong trends, scale in from 38% and in weaker trends, wait for 62%.
