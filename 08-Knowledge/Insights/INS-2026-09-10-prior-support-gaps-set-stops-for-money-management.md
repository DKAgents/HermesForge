# Prior Support/Gaps Set Stops for Money Management

## Discovery Summary

C065 establishes that violated support becomes resistance in a downtrend, giving a precise stop level above the new resistance for short entries. EN069 adds that price gaps act as support or resistance for timing, providing additional specific stop placement levels. RG035’s money management rule mandates that protective stops be placed at valid technical levels and uses the stop distance to determine position size (max 5% risk on a $100,000 account). Together, these Murphy concepts supply the exact technical inputs needed to satisfy RG035’s requirements and execute the position sizing formula.

## Trading Implication

Identify the specific price level from prior support (now resistance) or a gap, set the stop there, then divide the $5,000 maximum risk by the entry-to-stop distance to calculate the exact number of shares to trade.
