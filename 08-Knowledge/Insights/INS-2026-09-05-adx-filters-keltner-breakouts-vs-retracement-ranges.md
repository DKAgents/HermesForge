# ADX Filters Keltner Breakouts vs. Retracement Ranges

## Discovery Summary

E036 notes that a rising ADX favors moving-average-based indicators (such as Keltner Channels from N190), while a falling ADX points to ranging conditions where oscillators are preferred. C050 establishes that secondary trends retrace one-third to two-thirds of prior moves, with 50% being most common — these levels become relevant during ranges. ADX regime can therefore direct a trader to either trust Keltner Channel breakouts in trends or expect price to respond to retracement zones in ranging markets.

## Trading Implication

When ADX is rising, enter on Keltner Channel breakouts; when ADX is falling, fade moves into the 50% retracement of the prior swing.
