# Gap + Role-Reversal Levels Define Precise Stop Placement

## Discovery Summary

C065 establishes that violated support becomes resistance in downtrends, while EN069 specifies that gaps also act as resistance targets for selling rallies with stops placed above the gap. When a price gap coincides with a prior support level that has flipped to resistance, both rules converge on the same price zone, creating a higher-confidence resistance ceiling. The seed question makes explicit that stop distance from entry determines position size — so a tighter, more precisely defined resistance zone (gap lower end AND role-reversed support at same level) allows a tighter stop, directly enabling a larger position size for the same risk.

## Trading Implication

In a downtrend, identify rallies where a price gap's lower end aligns with a prior support-turned-resistance level; enter short at that confluence zone with a stop just above it, then size the position using the tightened stop distance to maximize risk-adjusted exposure.
