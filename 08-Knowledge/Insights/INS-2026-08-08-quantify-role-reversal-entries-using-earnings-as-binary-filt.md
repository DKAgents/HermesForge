# Quantify Role-Reversal Entries Using Earnings as Binary Filter

## Discovery Summary

E005 identifies that 'significant penetration' in role-reversal setups (Murphy, p. 83) is inherently subjective, creating an entry ambiguity. C065 and R029 both rely on this same subjective role-reversal principle — C065 for support-to-resistance flips in downtrends, R029 for speedline breaks. The seed question introduces a binary earnings calendar filter as a way to partially resolve this subjectivity: if an earnings event is within 7 days, the volatility-driven price move may falsely appear to be a 'significant' penetration, making any role-reversal signal unreliable. By adding the mandatory earnings check, traders gain one objective, measurable condition that narrows the ambiguity acknowledged in E005.

## Trading Implication

Before acting on any role-reversal entry — whether from a violated support level (C065) or a broken speedline (R029) — check the earnings calendar: if earnings are within 7 days, skip the trade entirely, as the penetration may not reflect genuine structural change and the subjectivity risk (E005) is compounded by event-driven noise.
