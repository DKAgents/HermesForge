# Volume Divergence Signals Stop Tightening at Midpoint Retracement

## Discovery Summary

Flag and pennant patterns (N043) occur at approximately the midpoint of a move, which aligns with the 50% retracement level identified in secondary trend theory (C050). R082 requires volume confirmation at breakout from these patterns. When volume diverges from price during a flag/pennant consolidation — meaning volume fails to surge on the expected breakout — the 50% midpoint rule from C050 provides a natural stop reference: if price retraces beyond 50% of the flagpole, the continuation thesis is invalidated, requiring tighter stops.

## Trading Implication

When a flag or pennant breakout occurs on insufficient volume (violating R082), tighten stops to just below the 50% retracement of the flagpole rather than using standard pattern invalidation levels, since low-volume breakouts have higher failure probability at the natural midpoint.
