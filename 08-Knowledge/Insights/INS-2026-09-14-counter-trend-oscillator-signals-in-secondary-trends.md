# Counter-Trend Oscillator Signals in Secondary Trends

## Discovery Summary

EN041's rule to buy oversold in an uptrend and sell overbought in a downtrend becomes more precise when applied to C366's secondary trends. In a primary uptrend, the market is often overbought at the end of a secondary rally; waiting for an oversold reading avoids entering at the top of a counter-trend move and aligns with the secondary correction's conclusion. The McClellan Oscillator (N186) can serve as the oscillator for this rule, as it provides broad-market overbought/oversold readings.

## Trading Implication

In a confirmed primary uptrend, use the McClellan Oscillator to buy only when it signals oversold during a secondary pullback, not on the initial overbought spike. Conversely, in a primary downtrend, short only when the oscillator is overbought during a secondary rally, avoiding counter-trend exhaustion points.
