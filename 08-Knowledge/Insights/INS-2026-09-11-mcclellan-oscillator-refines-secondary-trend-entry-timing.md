# McClellan Oscillator refines secondary trend entry timing

## Discovery Summary

EN041 advises buying oversold conditions in uptrends and selling overbought conditions in downtrends, while C366 defines secondary trends as counter-trend moves lasting weeks to months. The McClellan Oscillator (N186) provides objective breadth-based overbought/oversold thresholds that can distinguish between a legitimate secondary trend reversal entry and a failing primary trend. Using breadth extremes rather than price-based oscillators adds a market-internal confirmation layer that helps avoid entering secondary trends that are actually primary trend reversals.

## Trading Implication

Before entering a counter-trend trade per EN041 during what C366 defines as a secondary trend, confirm the overbought/oversold condition using the McClellan Oscillator—only trade when breadth extremes align with the oscillator signal, ignoring price-only oscillator readings that lack breadth confirmation.
