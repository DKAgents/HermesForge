# 50% Retracement as Ideal Entry Zone

## Discovery Summary

C050 notes that Dow theory's secondary trend retracements commonly hit 50% of the prior move. EN068 (Murphy) explicitly recommends 40–60% retracement zones for entries. N085 confirms that 50% is a key Fibonacci level. Together, they triangulate the 50% retracement as a high-probability entry point for pullbacks in trends.

## Trading Implication

In an uptrend, wait for a pullback to retrace approximately 50% (within the 40–60% band) of the prior advance before entering long; in a downtrend, short bounces to that same zone.
