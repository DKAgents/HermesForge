# Layered S/R Levels Create Precise Short Entry and Stop Placement

## Discovery Summary

C065 establishes that violated support becomes resistance in downtrends, and EN069 adds that price gaps also act as resistance levels on bear rallies — both provide discrete, technically valid resistance zones for short entry. RG035 (Murphy) requires that protective stops be placed above technically valid resistance, and that position size scales inversely with stop distance. When a rally back toward a violated support level (C065) coincides with or terminates at the lower end of an overhead gap (EN069), the two confluent resistance signals define a tighter, higher-confidence stop zone, which per RG035 directly permits a larger position size.

## Trading Implication

In a downtrend, when a rally approaches both a prior violated support level AND the lower edge of a price gap simultaneously, enter short at that confluence zone with a stop just above the gap — the tighter technical stop permits larger position sizing per the 5% max-risk rule from RG035.
