# Weekly trend filter for MACD and Stochastics with channel confirmation

## Discovery Summary

R142 prescribes using weekly MACD and Stochastics crossovers as a trend filter before acting on daily signals. C130 introduces the weekly price channel as a simple trend-following alternative. Combining these concepts yields a robust multi-timeframe filter: confirm the weekly trend via either the weekly MACD/Stochastic crossover or a fresh weekly price channel breakout before taking daily entries.

## Trading Implication

Before taking a daily MACD or Stochastics signal, first confirm the weekly trend using either a weekly-level MACD/Stochastic crossover (per Murphy) or a weekly price channel breakout (the weekly rule), and trade only in the direction of the weekly filter.
