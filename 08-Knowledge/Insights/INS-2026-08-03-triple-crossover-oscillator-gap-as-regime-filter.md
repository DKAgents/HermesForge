# Triple Crossover Oscillator Gap as Regime Filter

## Discovery Summary

C128 establishes that the difference between two moving averages can be viewed as an oscillator measuring momentum. R323 and N037 together show the 4-9-18 triple crossover as a signal system. The non-obvious connection is that the spread between all three MAs in the 4-9-18 system (N037) can be simultaneously reframed as two oscillators (C128 logic): the 4-9 spread and the 9-18 spread. When both oscillator spreads are narrow and oscillating without trend, this signals a ranging regime where triple crossover signals (R323) are likely to produce false breakouts and whipsaws.

## Trading Implication

Before acting on a 4-9-18 triple crossover signal, measure the magnitude of the 4-minus-9 and 9-minus-18 spreads as oscillators; if both spreads are compressed and mean-reverting rather than expanding directionally, suppress the trade signal as likely regime noise.
