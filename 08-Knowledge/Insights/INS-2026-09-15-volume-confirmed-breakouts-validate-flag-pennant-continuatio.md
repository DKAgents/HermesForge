# Volume-Confirmed Breakouts Validate Flag/Pennant Continuations

## Discovery Summary

Flag and pennant patterns (N043) require a breakout on heavy volume to complete, but this is reinforced by R082 and N013, which state that valid breakouts must be accompanied by volume surges and false breakouts often occur on light volume. Thus, when trading flags/pennants, the breakout volume is not just a confirmation—it's a necessary condition to distinguish genuine continuation from a bull trap.

## Trading Implication

When a flag or pennant breakout occurs, only enter if volume is significantly above average; if volume is light, wait for a subsequent heavy-volume move or avoid the trade, and consider protective stops just beyond the pattern's extreme to limit risk if the false breakout reverses.
